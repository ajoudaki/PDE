Those are examples of consequential advances, not a compulsory checklist.

## Reading the grades correctly

**A slash separates audiences; a dash denotes a range.** Frozen H23 is **H / S–H**: Highly significant for its specialists, Significant-to-Highly-significant for broader deep-learning theory. It is not a single “H-to-S” judgment. Likewise B/H meant specialist Breakthrough and broader Highly significant, not a combined B-to-H interval. The earlier shorthand could obscure this distinction.

The frozen H23 table gives a shared specialist **H center**. Its accompanying discussion already records the advocate's possible, disputed **B upper boundary** for computational unification. That boundary must accompany the table when comparing it with the current H–B assessment; comparing only the historical center with the current upper interpretation would be asymmetric.

The categories are comparable because the rubric and publication-time unit are unchanged. They are not numerical scores or a total ordering. A direct substantive comparison can favor one contribution even when its coarse band overlaps another's, or remain undecided despite different central labels.

There is also an audience constraint. Historical “specialist” sometimes means approximation theory, GP inference or kernel computation. Those native grades cannot silently become grades within feature-learning theory. The direct specialist comparisons below emphasize wide-network dynamics, feature-learning theory and their computation. Other cases provide caliber analogies; **broader deep-learning theory is the common audience across the table**. Later citations, reputation and landmark status are excluded from these publication-time comparisons.

## DMFT in detail: different gains, no supported strict superiority

H23 is **Bordelon–Pehlevan (2022), the self-consistent dynamical-field description and computation of wide feature-learning dynamics**. This assessment does not grade the entire DMFT tradition or every later DMFT theorem and solver. The frozen [case record](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/studies/closure_historical_calibration/HISTORICAL_ADVOCATE.md:275) and [calibration's residual-difference statement](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/studies/closure_historical_calibration/CALIBRATION.md:100) govern its grades.

| Scientific gain | H23 | Current T2+T3 |
|---|---|---|
| Added access to a previously known rich limit | Self-consistent two-time kernel/response variables, stochastic-process computation and informative simplifications. | Initialized current-state approximation preserving action/adjoint correlations, with convergence to specified dynamics and observations. |
| Principal strength | Mechanistic and computational access through evolving kernels and responses. | Constructive autonomous realizability and a scoped actual-network/numerical convergence bridge. |
| Consequential boundary | History and sample-pair state remain; the inspected derivation/solver is not a general certified convergence or efficiency theorem. | Fixed population order retains laws; effective accuracy-resource control and broad validated computational advantage are not established by the qualitative theorem. |

Both develop access to dynamics whose existence is already supplied by close predecessors. Saying “the process already existed” cannot discount H23 while the target receives B merely because it supplies another representation. Conversely, an older process description does not make the target's initialized approximation theorem redundant. Each adds a different capability.

Current-state autonomy at a chosen resolution also does not establish resources independent of horizon at a fixed target accuracy: the needed order and quadrature may grow with horizon. This is why the history-free equations do not yet demonstrate computational dominance over DMFT's practical self-consistent access. It limits that particular consequence without making rates a universal significance gate.

A proved convergence guarantee is a substantive mathematical capability. It is not an automatic significance premium over a formal but scientifically informative formulation. H23's kernel/response description and nonlinear computation retain their value without being a certified solver; the target's guarantee retains its value without predicting a learning clock or endpoint. Their different strengths support **comparable/overlapping publication-time significance**, not a claim that one solves everything the other does.

The advocate previously did prefer the target's central specialist category over H23's center, and explicitly said so in the intervening clarification. This report withdraws that preference after the symmetry audit. The critic's center never rose above H. Both retain an unresolved H/B boundary concerning how much constructive autonomy, by itself, changes what the specialist field can analyze or do. The inaccessible full π-limit paper remains a priority-comparison gap; absence of access is not evidence that a predecessor lacks an equivalent result.
