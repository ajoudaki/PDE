# Phase 2 — release only after the historical calibration is frozen

This assignment is sealed by process until the supervisor records the Phase 1
freeze and explicitly authorizes access. The same two agents continue, using
only the frozen calibration, the released primary evidence packet and relevant
public primary literature. Earlier agent verdicts, grading rubrics, assessment
milestone contracts and conversation opinions remain prohibited inputs.

## Task

The assessment target is the autonomous observable closure program C-H1–C-H4,
its direct circle experiments, and its general-dimensional first-order/MNIST/PCA
implementation. The complete canonical chapters are available to inspect its
dependencies; do not silently add unrelated book results to the contribution
being graded. Locate primary sources through PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_GUIDE.md and use
the frozen packet copies. Original data paths listed in its artifact map are
authorized for bounded read-only inspection if needed, not reruns.

Apply equal scrutiny to historical and target results. Reconstruct material
limitations even when a paper did not highlight them. Do not penalize a result
for a longer or more candid limitations record. Keep evidence confidence separate
from scientific significance, and judge restrictions by their consequence for
the central contribution and scope. Revisit prior historical judgments only
where this clarification materially changes reasoning; preserve any revisions.

Explain the closure construction simply, then establish the exact contribution.
Distinguish (a) finite architecture/optimizer, (b) initialization and closure
construction, (c) convergence theorem and its actual scope, and (d) computational
consequences and empirical evidence. Match equations, Gaussian initialization,
scaling/metric, depth/activation, data domain, observables, topology, time horizon,
limit order and information retained against the closest literature.

Classify comparisons as exact reformulation, extension, new guarantee for a
familiar construction, genuinely different approach or unresolved. Familiar
ingredients neither establish redundancy nor establish originality. Do not
deduce exact priority from a bounded search. Inspect recent primary predecessors
as needed; distinguish new literature checks from historical rubric changes.

The advocate defends the strongest present and prospective case supported by
mathematics/data; the critic challenges it through specific arguments, adverse
evidence and predecessors. Debate directly, case by case and claim by claim.
Every decisive significance argument names relevant frozen historical case IDs
and says why the target deserves a higher, comparable or lower judgment. Apply
like standards to like limitations, explaining any claim-specific difference.

Long-time results, explicit rates, practical certificates, applications and
competitive benchmarks are not universal requirements. If you demand any for a
particular claimed significance, justify it from the claim and frozen historical
comparators. Sufficient upgrades must not silently become necessary conditions.

Do not change the frozen rubric to suit a desired conclusion. If a genuine
mistake/missing distinction forces a revision, preserve the old calibration,
document the reason and reassess every affected historical case before applying
it. State established achievement, evidence-supported implication and speculation
separately. Explain which program steps are already enabled and which need new
ideas. Include adverse/inconclusive experiments and theoretical limitations.

## Milestones only after the assessment

Propose the smallest scientifically meaningful packages resolving identified
objections or establishing a specified capability. Give objective pass/fail/
inconclusive criteria, claim-specific numerical/proof validity, dependencies,
and each agent's explicit scoped significance commitment if successful. Explain
threshold choices and historical anchors. These are sufficient conditions unless
necessity has a separate argument. No experiments or proof searches are authorized.

Aim for substantive agreement, not unanimous labels. Preserve factual uncertainty
and defensible differences as ranges with reasons. Do not bargain labels between
unrelated items. Record every material concession and unresolved boundary.

## Outputs and ownership

Advocate owns ASSESSMENT.md, the joint synthesis, and PHASE2_ADVOCATE.md.
Critic owns PHASE2_CRITIC.md and PHASE2_AUDIT.md. Same flat study and generated
namespace as Phase 1. Both read the complete proposed final assessment and confirm
that it accurately represents their own views and remaining disagreements; a
shared document need not have shared labels. Use immutable source packet paths
and record exact coverage. No new proof re-audit, replication, implementation,
experiment, promotion, Git action or modification of prior assessment artifacts.

The final deliverable contains simple explanation, precise contribution and
equation/theorem crosswalk, calibrated assessment with anchor comparisons,
established/supported/speculative distinctions, material concessions, residual
disagreements and minimal milestones with separate agent commitments.
