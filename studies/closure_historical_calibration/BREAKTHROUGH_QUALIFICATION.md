Qualification draft, 2026-09-16. This document settles what the specified results would mean **before** judging their feasibility. It reopens the present verdict; it does not modify the frozen historical calibration, earlier assessment, or evidence packet. All proposed packages are sufficient conditions, not necessary conditions or a cumulative checklist. No experiment, implementation, proof search or proof development was performed for this debate. Exact joint approval and the immutable snapshot are recorded externally by the coordinator.

The audiences remain **feature-learning theory/computation** and **broader deep-learning theory**. B means Breakthrough/Groundbreaking in the frozen rubric; H means Highly significant; S means Significant. Landmark requires later demonstrated influence and is not forecast here. Confidence in validity, confidence in priority and scientific significance remain separate. The number of disclosed limitations is never a score.

In plain terms, the construction replaces dependence on an ever-growing training record by a state that can be advanced and restarted from its current values. At fixed order its population state still contains two probability laws; only after further quadrature is it a finite list of numbers. The order required for a requested accuracy is not yet selected by an effective certificate.

## 2. Shared model, observables and decision rules

Except where a stronger R4 variant explicitly changes the input dimension, use the bias-free two-hidden-layer tanh network on the unit circle, with normalized input $u=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ and physical input $x=\sqrt2u$:

\[
h^1=\tanh(W_1u),\qquad h^2=\tanh(W_2h^1),\qquad f_n=c^Th^2/n.
\]

Stored Gaussian entry variances are $(1,1/n,1/n^2)$, block mobilities are $(n,1,n)$, and loss is the probability-weighted **unhalved** square loss. Finite networks retain the random readout; the limiting readout starts at zero. The target is this actual rich width-limit flow, not an easier readout-only flow or an independently chosen low-rank network. The initialized closure retains the complete joint marks and both orientations of the initialized action. The compatible dictionary includes the exhaustive initialized-word tail; polynomial degrees in the small experimental core are not angular Fourier degrees.

Let **C4** consist of $m\in\{2,3,4\}$ equally weighted, distinct unit-circle inputs, labels in $\{-1,+1\}$ with both signs, and every pair's geodesic separation in $[\pi/12,11\pi/12]$. Include every common rotation. This is a continuous class of genuinely interacting geometries, without duplicate-label conflicts or antipodal oddness conflicts. Restricting the data class makes these packages bounded; it is not a claim about generic datasets.

For layer $\ell$, write

\[
G_{\ell,t}(u,v)=\mathbb E[h^\ell_t(u)h^\ell_t(v)],\qquad
C_{\ell,t}(u,v)=\mathbb E[h^\ell_t(u)h^\ell_0(v)].
\]

The stronger paired observation is

\[
J_{\ell,t}(u,v)=\operatorname{Law}\big(h^\ell_t(u),h^\ell_t(v),h^\ell_0(u),h^\ell_0(v)\big).
\]

Gram requirements concern these explicitly named moments. A joint-law requirement means Wasserstein-2 distance for this four-dimensional bounded vector, not only moment matching, and not the entire unbounded parameter law. Each package states which it needs.

Admissible predictive inputs are the data, the specified initialization law and model, and finite explicitly specified spectral, variational or Gaussian-integration operations. Defining an answer by solving the original training trajectory to its unknown event or endpoint is not prediction. A certificate cannot query the unknown exact trajectory or an uncharged Gaussian/path oracle. Ordinary representation of all randomness in one unrestricted real number is not finite information accounting.

Width $n$, closure order $N$, population quadrature $P$, initialization quadrature $Q$, input quadrature when needed, step size and arithmetic precision remain different limits. For a fixed finite horizon, first identify the actual width limit, and independently justify the closure/numerical limit that reaches it. A theorem may replace the earlier nested limits by a proved joint schedule; choosing a diagonal without proof is not sufficient. Endpoint claims take the width limit at each finite time **before** $t\to\infty$. R1 additionally takes $\delta\downarrow0$ after this definition; its uniformity requirements are stated below. No package silently demands uniform finite-width accuracy at infinite time.

For every theorem package, success means proving the stated consequence under these hypotheses. A counterexample or a necessary claimed property shown false is failure of that package. A remaining proof gap, unresolved numerical control or inaccessible comparison theorem is **inconclusive**, not a disproof. Evidence from simulations alone cannot settle an all-time, all-input or all-accuracy theorem. Alternative genuinely equivalent formulations are allowed, but changing the class, consequence or grade commitment requires an explicitly recorded qualification revision.

## 3. R1 — Predict the learning clock rather than replay it

**Small package R1a.** Use two equal-mass points at angles $-\delta/2,+\delta/2$, labels $-1,+1$, $0<\delta\le\pi/24$. Let $\tau_\delta$ be the first physical time with loss at most $0.1$. Give a finite data/initialization-derived power/logarithmic expression $A(\delta)>0$ with either $\tau_\delta/A(\delta)\to1$ as $\delta\downarrow0$, or explicit uniform two-sided constant-factor bounds $0<c\le\tau_\delta/A(\delta)\le C<\infty$ for sufficiently small $\delta$. Thus the powers and logarithmic factors must be correct; an exact leading constant is valuable but not mandatory. Establish a parametric difference from the same model's full initial-NTK clock: the ratio of the two clocks tends to zero or infinity. Merely fitting a clock to trained outputs, giving bounds with different powers/logarithms, or computing its event time by integrating the rich flow is insufficient.

The full initial-kernel comparator is already explicit in the frozen source: if $D_\delta=k(0)-k(\delta)$, then the frozen loss is $e^{-2D_\delta t}$ and its threshold clock is $\log(10)/(2D_\delta)$. No hidden-feature trajectory is attributed to that kernel-only baseline.

**Consensus package R1b.** The same clock mechanism also covers the interacting four-point family with angles

\[
(0,\delta,\pi/2,\pi/2+\kappa\delta),\quad
y=(+1,-1,+1,-1),\quad w_i=1/4,
\]

for $0<\delta\le\pi/24$ and $\kappa\in[1/2,2]$. Give $A(\delta,\kappa)$ with relative clock error tending to zero uniformly on that interval, **or** correct power/logarithmic scaling with explicit two-sided constants uniform in $\delta,\kappa$ as above. Prove a uniform parametric departure from the own-initial-NTK clock on $\kappa\in[3/4,5/4]$ (one specified ratio tends to zero or infinity). The four-point condition distinguishes a transferable interaction mechanism from an isolated integrable pair. It does not require the whole learned function, all-time state convergence, generic dimensions or numerical speedup.

|---|---|---|
| R1a pair alone | B / H | H–B / H |
| R1b interacting four-point clock | **B / H** | **B / H** |

H18 supports major exact training-clock theory in a structured family; H16 supports a restricted but explanatory dynamical law. The critic's R1a crux is whether the pair removes a reusable obstacle or supplies another isolated solvable model. R1b addresses that precise crux. A non-kernel clock alone does not establish a general representation-selection theory, hence neither assigns broad B.

Existing-result exclusion: C.4.5 already gives the orthogonal pair's feature clock and exponential fitting bound. C.4.9 gives an added-component slow clock $\tau=\epsilon t$ for a small-contamination episode. R1 requires the sharp singular-separation clock and interacting-family comparison above, not merely another declaration that a physical clock exists or differs locally from its frozen counterpart.

## 4. R2 — An energy principle that controls the omitted state for all time

On C4, construct a non-tautological Lyapunov/relative-energy mechanism controlling the initialized directions omitted by the finite-order closure, including their forward and reverse feedback. Derive a computable bound $\rho(N)\downarrow0$ such that, for the same initialized population closures,

\[
\sup_{\mu\in C4}\sup_{t\ge0}
\max\left\{\sup_u|f_{N,t}(u)-f_t(u)|,
\max_{\ell=1,2}\sup_{u,v}|G^N_{\ell,t}-G_{\ell,t}|,
\max_{\ell=1,2}\sup_{u,v}|C^N_{\ell,t}-C_{\ell,t}|\right\}
\le\rho(N).
\]

The bound may depend on the fixed C4 separation constants and model but not on the unknown trajectory or horizon. It must establish the needed noncollapse/stability rather than assume convergence or a uniformly positive tangent-Gram gap. The error bound and its ingredients must be available from initialization and, if needed for certification, the retained current state. A positive-energy norm with no control of the displayed errors does not suffice.

The new consequence is **order independent of horizon** for a nonlinear rich flow. Fixed order still includes two population laws. Finite-particle, finite-precision all-time computation is not required by this minimal package; adding it would bundle a different numerical result. Neither global interpolation nor identification of the final function is required. Infinite time is essential to this particular consequence, not a general breakthrough gate.

Both agents commit **B specialist / H broad**. This exceeds ordinary gradient-loss descent and the finite-time truncation consequence in H21; it addresses the persistent tail-feedback obstacle in history-based rich dynamics such as H23. H14 illustrates how an energy/geometry structure matters through its consequence, not through renaming the gradient metric. A coordinate change or decreasing original loss without this control is H or below, depending on what else it establishes.

Existing-result exclusion: fixed-order exact dissipation, polynomial raw bounds, existence through every finite time and restart are already proved in C-H2/C-H3. The source already bounds omitted initialized-action and learned-increment terms on compact sets of the exact T40 trajectory; those proof defects are not computable all-time certificates. C.4.6 bounds a homogeneous linearized propagator uniformly at the fitted orthogonal reference, and C.4.9 has horizon-independent source control under a small integrated perturbation premise. None is the stated nonlinear all-time closure error on C4.

## 5. R3 — Geometry that forces global fitting

For every C4 law, prove that the specified actual population gradient flow from its Gaussian initialization satisfies $\mathcal L(t)\to0$. Supply a new geometry/diversity/escape mechanism establishing the necessary persistence of useful directions; do not assume convergence of the trajectory, a uniform Gram spectral gap, or that training reaches an already favorable basin. Identification with the actual width-first flow is part of the claim. A theorem only saying that a stationary or convergent point is globally optimal, or that each finite-time Gram is nonsingular, does not establish this result.

This package does **not** require selecting a unique off-training endpoint function, a rate, a closure certificate, or all-time finite-width approximation. Both agents commit **B specialist / H broad**: it resolves a concrete rich-training optimization obstruction on a continuous interacting class. H14/H15 are relevant scoped global-optimization advances; H18 cautions that exact structure can make a family special without making it valueless.

The nearest new comparison is Chen–Yang–Zhao–Gu (2025). Its inspected theorem proves feature independence at finite discrete iterates under specific geometric assumptions; its corollary assumes weights cease changing at a finite time before concluding zero future batch errors. It does not supply the asserted asymptotic loss convergence. Its default effective readout variance is $n^{-2}$, whereas the target effective readout $c/n$ has variance $n^{-4}$. The target cannot inherit the theorem by matching only its zero initial output. These are comparison boundaries, not a criticism based on undisclosed assumptions.

Existing-result exclusion: the canonical orthogonal opposite-label tanh pair already fits globally. Other global families use orthogonal data, positive arctan-based activations, small affine perturbations or different architectures. C.4.9 supplies a finite constrained learning episode, while C.4.10 contracts finite-mode excess risk toward an explicit approximation floor; neither asserts arbitrary-accuracy fitting or an all-time endpoint for its changed law. R3 must retain unmodified tanh and the whole stated C4 class.

## 6. R4 — Predict the selected function

**R4a, C4.** Give an explicit data/initialization-only variational, spectral or comparably finite mathematical selection principle that uniquely determines an $L^2$ whole-circle function (up to equality almost everywhere) together with its training-point values. Prove that the actual width-first rich flow converges to that prediction in $L^2$ of uniform circle measure and at the training points. The principle cannot define its answer through the trained trajectory or an unknown endpoint state. Establish zero training loss and a strict difference from the own initial-kernel interpolant on an open neighborhood of the four-point configuration

\[
\theta=(0,\pi/2,5\pi/6,4\pi/3),\qquad y=(+1,-1,+1,-1),
\]

where each angle varies independently by less than $\pi/120$, with equal masses. This neighborhood lies within C4. Strict difference means a positive $L^2$ function discrepancy, not a differing parameter representation. Uniform endpoint convergence is deliberately not required; a meaningful selected function may have a discontinuous limit.

Both agents commit **B specialist / H broad**. Representability, oddness, nonzero higher Fourier modes, a fit of a tanh-shaped curve after training, or a closed formula containing the unknown trajectory does not meet the package.

**R4b, arbitrary finite circle data.** Extend the same selection mechanism to every finite set of distinct, pairwise non-antipodal unit-circle inputs with strictly positive probability weights and labels $\pm1$ containing both signs. No uniform minimum separation, number-of-points bound, or constants uniform in the dataset are demanded. Retain the convergence and non-kernel distinction above. Advocate: **B/B**. Critic: **B/H–B**. The critic's broad crux is the degree to which a circle selection mechanism organizes broader deep-learning theory, rather than only this geometry.

**R4c, each fixed dimension.** The same mechanism holds for every fixed $d\ge2$, every finite set of distinct pairwise non-antipodal unit-sphere inputs, strictly positive weights and both-sign labels. Use $x=\sqrt d\,u$, the same Gaussian model/metric, convergence in $L^2$ of uniform sphere measure plus training-point convergence, and a unique selected prediction almost everywhere. Constants need not be uniform in dimension. Preserve a strict non-kernel discrepancy at least on the R4a subfamily. Both agents commit **B/B**. The extra consequence is a transferable principle for the function selected by rich deep training, not a reward for merely increasing dimension. No activation/depth generality, rates or benchmark package is added.

H16/H17 calibrate the importance of implicit-bias discoveries despite restrictive models, and the need to distinguish a theorem from a broader conjecture. H20 supports a reusable principle for feature-learning behavior. Chizat–Bach (2020) identifies a max-margin limit for homogeneous mean-field classification assuming the requisite gradient/normalized-measure convergence; Lyu–Li identifies limiting-direction KKT behavior for homogeneous models and exponential-tail losses. Neither inspected result identifies the unique squared-loss, nonhomogeneous tanh function asserted here. The new claim must prove its own convergence, rather than borrow language from those different limits.

Existing-result exclusion: C.4.5 characterizes the reference endpoint by running its feature equation. C.4.9 already derives a nonlinear current-state selection equation, recomputing its projection and hidden features during one finite added-atom episode. Thus “a nonlinear selection equation exists” is not the new result. C.4.10's Fourier target family is independently specified and learned to a controlled finite-episode floor; C.5 already proves a strict local matched-loss test advantage for a fixed cosine teacher. R4 instead identifies the actual endpoint function without solving its training path. Its non-kernel clause does not claim the first evidence that features change or can improve test risk.

## 7. R5 — Certified finite-horizon approximation of the rich limit

On C4 through physical $T=40$, provide an initialized solver and a certificate computed solely from admissible inputs and its finite retained calculations. For every $0<\varepsilon\le1/4$, the algorithm must terminate and certify

\[
\sup_{0\le t\le40}\sup_u|\widehat f_t(u)-f_t(u)|\le\varepsilon,
\qquad
\max_{\ell=1,2}\sup_{0\le t\le40}\sup_{u,v}
W_2(\widehat J_{\ell,t}(u,v),J_{\ell,t}(u,v))\le\varepsilon.
\]

Give an explicit **uniform polynomial bound in $\varepsilon^{-1}$** for total initialization and integration resources on this fixed class and horizon: order, population/initializer quadrature, precision and bit operations are counted. Input coordinates must be provided to certified accuracy; their reading/representation cost is counted in the usual accuracy input, with no hidden exact-real oracle. A finite, proved joint refinement schedule is admissible. The certificate must control the actual population target, including both initialized action orientations, not only successive closures or numerical error inside a declared reduced ODE. No dense width or uncharged historical trajectory may be hidden in preprocessing.

Both agents commit **B specialist theory/computation / H broad**. H01 means that rates are not necessary for all B claims; here the accuracy-resource guarantee is the particular new capability being promised. Compared with H21, this certifies execution of the rich nonlinear limit and its hidden observations, rather than a correction hierarchy about a kernel regime. H06/H07 support the significance of making a limiting object reliably accessible. A finite-order bound involving the unknown exact trajectory, generic computability, or a certificate that never reaches requested tolerances would not suffice. A useful qualitative extension or partial defect estimate can still merit H without fulfilling this package.

Existing-result exclusion: C-H3 already has finite algorithms, ordered numerical limits and explicit fixed-resolution state counts. Its proof-only omitted-source error is not a solver input or a tolerance selector. The initialized-word tail establishes compatible-dictionary density, not completeness or a rate for the small polynomial core alone. R5 must account for whichever dictionary it actually uses.

## 8. R6 — Reusable computation without growing history under repeated tasks

Use four fixed circle angles $(0,\pi/6,\pi/3,\pi/2)$ and equal weights. Each episode lasts 40 units of physical time, with labels chosen online from

\[
A=(+1,-1,+1,-1),\quad B=(+1,+1,-1,-1),\quad C=(+1,-1,-1,+1).
\]

The Gaussian initialization occurs once. Training carries its reached state between episodes; it does not reinitialize or restart from an answer supplied by the dense network. For every finite episode count $K$, every sequence in $\{A,B,C\}^K$, and every $0<\varepsilon\le1/4$, a finite algorithm must approximate the actual width-first flow's predictions and both layers' current/current and initial/current Grams on the fixed panel $\theta_j=2\pi j/256$, uniformly over physical time through $40K$, with absolute maximum error at most $\varepsilon$.

Prove certified scientific working storage of at most $S(\varepsilon)$ **bits**, independent of $K$ and width, and total scientific **bit operations** at most $K C(\varepsilon)$, including initialization, the number of integration steps and all numerical precision. This is an overall work bound, not merely bounded work per step. An episode counter needs $O(\log K)$ bits and may contribute $O(K\log K)$ elementary counter/stream bookkeeping; this explicit overhead is not hidden in “constant memory.” The prescribed label stream and streamed outputs are not stored as part of the working state. No Gaussian/path oracle, stored training history, dense initialization sample growing with $K$, or replay through all previous episodes is allowed. A fixed-dimensional exact-real ODE without certified finite information/accuracy is insufficient.

Both agents commit **B computational specialist / H broad**. This is a new indefinitely continuable capability under a concrete family of forcing, not a finite constant-factor speedup. Total work is not required to be independent of time. Neither all dimensions nor all data distributions are required.

Compare retained information and equations with matched versions of π/TP/DMFT and with initialized fixed-basis or dynamical low-rank methods; do not assume a universal lower bound against all future algorithms. A complete proved algorithm is sufficient. If an implementation is claimed, report matched-accuracy comparisons to the actual dense finite-width reference, independently initialized fixed two-sided core, and DLRT with fair accuracy/resource tuning on $K\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$; include initialization, precision, steps, population refinement and the finite-width reference error. These measurements validate the implementation, not a separate significance quota. A method that fails the required target fidelity is not an eligible cost comparator.

H06/H07/H24 support computational reformulations when they unlock a reusable capability. H23 and the π primary account supply the relevant history burden; DYNAMITE already shows adaptive history compression, so a claim that every contemporary solver stores all past times would be false. The old three-MNIST-pair matched-fidelity speed/memory package remains **H/H**, not B simply because this debate was reopened.

Existing-result exclusion: fixed-order own-state continuation for arbitrarily many finite times is already available, but does not supply uniform target fidelity at fixed finite information. C.4.9's one small-contamination episode and C.4.10's finite added-data learning statement are different from indefinitely many arbitrary label switches. R6 requires target accuracy and the stated resource guarantee, not just a loop around an existing restart routine.

## 10. Exclusions, precedents and the qualification boundary

Several attractive-looking claims are already present in the permitted canonical dependencies or source-level identities, and are not new completion packages: global existence/energy for other specially chosen positive activations; the exact orthogonal opposite-label tanh feature clock, interpolation and exponential loss bound; representation of its endpoint by integrating that feature ODE; ordinary PSD tangent dynamics; angular oddness and lack of a literal Fourier cutoff. These results are inspected as novelty exclusions only. They are not silently added to T1–T7's present grade.

The selected packages isolate different consequences: R1 predicts a time; R2 controls omitted state uniformly in time; R3 guarantees fitting; R4 selects a function; R5 certifies a finite-horizon computation; R6 guarantees reusable finite-memory continuation. R4 includes fitting for its own class, but R1/R2/R5/R6 do not require R3 or R4. R2 does not require finite-node all-time numerics. R5 does not require an endpoint. Their labels are not additive and they are alternatives, not prerequisites for each other.
