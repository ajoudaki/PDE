## Scientific debate BEFORE feasibility

First reopen the strongest present breakthrough case, including the possibility
of a specialist breakthrough for a particular component even if the whole
program merits a different broad judgment. Explain the actual obstacle removed,
closest predecessor, historical comparator and central consequence. Explicitly
agree or disagree on each supported present claim.

Then explore a genuinely varied portfolio. At minimum address:

1. Training-horizon prediction: forecasting time to a declared loss/feature event,
   or a useful validity/settling horizon. Distinguish computing a trajectory from
   predicting its timescale, and a finite fitting horizon from an endpoint.
2. Energy/Lyapunov principles: what would add substance beyond the usual decrease
   of the same training loss under a gradient metric? Name the new consequence.
3. Reformulated geometry and global convergence: distinguish coordinate changes
   from a mechanism resolving a real optimization obstacle; separate convergence
   to a stationary point, global optimality and selection of a particular limit.
4. Learned-function classes and implicit bias: distinguish Fourier truncation,
   representability, fitting an observed curve and predicting the learned function
   from initialization/data without observing the trained answer.
5. Stronger approximation theory: finite-order accuracy, density, stability,
   refinement, dimension/depth/activation extensions or useful certification.
   Do not restrict this route to the previous M2b.
6. Computational advances: a concrete capability or accuracy-cost regime, with
   appropriate comparators and full information/resource accounting.

You may add a more promising route. A negative/obstruction/classification theorem
can qualify if its consequence is comparably substantial; success need not mean
that the current approximation works everywhere. Reject merely restated identities,
vague desired outcomes, hidden trajectory oracles and easier-model substitutions.
Do not demand all dimensions, depths, horizons, rates and benchmarks together.

For each promising route negotiate one smallest scientifically meaningful
breakthrough package, plus a stronger variant only if needed for the broader
audience. Specify the model, initialization/metric, data/target class, observable,
horizon or endpoint limit, admissible inputs, limit order, and objective theorem

than saying it will be predeclared later. State why each condition is needed
