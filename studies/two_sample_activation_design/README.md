The global positive theorem permits C2 shapes with bounded first and
second derivatives, including unbounded linearly growing shapes.
Its sufficient coefficient is polynomial in input separation, but its
prefactor remains microscopic. A practical moderate-amplitude global
training theorem has not been established.

Three independent complete-proof reviews returned PASS for the stated
extension and delimited supporting results, with no required repairs.
Their acceptance does not cover a moderate-amplitude global theorem.

The original architecture, Gaussian initialization, raw metric and
finite readout are retained. All old mathematical files remain unchanged.
DEPENDENCIES.json and CANDIDATE_HASHES.json identify exact mathematical
versions. No training experiment or optimizer change was used.
