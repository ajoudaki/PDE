# Odd convex activation under absolute angular separation

Target: the original canonical three-hidden-layer raw-metric Gaussian initialized network, now with the same activation psi_e(z)=(1-e)z+e arctan(z) in every hidden layer, and normalized inputs whose pairwise cosine belongs to (-1+delta,1-delta). Prove a positive separation-only cutoff valid for every fixed smaller positive e, first for two binary-labeled inputs, then attempt the three-input class. The stronger closed restriction |rho|<=1-delta is used where nonvacuous; for strict separation the nonempty range is 0<delta<1.

Preserve raw initialization, GF and simultaneous raw GD with step n^-2, canonical action spaces and genuine adjoints, uniqueness/continuation, full-sequence compact-time joint convergence in the earlier prediction/kernel/path/velocity/probe topologies, and all-time nonaffinity plus nonzero initial hidden block/sample motion and projected-kernel variation. Do not replace unit-slope convex mixtures by shifted or large-gain activations. No width-dependent or horizon-dependent activation coefficients.

Quantitative aims: explicit positive monotone cutoff; distinguish sufficient bounds from optimal thresholds and necessities. A user-suggested power such as delta^2 must not be claimed sufficient without proof, and a deliberately smaller witness does not establish sharpness. The monotonicity of an intrinsic uniform admissibility threshold follows from nested geometric classes, not monotonicity of arbitrary proof constants.

Authorization: theoretical derivations, local proof artifacts, checking primary source proofs as needed, and diversified independent adversarial agents. No numerical experiments or publication. Existing studies remain unchanged.

Preliminary state: an existing two-input odd-activation proof is available and is being rechecked without treating its past review verdicts as mathematical evidence. Its source cutoff contains a finite but unspecified response constant; this project is to make that dependence explicit and audit the resulting report. Pairwise separation allows singular three-input Gram matrices, including the equilateral triple with equal labels, whose odd affine reference is stationary. This is a failure of the direct affine-reference route, not a counterexample to nonlinear global population existence.

Final audit isolation: new reviewers receive only an immutable mathematical report/bundle, no prior dialogue, authoring notes, previous findings or review endorsements. Substantive objections require repair and fresh manuscript-only reviewers.
