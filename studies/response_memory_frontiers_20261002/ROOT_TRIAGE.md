# Root triage and rejected shortcuts

This note records root's independent screening. It is not evidence of novelty
or a completed research result. Source URLs were retrieved through the web tool
on 2026-10-02; abstracts/search excerpts alone do not support imported theorems.

## Initial three-route comparison

Attention: extending the outer-product gradient identity is straightforward,
but that alone is not a substantive application. Worst-case softmax sensitivity
in head width is not a reachable trained-trajectory separation. Gauge/balance
identities and repairs have substantial direct precedent, including
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34549,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17816 and
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34272. The route is asked to inspect these primary
sources before accepting any balance-based originality claim. No GPU campaign
is authorized for this route yet.

Recurrence: exact Schur/Woodbury reduction of a contractive reservoir with a
low-rank correction is insufficient as novelty. The route's deciding question
is whether causal paired training memories reproduce actually learned slow
responses at a rank useful for end-to-end solving, against strong nonlinear
solvers and matched factor-dynamics controls. GPU1 pilot authorization:
at most 12 trained runs and 25 GPU-process minutes, protocol before execution.

Control: the potentially distinctive claim is approximation uniform over
arbitrarily frequently switching bounded sample weights. The product-tail
mechanism may tolerate poorly approximated credit histories because the forward
factor remains regular. Need a complete controlled-flow proof, including zero
activity intervals, and a numerical stress that explicitly resolves every
switch. This is not a replay-free continual learner: the original input list
is still evaluated and stored. GPU0 pilot authorization: at most 12 fits and
20 GPU-process minutes, protocol before execution. Primary metrics must test
the claimed separation rather than just show low loss.

## An additional random-sketch idea rejected before implementation

Root considered replacing input/time indices with common random-sign or
Brownian projections of forward and backward histories. Products of paired
sketches could give an unbiased instantaneous matrix drift, but endogenous
feedback defeats naive unbiased-trajectory claims and induces additional
diffusion. More fundamentally this has close precedent in the rank-one trick
behind UORO and low-rank gradient accumulation for nonvolatile-memory training:
https://openreview.net/pdf/ded4869c017f6803939dcc2ebf18c9cca5342392.pdf.
The retrieved primary-source excerpt explicitly discusses sums of outer
products of activations and backpropagated errors, low-rank accumulation and
the sample-versus-time distinction. No implementation is justified without a
new discriminator. No claim that this eliminates every stochastic extension.

## Guardrails applying to every branch

- All-time small-label theorem and its population conclusion do not transfer
  to new architectures or controlled losses without a proof.
- An operator identity is not a faster algorithm; count every solve and dense
  initialized-matrix action, and compare complete runtime at matched accuracy.
- Retaining low-rank physical weights is not the same as retaining the dense
  learning dynamics; current prediction equality is not continuation equality.
- A certificate using inaccessible dense histories or a huge exponential bound
  does not demonstrate useful online control.
- Negative results or correct elementary extensions do not complete the user's
  active goal of a substantial, independently validated research contribution.
