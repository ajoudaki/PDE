# Frozen protocol: autonomous closure search for exactly three hidden layers

Status: frozen before candidate construction and before inspecting any
depth-three project artifact or accepted derivative table, 20 August 2026.

## 1. Canonical model

There is one unit-norm input, one scalar label \(y_\star\), identity
activation, three equal hidden widths \(n\), and four trainable effective
linear maps.  After projecting the first map onto the single input,

\[
f_n(A,V,W,u)=n^{-2}A^T V W u,
\qquad
A,u\in\mathbb R^n,\quad V,W\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}.
\]

All raw parameter entries are independent \(N(0,1)\) at initialization.
Every block has unit metric weight.  Feature time is generated by
\(D_n=n\nabla f_n\cdot\nabla\).  Physical full-MSE time is

\[
\dot\theta=2\eta(y_\star-f_n)n\nabla f_n,\qquad \eta>0.
\]

The limit order is width first at fixed physical time.  The required
identification mode is convergence in probability, uniformly on every
compact physical-time interval.

## 2. Admissible closure

An admissible witness must have:

1. exactly one evolution-time coordinate \(s\);
2. one explicit, time-independent source space \((\Omega,\nu)\), derived
   only from initialization;
3. a fixed finite collection of scalar real or complex fields
   \(\Psi_j(\omega,s)\), \(\omega\in\Omega\);
4. a current-time autonomous vector field using only those fields,
   explicit source functions, and current integrals against \(\nu\);
5. restartability from the present fields;
6. direct current-state readouts of prediction and loss;
7. state and source complexity independent of width, Taylor order, elapsed
   time, and requested horizon.

A finite-dimensional ODE is admissible and stronger.  A fixed
multidimensional classical source coordinate is admissible when it carries
genuine initialization data or noncommutative overlap data.  An integral
with no mathematical role is not admissible.

## 3. Forbidden substitutions and hidden-state loopholes

The following fail the contract:

- a second training-time coordinate;
- covariance or response kernels \(C(s,s')\), \(R(s,s')\);
- Volterra or other integrals over past training time;
- trajectory playback, time-dependent oracle forcing, or coefficients fitted
  from the output/loss curve;
- width-dependent vectors, matrices, eigenvector tables, outer products, or
  atomic measures with \(n\) atoms;
- an abstract operator, Hilbert-space vector, path space, Fock space,
  noncommutative word hierarchy, or unbounded matrix-level resolvent used as
  a finite symbol for hidden unbounded information;
- a source real number or field initialized with arbitrary-precision
  encodings of the future trajectory;
- DMFT or any weaker nonrestartable two-time representation;
- replacement by depth two, frozen features, lazy training, finite Taylor
  data, or another optimizer/scaling.

## 4. Independence firewall

Before a candidate is frozen:

- derive only from the finite network equations and the valid depth-two
  result already contained in the chat;
- do not inspect any other project study, prior depth-three proposal, DMFT
  formulation, external closure theory, or accepted depth-three derivative
  value;
- do not fit, infer, or reverse-engineer coefficients from the loss curve.

A candidate is frozen only when its source, initial fields, autonomous
equations, and readouts are explicit enough to generate derivatives without
free fitted coefficients.  Record its file hash before opening comparison
artifacts.

## 5. Claim ladder

- A1: exact finite-width feature equations.
- A2: exact finite-width conserved quantities and reductions.
- A3: explicit deterministic fixed initialization source.
- B1: internally well-posed autonomous field dynamics.
- E1: identification of the field dynamics with the width limit.
- F1: uniform-in-probability output/loss convergence on every compact
  physical-time interval.
- W1: the particular frozen witness satisfies the whole contract.
- H1: some admissible witness exists.

Derivative agreement is only a coefficient-level check on W1.  It cannot
establish E1 or F1.  Failure of W1 does not falsify H1.

## 6. Sharp conjecture

There exists a single explicit classical measured space \((\Omega,\nu)\), a
fixed integer \(m\), initialization functions, an autonomous locally
Lipschitz current-time evolution for
\(\Psi_1,\ldots,\Psi_m:\Omega\to\mathbb C\), and direct readouts \(F,L\),
all independent of \(n\) and of the time horizon, such that the finite-width
three-hidden-layer full-MSE output and loss converge to \(F,L\) uniformly in
probability on every compact physical-time interval.

## 7. Concrete falsifiers

- **Witness falsifier:** an exact finite-width term is not represented by the
  proposed current fields/source, or the frozen jet mismatches an accepted
  exact coefficient.
- **Identification falsifier:** the proposed finite-width source has no
  deterministic limit controlling the named observables.
- **Restartability falsifier:** two realizable histories have the same
  proposed present state but different future derivatives.
- **Non-vacuity falsifier:** a source coordinate hides a width-dependent
  matrix, a noncommutative word tower, or past-time data.
- **Existence falsifier:** only a theorem excluding every admissible witness
  could reject H1.  Failure of any finite route below is not such a theorem.

## 9. Preregistered derivative comparison

Only after a candidate file and its SHA-256 hash are frozen:

- compare its exact feature-time derivatives
  \(F^{(k)}(0)\), \(0\le k\le13\), with the authoritative independent
  depth-three table;
- primary metric: exact equality of every rational/integer coefficient;
- pass: all fourteen entries agree and parity/normalization checks pass;
- fail: one exact mismatch, which falsifies that witness;
- inconclusive: the comparison source is ambiguous, the candidate recurrence
  is not exact, or a resource/validity gate fails.

The comparison may use one exact-symbolic run, capped at two minutes and
1 GiB.  No parameter fitting, search over candidate coefficients, or
post-comparison repair is allowed under the same candidate hash.  A repaired
candidate requires a new explicit version and is reported as a new witness,
not as the preregistered one.

Regardless of a pass, E1 and F1 require separate analytic proofs.
