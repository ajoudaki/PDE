# Author response to Round 0

We thank the reviewer for a technically exacting report. We accept the major-revision recommendation and have revised the manuscript around the contract the review identified as both valid and scientifically substantive: a fixed-finite-width, deep nonlinear gradient-flow trajectory is approximated on every prescribed compact horizon by an autonomous response-history moment system whose state does not grow with elapsed training time. The revision no longer confuses this theorem with net model-memory or runtime compression.

The central mathematical contribution survives the audit intact and is now presented more clearly. Each learned internal increment is generated by a time integral of a backward response against a forward response. The closure projects both endogenous histories, reconstructs the matrix, and feeds that reconstruction back through the nonlinear network. Orthogonality cancels the two mixed terms when the reconstructed product is differentiated, leaving a product of projection errors rather than a single projection error. This identity is what makes complete physical-trajectory control possible despite nonlinear feedback. Variant A turns it into an $O(P^{-1})$ compact-horizon bound; Variant B uses a joint response-speed coordinate to regularize both factors and obtains $O(P^{-2})$. These are fixed-width parameter-trajectory theorems, rather than output-only, kernel-only, width-limit, or exogenous-signal approximation statements.
