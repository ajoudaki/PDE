## Scientific contract to check

Determine from the candidate whether it establishes each item below.

- A deep fully connected network with fixed finite width `n`, training sample count
  `m`, and hidden depth `H >= 2`, under continuous-time feature-learning-scaled
  gradient flow and nonlinear hidden activations.
- Compression of the learned increments of the internal `n x n` matrices while
  retaining the initialized matrices and the uncompressed outer blocks.
- An autonomous, restartable order-`P` moment ODE whose stored history does not grow
  with elapsed training time.
- Uniform approximation of the physical parameter trajectory on every prescribed
  finite interval `[0,T]` at fixed `n,m,H,T` as `P -> infinity`.
- The exact hypotheses and proof status of the residual-activity-clock
  `O_T(P^{-1})` rate and response-speed-clock `O_T(P^{-2})` rate.
- Fidelity of the reported circle and MNIST predictors, with the numerical and
  experimental limitations stated in the evidence.

“Global on compact horizons” means uniform on each fixed finite `[0,T]`; it does not
mean one order or constant works for every `t >= 0`.
