# Quadratic depth two: exact one-time reductions and the unresolved bridge

Status: rigorous finite-width reductions and exact fixed-order controls;
**no claimed strict classical single-source closure**, 20 August 2026.

## 1. Normalization

Equip every width-`n` neuron space with

\[
\langle v,w\rangle_n=\frac1n v^{\mathsf T}w
\]

and write `G=W/sqrt(n)`.  For vectors `p,q`, let

\[
(p\otimes q)v=p\langle q,v\rangle_n.
\]

Feature time is generated by `D_n=n grad(f_n) dot grad`.  Physical full-MSE
time is obtained by multiplying the feature vector field by
`2 eta e`, where `e=y_* - f`.

The point of using feature time in the algebra below is only to remove the
common scalar clock.  Every final equation is returned to physical time.

## 2. Inner square and outer identity

Put

\[
X=u^2,\quad Z=GX,\quad R=G^*A,\quad
f=\langle A,Z\rangle_n=\langle R,X\rangle_n.
\]

Direct differentiation gives the exact feature equations

\[
\boxed{
A'=Z,\qquad X'=4X\odot R,\qquad G'=A\otimes X.}
\tag{2.1}
\]

Define `Q : R direct-sum H_C -> H_R` by

\[
Q(c,v)=cA+Gv,
\]

and define the rank-two self-adjoint operator

\[
S_X(c,v)=(\langle X,v\rangle_n,cX).
\]

Then (2.1) is exactly

\[
Q'=QS_X.                                                   \tag{2.2}
\]

Let `J=diag(-1,I)`, `M=Q^*Q`, and `L=JM`.  Since
`JS_X=-S_XJ`,

\[
\boxed{L'=[L,S_X].}                                       \tag{2.3}
\]

Thus `L` is isospectral.  Equivalently,

\[
GG^*-A\otimes A                                           \tag{2.4}
\]

is conserved.  The current feedback field is the lower block of `Le_0`:

\[
R=P_CLe_0.                                                 \tag{2.5}
\]

Equations (2.3), (2.5), and `X'=4X odot R` are an exact one-time Lax
reduction.  The feature kernel is

\[
\boxed{
K_{QI}=\langle X,HX\rangle_n
       +g\langle X^2\rangle_n
       +4\langle X\odot R^2,1\rangle_n,}
\tag{2.6}
\]

where `g=<A,A>_n` and `H=G^*G` are the blocks of `M=JL`.
At Gaussian initialization this gives `K_QI(0)=3+3+4=10`.

### Eigenvalues alone do not close

The isospectral law is useful, but it is not the identity-model solution.
The evolving spectral weights are essential.  Here is an exact finite-state
witness.

Take a three-dimensional column space, `H=I`,

\[
X=(1,2,3),\quad
r_1=(1,0,0),\quad
r_2=(1/3,-2/3,2/3),\quad g=1/3.
\]

Both vectors have the same norm and the same inner product with `X`.  The
positive semidefinite Gram blocks

\[
M_i=\begin{pmatrix}g&r_i^*\\r_i&I\end{pmatrix}
\]

have rank three and are related by a column-space orthogonal conjugation.
Consequently `L_i=JM_i` have the same eigenvalues.  They also have the same
`X` and the same output

\[
\langle r_1,X\rangle_n=\langle r_2,X\rangle_n=1/3.
\]

Nevertheless,

\[
\langle X\odot r_1^2,1\rangle_n=1/3,
\qquad
\langle X\odot r_2^2,1\rangle_n=7/9,
\]

so (2.6) gives different instantaneous output speeds.  A fixed scalar
eigenvalue measure, even supplemented by the current `X` marginal and
output, therefore cannot be restart sufficient.  A successful inverse-
spectral IDE must evolve the relative spectral-weight field as well.

## 3. Inner identity and outer square

Now put

\[
X=u,\quad Z=GX,\quad B=A\odot Z,\quad R=G^*B,
\quad f=\langle A,Z^2\rangle_n.
\]

The exact feature equations are

\[
\boxed{
A'=Z^2,\qquad X'=2R,\qquad G'=2B\otimes X.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Define `P : H_C -> H_R direct-sum R` by

\[
Pv=(Gv,\langle X,v\rangle_n)
\]

and

\[
S_B(y,c)=(cB,\langle B,y\rangle_n).
\]

Then `P'=2S_BP`.  With `N=PP^*`, `J=diag(I,-1)`, and `L=JN`,

\[
\boxed{L'=2[L,S_B].}                                      \tag{3.2}
\]

The upper block of `Le_*` is `Z`; hence (3.2) together with
`A'=Z^2` is again an exact one-time Lax reduction.  Its kernel is

\[
\boxed{
K_{IQ}=\langle Z^4,1\rangle_n
       +4q\langle B^2,1\rangle_n
       +4\langle R^2,1\rangle_n,}
\tag{3.3}
\]

where `q=<X,X>_n`.  This gives `K_IQ(0)=3+4+4=11`.

As in Section 2, the Lax eigenvalues do not determine products formed in the
fixed neuron-coordinate algebra.  Spectral weights relative to that algebra
remain dynamical data.

## 4. Both layers square

For the canonical model let

\[
X=u^2,\quad Z=GX,\quad B=A\odot Z,\quad R=G^*B,
\quad f=\langle A,Z^2\rangle_n.
\]

The exact feature system is

\[
\boxed{
A'=Z^2,\qquad
X'=8X\odot R,\qquad
G'=2B\otimes X.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Its tangent kernel is

\[
\boxed{
K_{QQ}=\langle Z^4,1\rangle_n
       +4\langle B^2,1\rangle_n\langle X^2,1\rangle_n
       +16\langle X\odot R^2,1\rangle_n.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

At initialization the three terms are `27`, `36`, and `48`, giving the
required `111`.

The physical-time system is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\dot A&=2\eta e Z^2,\\
\dot X&=16\eta e X\odot R,\\
\dot G&=4\eta e B\otimes X,\\
\dot e&=-2\eta eK_{QQ},
\end{aligned}}
\qquad
Z=GX,\ B=A\odot Z,\ R=G^*B.                              \tag{4.3}
\]

Thus the output and loss are direct current-state readouts,

\[
f=y_*-e,\qquad \mathcal L=e^2.                            \tag{4.4}
\]

The row and column balance laws are

\[
\sum_jW_{ij}^2-2a_i^2=\text{constant},\qquad
\sum_iW_{ij}^2-\frac12u_j^2=\text{constant}.              \tag{4.5}
\]

Unlike (2.2) and (3.1), the rows or columns in (4.1) are driven by
coordinate-dependent boost generators.  Consequently the **same block-Gram
construction** stops at the diagonal balance data (4.5); it does not produce
one common Gram operator obeying a closed Lax commutator.  This is a failure
of that construction, not a theorem excluding every possible Lax
representation.

## 5. What the unbounded one-source resolvent would be

If an independently constructed source `rho` satisfies

\[
K(y)=K(0)+y^2\int_0^\infty\frac{\rho(d\lambda)}
                                      {1+\lambda y^2},     \tag{5.1}
\]

then the desired physical loss closure is simply

\[
\boxed{
\dot y=2\eta(y_*-y)
\left[K(0)+y^2\int_0^\infty
\frac{\rho(d\lambda)}{1+\lambda y^2}\right],
\qquad \mathcal L=(y_*-y)^2.}                             \tag{5.2}
\]

Unbounded support is allowed.  Hence (5.2) can have a finite real-axis
integral and a zero Taylor radius at the origin.  Zero radius is not an
obstruction to (5.2).

For `QQ`, all currently decidable moment gates through `mu_7` pass.  For
`IQ`, the new order-17 calculation also passes every gate through the two
`4 by 4` matrices.  For `QI`, however, the shifted `4 by 4` determinant is
strictly negative; see
[`PARTIAL_ORDER17_RESULTS.md`](PARTIAL_ORDER17_RESULTS.md).  Thus (5.1) with
a **nonnegative** source is already false as a universal partially
quadratic mechanism.  A signed/Krein resolvent or a different field IDE is
not excluded.

## 6. The exact operator lift and why it is not claimed as the answer

Equations (2.1), (3.1), and (4.3) have a constant number of vector/operator
species and only one training time.  Replacing the finite neuron spaces by a
fixed Gaussian traffic/Fock source would turn them into a width-independent
one-time operator equation and would reproduce the complete fixed-order Wick
grammar.

That observation is not promoted to the requested theorem for two reasons:

1. without an explicit finite-dimensional classical source and scalar-field
   realization, the traffic operator retains the full noncommuting relative
   basis information that the word `compressed` was meant to eliminate;
2. fixed-order traffic convergence does not identify a real positive-time
   Gaussian solution of the unbounded equation.  A well-posed real-axis
   solution and compact-time finite-width convergence still have to be
   proved.

Calling this operator lift the final PDE would therefore hide, rather than
solve, the two hard obligations.

## 7. Rigorous verdict

The following statements are proved here.

1. Each one-square model has an exact isospectral one-time Lax reduction.
2. The immutable eigenvalues alone are not a sufficient state; relative
   spectral weights affect the immediate loss velocity.
3. The exact partial jets through order seventeen are available, and the
   inner-square model disproves a universal positive-Stieltjes mechanism.
4. The both-square model has the exact one-time finite-width system (4.3),
   direct loss readout, and two balance families, but the partial block-Gram
   Lax construction does not extend to it.

What is **not** proved is the theorem requested in the protocol: an explicit,
strictly compressed, classical fixed-source IDE with a positive-time
width-limit identification for the Gaussian `QQ` network.  Nor is
nonexistence of every such IDE proved.  The zero-radius fact and the presence
of a two-time representation prove neither statement.
