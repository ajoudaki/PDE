# Project-wide research audit — working contract

Status: recovered-source consolidation and three-round isolated document review completed. All three final reviewers returned CLEAN at the synthesis's declared scope. Exhaustive private-history recovery and explicitly unclosed source-proof obligations remain incomplete. This is not a replacement theorem or completion of the main research goal.

## Objective

Recover the principal contributions of the 17 named PDE tasks and 19 named PDE-2 tasks, inspect their persisted mathematical sources and relevant corrections, and produce one unified master document connecting exact finite-order calculus, representation obstructions, population/gradient-flow limits, optimization, and the longer-term generalization question.

The scientific target is a predictive, autonomous, restartable description of genuinely deep, nonlinear, feature-learning training, justified as a joint width/step limit of the actual trained network, on horizons long enough to reach any prescribed training accuracy. A population description is a tool toward explaining learned representations, not an explanation of generalization by itself.

## Authority and exclusions

- Authorized: source inspection on both named projects, mathematical audit and correction, literature verification, creation of this consolidation, fresh isolated source-group audits and final adversarial reviewers (explicit user approval in this task).
- Existing research tasks remain paused. No task migration, messages to resume research, new mathematical campaign, numerical experiment, optimizer modification, commit, or branch change.
- Preserve all pre-existing repository changes. Work products live in this new directory; existing master reports remain historical sources.
- No imposed time, token, or compute budget. A source or review gap is recorded as such, not silently filled.

## Evidence rules

1. Conversation locates sources; persisted proofs and corrected exact-version audits determine claim scope.
2. Track depth as number of hidden layers, sample count/geometry, activation, initialization and readout scale, parameterization, clock, approximation family, observables, probability mode, time horizon, and limit order separately.
3. Distinguish identities, fixed-order jets, internally well-posed surrogate equations, finite-width bounds, identified population limits, compact-time convergence, all-time optimization, and generalization.
4. A representation no-go is not population nonexistence. Zero Taylor radius is not, by itself, a no-go for smooth ODEs or infinite-dimensional flows. A conditional arbitrary-depth theorem cannot be promoted using a separate fixed-depth proof.
5. Different clipping rules and different cutoff/width limits cannot be composed without a proved bridge.
6. Prior-art and novelty statements require checked primary sources. Physics-style derivations and rigorous DMFT theorems must not be conflated.
7. Record precisely what was freshly read, checked, inherited from an audit, only computationally evidenced, inaccessible, or superseded.

## Work stages

1. Resolve all named tasks and identify primary artifacts, including older turns and inherited dependencies.
2. Run disjoint isolated source-group audits; maintain source hashes, claim scopes, corrections, and coverage.
3. Independently inspect decisive proof steps and reconcile conflicts across groups and projects.
4. Write the single master document in scientific order, with a per-task contribution index and source/review provenance.
5. Run fresh isolated adversarial reviews of the document, correct findings, and record unresolved evidence limitations explicitly.

## Acceptance criteria

Every requested task has a resolved identity or an explicit unresolved-access entry. Every headline claim has a primary source, an exact scope, and a review-status qualifier. The main narrative contains no contradictory superseded conclusions, no unsupported global theorem, and no unsupported claim of literature-wide novelty. Recommendations preserve correlated data, effective nonlinearity and feature learning, and distinguish proved restrictions from convenient proof assumptions.
