## Shared normalization and geometry checks

Here `L` in a family name counts hidden layers. All selected networks have no biases and equal hidden widths `n`. Inputs are fixed independently of initialization, with `||x_a||^2=d` and Gram matrix `C_ab=x_a^T x_b/d`. Orthogonal or strictly interior correlations require dimension at least two. Antipodality means exactly `x_2=-x_1`, not merely negative correlation.

For `L` hidden layers, the stored readout is `W^(L+1)`, initialized with entry variance `n^-2`, and prediction is

`f_a = (W^(L+1))^T h_a^L / n`.

First-layer entry variance is `1/d`, intermediate matrix entry variance is `1/n`, and the initialization arrays are independent. Thus, if one instead calls the coefficient multiplying each top feature without an external `1/n` the readout, that coefficient is `W^(L+1)/n` and has variance `n^-4`. These are two descriptions of the same normalization; the stored array is not initialized with variance `n^-4`.

Loss is the unaveraged, unhalved sum `L_loss=sum_a(f_a-y_a)^2`. With residual-free deltas, the parameter metric is

`(d/n)||dW^1||_F^2 + sum_(ell=2)^L ||dW^ell||_F^2 + ||dW^(L+1)||^2/n`.

Consequently GF has first rate `-(2/d)sum_a r_a delta_a^1 x_a^T`, intermediate rates `-(2/n)sum_a r_a delta_a^ell (h_a^(ell-1))^T`, and readout rate `-2sum_a r_a h_a^L`. The associated residual equation is `dot r=-2Kr`; loss dissipation is `-dot L_loss=4r^TKr`, equal to squared parameter speed in this metric. These factors were checked in S (6)–(12), (24), C (22)–(24), E §1, and M (5), (8)–(10).

For S, C, and A/E, certified raw GD is exactly simultaneous Euler in these arrays with `eta_n=n^-2`, physical time `t=k eta_n`, affine raw-parameter interpolation, and recomputed nonlinear fields. It is not ordinary Euclidean GD on all displayed arrays. M/G concern continuous physical GF; neither their weighted clock nor their unweighted residual integral is a GD step schedule.

At `rho=-1`, first-row motion is counted once. S reduces to a single independent field. C uses `D=C/4` on the invariant antisymmetric subspace; at interior angles it uses `D=C^-1`. E gives the equivalent one-independent-field first metric at antipodality. In L3 the shifted activation is not odd: only the first preactivations are forced to be antipodal. Higher-layer oddness identities from pure atan cannot be imported.
