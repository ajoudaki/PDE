## 6. Logical corrections to carry into the master

- A duplicate input with contradictory labels obstructs zero empirical risk and a universal strict-activity demand, not existence of a stationary or positive-loss population flow.
- Positive finite-time initialization kernel definiteness is not a global lower bound along training.
- Vanishing Taylor radius excludes an analytic finite-dimensional ODE with analytic observations reproducing those actual jets, not every smooth ODE, PDE or operator flow. A formal jet sequence requires its own trajectory-identification bridge before it says anything about actual regularity.

These distinctions preserve the broad nonlinear/nonlazy scientific goal while rejecting unsupported reductions and unsupported impossibility claims.
