## Exact obstruction and precise exclusion

For the maintained bias-free tanh model, the input-to-hidden maps are
compositions of linear maps and odd functions. Hence, at every parameter state,
`h1(-u)=-h1(u)`, `h2(-u)=-h2(u)` and `f(-u)=-f(u)`. In the maintained closure,
`b1` and `b2` are fixed feature tables in linear contractions, not additive
biases. The same reasoning applies to its forward map at every retained order.

For an equally weighted antipodal pair with a common label `y` in `{−1,+1}`,
writing `a=f(u)` gives

```text
[(a-y)^2+(-a-y)^2]/2 = a^2+1.
```

An equally weighted 16-point law composed of such pairs therefore has
`L=1+(1/16) sum_i f(u_i)^2 >= 1`. The objective is exactly the corresponding
zero-label objective plus one. At closure initialization `c=0`, the fields
`d,q` vanish, so the `w,M` velocities vanish; the `c` velocity cancels pairwise
because `h2` is odd. This is exact stationarity for the exactly paired law
and zero readout. The finite Gaussian readout is not exactly zero, so its
initialization need not be stationary. Floating-point representations of
antipodes and cancellation are separate numerical questions.

This excludes the initially proposed symmetric same-label antipodal XOR
layout as a fitting example for this architecture. It does **not** exclude the
completed experiment: its centers are `0°,90°,150°,240°`, with labels
`+1,−1,+1,−1` and the declared offsets. Nor does the exact finite paired-loss
formula automatically hold for independently jittered, unpaired finite data.
For a symmetric population input law with an even binary target, odd prediction
still cancels the target cross term, giving the analogous population loss
floor. None of these statements is a general obstruction to observable closure.

The duplication check used the complete short subsection “Genuine endpoint
obstructions and the extra odd symmetry” in
[`docs/special_data_limits.md`](../../docs/special_data_limits.md), lines
17619–17626 at the inspected version. It already gives `f(-x)=-f(x)`, the
antipodal equal-label obstruction and stationarity at zero population readout.
The C.3 discussion in
[`docs/global_nonlinear.md`](../../docs/global_nonlinear.md), lines 3804–3815,
also lists the antiparallel odd-activation equal-label frozen case. Thus the
XOR identity is a concise specialization of existing architecture restrictions,
not a missing closure foundation.
