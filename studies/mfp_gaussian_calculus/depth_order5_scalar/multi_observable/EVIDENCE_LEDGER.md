# Amortized multi-observable evidence ledger

## MO-1: universal observable calculus

- **Statement:** the parameter-flow jets \(p,Dp,D^2p,D^3p\) are universal,
  and the first four derivatives of any scalar observable are obtained by
  contracting its derivative tensors against those jets.
- **Level:** exact finite-width identity.
- **Status:** passed by two independent derivations and an exact symbolic
  composition audit.

## MO-2: hidden-Gram dictionary and parity

- **Statement:** \(\Gamma_{11}=w\), \(\Gamma_{02}=q02\),
  \(\Gamma_{22}=q22\), \(\Gamma_{13}=q13\), and odd annealed hidden-feature
  derivatives vanish under readout reflection.
- **Level:** exact semantic identification plus large-width coordinate limit.
- **Status:** zero exact discrepancies at every layer for \(H=2,3,4\);
  seedwise readout-reflection jet law passed.

## MO-3: moment-only \(\Gamma_{04}\) head

- **Statement:** after `R3`, \(\Gamma_{04}\) is emitted by one forward sweep
  with the two dynamic coordinates \((\gamma04,a41)\).
- **Level:** algebraically audited Gaussian normal form.
- **Status:** two independently frozen producers agree on all raw
  `83/20/1` terms and all reduced `64/17` terms.  The reduction
  \(1+a43_\ell=\tau_\ell\) is exact.  No minimality claim.

## MO-4: equality partitions, width, and transpose responses

- **Statement:** all order-four Bell partitions, all four transpose-response
  grades, and every local equality class are included before negative-width
  sectors are removed.
- **Level:** algebraic audit.
- **Status:** passed by the frozen branch ledger and independent
  canonicalizers.  The first hostile canonicalizer was falsified and
  preserved: it aliased a fifth forward innovation into a four-slot engine.
  The corrected five-slot hostile route reproduces `83/20/1`.

## MO-5: exact and empirical controls

- **Statement:** the head passes constant, linear, nontrivial unit-affine,
  and smooth nonpolynomial tests.
- **Level:** exact controls plus empirical regression.
- **Status:** exact controls passed through \(H=4\).  Two separately
  preregistered normalized-sine \(H=2\) panels passed.  The stricter hostile
  \(H=3\) panel is **inconclusive**: all four z-scores pass, but three widths
  cannot identify its preregistered quadratic-curvature diagnostic.  A
  separately frozen fourth-width extension adds \(n=512\), reproduces the old
  samples exactly, and passes the unchanged validity and curvature gates.
  The follow-up discharges the promotion gate while the original outcome is
  retained.

## MO-6: annealed theorem

- **Statement:** at every separately fixed \(H\), the deterministic head is
  the annealed width limit.
- **Level:** theorem.
- **Status:** the probability bridge is proved under the all-orders
  polynomially-smooth tensor-program hypothesis.  With only finite-order
  differentiability, a separate convergence and uniform-integrability bridge
  is required.  The scoped head is promoted because MO-5 now passes via its
  separately frozen extension.

## MO-7: amortized architecture

- **Statement:** one universal backbone can serve output/kernel/loss and
  hidden-activation RMS heads; the latter costs one additional forward sweep
  and emits all layers in \(O(H)\) time.
- **Level:** exact construction for the named heads.
- **Status:** passed for the output head and the promoted hidden-activation
  RMS head.  The analogous
  preactivation-RMS contraction and a grammar-wide small-head theorem remain
  open.

## MO-8: order-seven extension

- **Statement:** the order-five graph may embed grade-triangularly in an
  order-seven graph with further grade sweeps and derivative ceiling seven.
- **Level:** roadmap only.
- **Status:** open.  Abstract enumeration found 23 free-tree shapes, but the
  rank-labelled identity, peeling, state closure, sweep count, derivative
  ceiling, and factored complexity have not all been constructed and audited.
