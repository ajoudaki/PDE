# Independent Route A evidence ledger

## Research contract

- Object: one-sample shared-activation fully connected network under
  feature ascent `theta_dot=n grad f`.
- Limit order: each hidden depth H is fixed first, then width n tends to
  infinity, then annealed expectation is taken under an explicit UI bridge.
- New observable: hidden activation squared RMS and RMS through fourth
  feature-ascent order, followed by the exact label-1 MSE time change through
  fifth time order.
- Permitted final grammar: deterministic scalar recurrence and
  one-dimensional M_nu atoms only.
- Forbidden substitutions: frozen Gamma04 for moving Gamma04, polynomial or
  Hermite activation approximation, hidden response matrices, or oracle
  trajectory playback.
- Complexity target: a fixed-dimensional head attached to the existing
  arbitrary-fixed-H backbone, with factored work O(H).
