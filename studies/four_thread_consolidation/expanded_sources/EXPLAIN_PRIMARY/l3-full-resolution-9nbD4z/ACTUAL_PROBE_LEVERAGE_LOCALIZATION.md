# Actual Gaussian-probe covariance: a small-support localization

Status: a proved finite-width bound for part of the actual causal
response. The exceptional contribution and the global response-moment
estimate remain open. This note does not use a backward-field tail
bound, or replace the actual bulk by an independent pruned bulk.

## 1. Normalizations and exact adaptive support

Use the actual uncut feature-time trajectory and its single-column
probe tangent from ACTUAL_BULK_GAUSSIAN_TANGENT_ENERGY.md on [0,S].
All constants depend only on the established primal/operator bounds.
For vectors and matrices, respectively, put

    ||v||_n^2 = (1/n) sum_j v_j^2,
    <v,w>_n = (1/n) sum_j v_j w_j,
    ||A||_HS^2 = sum_(j,k) A_jk^2.

Thus HS is the ordinary Frobenius norm. The tangent state is
y=(x,A,B,c), with the sum of the four squared component norms.
The independent auxiliary probe is xi~N(0,I_n), and its initial
top-matrix direction is K=xi e_i^T/sqrt(n), for a fixed index i.
The expectation E_xi below is conditional on the ENTIRE actual
initialization and trajectory. In particular, it does not average
the original Gaussian matrices.

The exact tangent equation and bounded maps are

    y' = L_s y + R_s M_(b_s) T_s y + f_s(K),   y(0)=0,
    b_s = phi''(z2(s)) q2(s),
    T_s y = A H1 + W2 D1^2 x =: zeta,
    R_s* y = W2 x + A H1 =: gamma.

Here ||L_s||, ||R_s||, ||T_s||<=C, ||b_s||_n<=C, and
n E_xi||f_s(K)||^2<=C. The existing scaled energy and its mixed
coordinate covariance are

    Lambda = n E_xi||y||^2,
    Gamma_j = n E_xi[gamma_j zeta_j].

Introduce the two nonnegative coordinate variances

    u_j = n E_xi gamma_j^2,
    v_j = n E_xi zeta_j^2,
    ell_j = u_j+v_j.

The bounded maps give, with one constant C_0,

    (1/n) sum_j ell_j <= C_0 Lambda.                    (1)

For a deterministic threshold R>=1 define, at EACH time s,

    E_R(s) = {j : ell_j(s)>R Lambda(s)},   if Lambda(s)>0,
    E_R(s) = empty set,                   if Lambda(s)=0,
    B_R(s) = E_R(s)^c.

This is the exact adaptive support; it is selected using the actual
probe covariance, not using large coordinates of q2. It depends on
the original initialization but not on the realized auxiliary probe
xi. By (1),

    |E_R(s)|/n <= min{1,C_0/R}.                         (2)

When Lambda=0, y=0 in L2 of the probe, so u=v=Gamma=0 and all
statements below hold with this convention. Finite-width continuity
of the covariance makes these time-dependent sets measurable; their
membership is not asserted to be time-differentiable.

## 2. Controlled signed covariance and forcing on the complement

The following two bounds hold for the actual tangent at every time:

    |<b_s, 1_(B_R(s)) Gamma(s)>_n|
        <= C sqrt(R) Lambda(s),                         (3)

    n E_xi ||1_(B_R(s)) b_s zeta(s)||_n^2
        <= C R Lambda(s).                               (4)

To prove them, conditional Cauchy--Schwarz gives

    |Gamma_j| <= sqrt(u_j v_j) <= ell_j/2.

On B_R, ell_j<=R Lambda. Consequently

    ||1_B Gamma||_n^2
      <= (1/(4n)) sum_(j in B) ell_j^2
      <= (R Lambda/(4n)) sum_j ell_j
      <= (C_0/4) R Lambda^2.

Pairing this with ||b_s||_n<=C proves (3). Also

    n E_xi ||1_B b_s zeta||_n^2
      = (1/n) sum_(j in B) b_(s,j)^2 v_j
      <= R Lambda ||b_s||_n^2,

which proves (4). Boundedness of R_s also yields
n E_xi||R_s(1_B b_s zeta)||^2<=C R Lambda.

These calculations need no independence between b_s and any actual
response coefficient. They also hold with b_s replaced by
phi''(z2(s)) W3_0* d(s), since that coefficient has the same existing
L2 bound. Thus they can be applied after removing the already harmless
trained top-history contribution.

## 3. The time-integrated controlled part

Let U_L(s,t) be the bounded propagator generated by L. Variation of
constants gives the exact decomposition y=y_0+y_B+y_E, where

    y_0(s) = integral_0^s U_L(s,t) f_t(K) dt,
    y_B(s) = integral_0^s U_L(s,t) R_t
                         [1_(B_R(t)) b_t zeta(t)] dt,
    y_E(s) = integral_0^s U_L(s,t) R_t
                         [1_(E_R(t)) b_t zeta(t)] dt.    (5)

Here zeta is the actual FULL response, not the response of an
independently evolved restricted system. Minkowski, (4), and the
bounded propagator prove

    sqrt(n E_xi||y_0(s)||^2) <= C s,
    sqrt(n E_xi||y_B(s)||^2)
       <= C sqrt(R) integral_0^s sqrt(Lambda(t)) dt.      (6)

No derivative of a moving projection has been used. Equation (6)
is a controlled time-integrated response contribution. It does not
estimate y_E, and is not a closed inequality for Lambda.

Likewise, the exact energy estimate in the earlier note now becomes

    Lambda'(s) <= C(1+sqrt(R))(1+Lambda(s))
       + 2 <b_s,1_(E_R(s)) Gamma(s)>_n.                 (7)

One can replace the first term by C+C(1+sqrt(R))Lambda;
the displayed weaker version emphasizes the sole exceptional term.
The estimate is pathwise in the actual initialization. Applying
original-Gaussian integration by parts to the hard adaptive indicator
would require extra justification and boundary terms; none is used
or discarded here.

## 4. Exact remaining scope of rare-block cancellation

The new bounds control the complement of a set of mass at most C_0/R.
They do NOT give a vanishing bound for either

    <b_s,1_(E_R(s)) Gamma(s)>_n,
    sqrt(n E_xi||y_E(s)||^2).                            (8)

The L1 bound ||Gamma||_(1,n)<=C Lambda permits the entire covariance
mass to lie on E_R. Its small counting measure alone does not improve
that bound. No statement of a finite-time concentration or
delocalization counterexample for the canonical flow is made.

The all-subset events in ACTUAL_TWO_TIME_RARE_RETURN.md apply to this
adaptive support without a new independence assumption. For different
times, they may be applied to E_R(s) union E_R(t), whose mass is at
most 2C_0/R; the full/pruned distances must then be those for that
entire union. They still leave those distances and multiplication by
b_t unbounded by the available estimates.

The single-pruned estimate in SINGLE_PRUNED_OFFBLOCK_OSGOOD.md is also
available on E_R(s). Its residual bound includes the unclosed actual
full/pruned distance d_(E_R(s)). Moreover, a bound on that PRIMAL
residual does not bound its initial-data derivatives or its signed
contraction with the probe covariance. Such a derivative estimate
cannot be inferred by differentiating a pointwise norm inequality.

Diagonal resummation produces NO newly controlled exceptional term
in this note. The known scalar own-site cancellation and natural-
coordinate commutator identity do not by themselves estimate (8):
the actual rare-to-bulk forcing and its causal variations remain.
The present advance is precisely (3)--(4) and their integrated form
(6), not a second proof of that earlier cancellation.

The narrower next target is therefore the actual SIGNED exceptional
pairing in (8), or its causal accumulated response y_E, on the
explicit probe-variance support above. Estimating it requires more
than the existing small-support primal geometry. No global
continuation or Osgood moment estimate follows yet.
