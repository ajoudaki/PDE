## 1. How to read the results

**L counts hidden layers**, m counts samples, and n is hidden width. “One sample” is not “width one.” Except where stated otherwise, datasets and depth are fixed before n tends to infinity.

- **Global joint:** an autonomous population GF and its stated finite-algorithm limits on every fixed finite physical interval [0,T]. The activation is chosen independently of T. This is not a width limit uniform over [0,infinity).
- **Semi-global joint:** an absolute positive interval [0,T_*], chosen before width and mesh limits, with the stated population/algorithm/observable conclusions. T_* may depend on the theorem's fixed depth, activation, initialization, label and input bounds. An interval uniform over geometry is explicitly identified when proved.
- **Population + joint GD limit:** some earlier manuscripts explicitly prove simultaneous n→infinity, eta_n→0 exact-GD convergence to population GF, but do not separately state a finite-width-GF convergence theorem. Such rows retain that exact distinction; they are not silently enlarged to the full finite-GF/GD bundle.
- **Finite optimization:** actual finite networks exist/train/fit, possibly with bounds uniform in width. This alone does not identify a global population limit.
- **Initialization / conditional / representation obstruction:** these are separate conclusions and do not establish a trained global limit.

For normalized inputs, write u_a=x_a/sqrt(d), ||u_a||=1 and Gamma_ab=<u_a,u_b>. **One-sided separation** is Gamma_ab<=1-delta; it permits antipodes. **Two-sided separation** is |Gamma_ab|<=1-delta. The user's strict interval is included in the latter. Three pairwise-separated samples may have a singular input Gram matrix; the relevant results do not quietly assume full rank.

The initialization and optimizer are part of every theorem. In the common small-readout model the first preactivations have order-one Gaussian variance, middle entries have variance 1/n, prediction is n^-1 C^T h^L, and the **stored readout** has coordinate variance n^-2. Its population limit C0=0 does not replace the actual finite Gaussian initialization. Many modern results use exact raw step n^-2. Earlier order-one readout and deep-linear models are stated separately below. A half-sum, sum and average of squared losses differ by a physical-time factor; their numerical constants cannot be copied without that conversion.

The strongest nonlinear joint theorems include predictions/loss, all true kernel blocks, both current action orientations and actual adjoints, same-layer joint sample path laws in W2 for the uniform path norm, and the stated hidden velocity/energy measurements. Generic probe classes have bounded-product/Lipschitz restrictions; they do not include arbitrary products of unbounded fields. Precise classes differ between manuscripts. Cross-width operator-norm convergence, cross-layer pairing of individual neurons, growing m or d, and L=L(n) are not asserted.

## 2. The broad semi-global theorem that was missing

**SG — Every fixed L>=2 and every fixed finite m,d; arbitrary fixed data; a broad layer-dependent activation class.**

For each hidden layer, phi is C1, its derivative is bounded and globally Lipschitz. The activation itself may grow linearly. This includes ordinary arctangent, tanh, sigmoid, softplus, exact GELU, SiLU, sine/cosine combinations, smooth bounded plateau activations, and the fixed odd convex mixtures discussed later. ReLU's discontinuous derivative is excluded. Different layers may use different activations. Nonaffinity is unnecessary for existence.

Inputs need only ||x_a||/sqrt(d)<=X; they can coincide or have a singular Gram matrix. Labels may be arbitrary real numbers with |y_a|<=Y. Use weighted average squared loss sum_a omega_a(f_a-y_a)^2 with omega_a>0 and sum omega_a=1. The first weights may be iid centered subGaussian; independent middle matrices are Gaussian with variance sigma_l^2/n. The stored readout may have **iid entries from any fixed subGaussian law, including a noncentered or order-one Gaussian law**. Zero and RMS-vanishing perturbations, including every Gaussian scale n^-beta with beta>0, are covered. The stated rates are eta_n(n kappa_1,kappa_2,...,kappa_L,n kappa_(L+1)); nonnegative fixed rates are allowed for existence. Here z1=W1 x/sqrt(d), with principal first-weight variance sigma1^2. Equivalently, for z1=V1 x and V1=W1/sqrt(d), the first Euclidean gradient rate is eta_n n kappa_1/d.

There is T_*>0 depending only on depth and the displayed regularity, initialization, rate, input and label bounds, **independent of m,d and the input angles under those bounds**. For every deterministic eta_n>0 tending to zero, with no condition coupling eta_n to width, the unique strong autonomous population GF exists on [0,T_*], and exact GD converges jointly to it with predictions, all kernels, same-layer hidden path laws in W2, integrated squared speeds, and its prescribed action probes. The dataset is nevertheless fixed in this convergence statement. For summed rather than averaged loss, the guaranteed physical interval is divided by m.

The same result permits separable losses sum_a omega_a ell_a(f_a), with ell_a C1 and derivatives locally bounded and locally Lipschitz with common bounds on bounded prediction intervals. Convexity is not required on this fixed short interval.

**Finite-GF wording:** the numbered theorem and its full proof explicitly certify exact-GD→population-GF for every vanishing mesh. They do not separately formulate a finite-width-GF convergence clause. The proof's one-sided stability estimate strongly suggests that corollary, but this consolidation does not relabel it as a separately reviewed claim. This distinction does not diminish its actual simultaneous width/vanishing-step theorem.

**Strict feature activity is a separate L=2 corollary.** It assumes Gaussian first weights, zero limiting readout, positive scales/rates, normalized pairwise nonparallel inputs, every label nonzero, and both activations nonaffine. It proves every input's hidden preactivation and activation RMS displacements of positive order t^2, speeds of order t, positive changing kernel blocks, decreasing loss, and nonaffinity on an initial interval. That interval may depend on geometry/labels. No corresponding all-depth strict-activity statement is certified by this theorem.

Authority: [complete general theorem](expanded_sources/GENERALIZE_PRIMARY/GENERAL_POPULATION_GF_PROOF.md), [complete response proof](expanded_sources/GENERALIZE_PRIMARY/GENERAL_DEPTH_RESPONSE_PROOF.md), and [scope, source and three fresh full-review PASS records](expanded_sources/GENERALIZE_AUDIT.md). Consequently, ordinary smooth activations already have a broad semi-global result, including three samples. The earlier “only a local population construction is known for the three-input odd mixture” classification was incomplete. The difficult remaining question is **global continuation with the desired activation and the full requested measurement contract**, not the absence of any positive-time joint theorem.

## 5. Global finite-network training is another substantive positive result

**F1 — Pure atan, L=3, one sample x=y=1, canonical small Gaussian readout.** The full local population theorem is proved; the global population theorem remains open. Independently, actual finite GF **and exact raw GD with eta=n^-2** have stronger global optimization theorems. With probability tending to one, uniformly over all physical times / all GD iterations, the top feature kernel stays at least m3/16>0, the predictor increases to 1, residual and loss decay exponentially, and every finite parameter converges to an interpolating endpoint. Here m1=E atan(G)^2, m2=E atan(sqrt(m1)G)^2, m3=E atan(sqrt(m2)G)^2.

The deterministic hypotheses need small initial readout **RMS**, bounded hidden operator norms, and a positive initial top feature norm. A maximum-readout-coordinate condition is unnecessary. Under exactly zero readout the GF kernel floor improves to the initial top feature second moment. Finite GF has residual bound (1-f0)exp(-m3 t/8); exact GD has (1-f0)exp(-m3 k eta/16) on the stated high-probability event. These different rates should not be equated.

This does not establish convergence of the fitted endpoints with width, global GF/GD closeness, a global strong population limit, or interchange of infinite-time and width limits. Both finite proofs received two full-file PASS reviews, with the freshness qualifications recorded in the audit. [GF proof](expanded_sources/EXPLAIN_PRIMARY/l3-full-resolution-9nbD4z/READOUT_COERCIVITY.md), [exact-GD proof](expanded_sources/EXPLAIN_PRIMARY/l3-full-resolution-9nbD4z/EXACT_GD_COERCIVITY.md), [audit](expanded_sources/EXPLAIN_AUDIT.md).

**F2 — L=2, two opposite-label inputs at any fixed interior angle, mixed saturated/linear-tail activations.** Let -1<rho<1, y=(1,-1), phi1(z)=integral_0^z p(u)du, where p is even, nonnegative, smooth, supported on [-R,R] and strictly positive inside, and phi2(z)=z+epsilon atan(z) for fixed epsilon>0. Use independent Gaussian first/middle weights, stored readout variance n^-2, summed squared loss and the stated fully trained raw mobilities (n/d,1,n).

Actual finite GF exists globally for every finite initial state. On an explicit Gaussian initialization event of probability 1-O(1/n)-O(exp(-c n)), it achieves a strict loss margin, maintains a positive first-feature Gram, then has exponential loss decay to zero, finite accumulated residual and weighted residual clocks, width/time-uniform middle-operator and stored-readout RMS norms (and centered middle Frobenius displacement), and finite-parameter interpolating endpoints. Constants depend on the fixed angle/activation; they are not uniform near rho=±1. The full proof passed two isolated complete reviews.

A **separately reviewed strengthening** adds a strip-count event and proves that, for each sample, a positive fraction of fixed first-layer rows remains uniformly unsaturated for every t>=0, with a time-uniform positive average squared gate derivative. This proves permanent gate mass, not permanently nonzero backward forces or velocities. The original optimization event alone does not imply it: all first gates can be zero while upper layers successfully train.

This branch does not establish a global population limit. Its complete 1,008-line exact-GD extension is recovered, but an independent acceptance record has not been recovered; it is therefore excluded from the certified table rather than incorrectly described as a proved full joint result or merely a conditional stub. ## 6. Deep linear networks: both a global positive theorem and precise no-go theorems

This is a **different initialization/scaling**, not a substitution into the nonlinear tables. There are three hidden layers and one sample, with

f_n=a^T R B x, a,x in R^n, B,R in R^(n by n),

all initial entries independent N(0,1/n). The activation is the identity and the real label y is arbitrary. Loss is (y-f_n)^2 and physical mobility eta>0. Equivalently the four standard-Gaussian raw factors are divided by sqrt(n), raw output has prefactor n^-2, and raw mobility is n eta.

**DL+ — A global autonomous operator population limit exists.** On a fixed separable Hilbert space with a fixed bounded Gaussian/noncommutative source C0, the current state is a trace-class kernel Q and residual e:

    dot Q = 2 eta e (C0* + Q*)^3,
    K = Tr[(C0+Q)^3(C0*+Q*)^3],
    dot e = -2 eta e K,
    Q(0)=0, e(0)=y, f=Tr(C0+Q)^4/4=y-e.

The flow is unique, global, autonomous and restartable in its stated class; ||Q(T)||_1<=2|y|sqrt(eta T). Actual finite GF and simultaneous exact GD converge in probability uniformly on every fixed finite interval for f,K,loss and every fixed finite family of the specified rooted-word/finite-rank observables. The population predictor also tends to y and loss tends to zero when y is nonzero; the selected y=0 population path is stationary. **Every normalized physical mesh delta_n→0 is allowed.** This is the named operator-observable theorem, not an assertion of the entire nonlinear W2/velocity measurement bundle. Three isolated complete final reviews accepted the frozen theorem.

**DL−ODE — No state-universal finite scalar closure by uniformly bounded-degree tensor contractions.** There are no fixed integers k,D, independent of width, such that a k-coordinate state containing f, each coordinate a finite linear combination of same-time complete tensor contractions of degree <=D, obeys one exact polynomial ODE for feature ascent on all parameter states at all sufficiently large widths. The analytic-germ version also fails on a full neighborhood of the zero state. Repeated derivatives generate connected paths of unbounded degree; products of finitely many fixed contraction types cannot supply those new connected types.

**DL−PDE — Passing to finitely many local fields does not fix this within the same contraction class.** Fix the field count, spatial dimension, finite PDE differential order, finite readout jet order, and a uniform contraction degree D. Encode each field from current parameter contractions of degree <=D, with smooth spatial coefficients (even width-dependent ones). No exact local differential-polynomial PDE with finite-jet polynomial output can represent the dynamics on a full open set of parameter states for all sufficiently large widths. The corresponding analytic-germ statement requires full neighborhoods of the zero-state jets. The theorem covers both feature ascent and **physical squared-loss flow for every real y, including zero**, with eta>0. Three final isolated reviewers returned CLEAN at the exact frozen hash **before** the task was interrupted.

The word “bounded” here concerns the **number/degree of contraction coordinates or encoder**, not an ODE whose norm contracts. Neither no-go excludes a fixed number of infinite-dimensional operator fields, a width-dependent finite system, an ODE fitted to one chosen trajectory, or arbitrary encodings. In particular the formally exact scalar transport representation U_t=U_s, initialized with the entire future output profile, lies outside the bounded-contraction class. It preloads the answer and is not a constructive compression.

DL+ and both no-go results are compatible. The positive model retains an infinite-dimensional current action source. An additional checked negative shows that merely retaining the separate spectral marginals of its two source operators loses needed mixed noncommutative words; those marginals are not a sufficient substitute for the joint source.

**I2 — Initialized normalized eigenvalues improve with depth.** For the initialized three-sample covariance recursion of the odd mixture with 0<theta<=1/2, the smallest eigenvalue of C_L is nondecreasing and the largest is nonincreasing. This is eigenvalue monotonicity, not Loewner monotonicity, and not monotonicity during training. The Gaussian variance bounds and exact fixed-theta asymptotics distinguish normalized conditioning from absolute feature scale. [Proof](../three_sample_near_identity_all_depths/INITIALIZATION.md).

**I3 — Calibrated layers can accumulate nonlinear effects at initialization.** Let chi be the L2(Gaussian)-normalization of exp(3/2)sin(2z)/2-sin(z), so E chi(G)=E[G chi(G)]=0 and E chi(G)^2=1. For fixed tau>0 set

    phi_L(z) = [z+sqrt(tau/L) chi(z)] / sqrt(1+tau/L).

Every initialized layer has unit variance, separated triples have a positive feature-Gram floor uniform in depth, and there is a nontrivial sequential width-first/depth-second correlation limit. An explicit floor is

    [p3 delta^2(2-delta)^2/3] [1-(1+tau/L)^(-L)]
      >= [p3 delta^2(2-delta)^2/3] tau/(1+tau),

where p3>0 is the specified cubic Hermite mass. This is neither a trained-flow nor a simultaneous width/depth theorem. Two isolated whole-note PASSs. [Proof](../calibrated_near_identity_reviews/manuscript.md).

**I4 — A fixed moderate calibrated sine has exact initialized nonlinear variance.**

    Phi(z) = [z+(2/5)(sin(2z)-2 exp(-2)z)] / sqrt(1+(4/25)v),
    v=(1-exp(-8))/2-4 exp(-4).

It is strictly increasing, has initialized variance one at every layer, and its best-affine residual is exactly the variance fraction (4v/25)/(1+4v/25), approximately **6.389055%**. In the two-input model the initial top feature Gram floor is at least delta. This is a useful quantitative witness independent of SG's existence result. [Initialization proof](../two_sample_activation_design/SINE_INITIALIZATION.md).

The same Phi also has a separately reviewed **full population/finite-GF/GD semi-global theorem at L=3,m=2** with the exact coefficient 2/5. Its explicit physical T_* is about 5.05073*10^-50, independent of normalized geometry and binary labels. Put B=20,D=2^10 B^5,A=10^4 D^4 and

    T_* = (1/3) min{1, [100*2^3*B^3]^-1,

The source's L=2 in this constant calculation denotes a slope bound, not hidden depth. ## 8. Further substantive negative results and their exact targets

1. **Oddness and antipodal equal labels are incompatible with fitting.** In a bias-free network with odd activation, f(-x)=-f(x) at every width and parameter state. Equal labels on an antipodal pair force a positive loss floor and a common raw-kernel null direction. Such a pair is admissible under one-sided separation. This motivates the two-sided geometry for odd activations; it does **not** refute existence of GF or a population limit. [Proof](../three_sample_separated_angle_theorem/SHIFT_AND_ENERGY_NORMALIZATION.md:10).

2. **Pairwise separation does not guarantee an input spectral gap.** The equilateral planar triple has cosines -1/2 and a singular Gamma. There are full-rank triples with the same fixed absolute separation and lambda_min(Gamma) tending to zero. Pure affine, zero-readout population flow is stationary on the equilateral triple with all labels +1, at loss 3/2. This invalidates a uniformly successful affine comparator for that label component, not positive-theta nonlinear training. [Geometry](../three_sample_odd_activation_threshold/REPORT.md:68), [stationarity](../odd_activation_lower_powers_three_inputs/THREE_INPUT_ANALYSIS.md:113).

3. **Fitting cannot have theta-independent speed over the entire small-theta family.** For the exact convex mixture on the equilateral all-positive-label triple and each fixed L>=2, any actual strong GF that reaches loss <=3/8 requires T=Omega_L(theta^-1). At L2 the checked sharper bound is T>=(2/3)[sqrt(9+2/(pi theta))-3]^2 and liminf_(theta->0) theta T>=4/(3pi). The successful raw-state excursion also diverges. No fitting or global continuation is assumed in this necessary condition. It rules out theta-uniform fitting rates, not global existence for one fixed theta. [General-depth proof](../three_sample_near_identity_all_depths/NECESSARY_FITTING_SCALE.md:95), [L2 bound](../three_sample_odd_activation_depth2/REPORT.md:96).

4. **Additional three-sample small-direction bounds are exact.** For L3, Gram-null directions satisfy v^T K v<=256 theta^2 ||v||_1^2(11+R)^6 on a raw ball of radius R, for the sum of all four kernel blocks. Clustered separated triples additionally require R>(10 theta sqrt(delta))^-1/4-11 to reach loss <=3/8. These preclude uniform conditioning or fitting inside fixed balls as the parameters shrink. They do not prove loss failure along the true trajectory. [Kernel bound](../three_sample_odd_activation_threshold/routes/NONLINEAR_REFERENCE.md:83), [distance bound](../three_sample_odd_activation_threshold/REPORT.md:93).

5. **Fixed convex mixtures cannot have a positive depth-uniform raw rate or absolute nonaffinity floor.** In the two-sample model with phi_theta=(1-theta)z+theta atan(z), fixed 0<theta<1, initialized variance q_L~1/(2theta L); the initial loss-decay slope tends to zero, and initialized absolute nonaffinity decays as L^-3. Consequently a positive exponential rate with the exact initial-loss prefactor cannot be uniform over all depths. This does not rule out a common activation with depth-dependent rates and qualitative global existence at every fixed L. [Proof](../two_sample_odd_activation_depth4/DEPTH_UNIFORMITY.md:15).

6. **Literal convex mixtures of the offset affine map have a separate exponential conditioning obstruction.** For phi_epsilon=(1-epsilon)(1+z)+epsilon psi(z), fixed 0<epsilon<1/2 and psi in B, there is kappa<1 with initialized pair feature distances and a kernel eigenvalue <=2(1-Gamma_ij)kappa^(2L). Hence a depth-independent positive initial kernel floor is impossible for this family. Two isolated full-report PASS reviews. This is not a counterexample to qualitative global population existence at each finite L. [Proof](../convex_offset_all_depths/REPORT.md).

7. **Exact convexity, Gaussian normalization and relative nonlinearity have restrictions.** The literal nontrivial convex mixture of z and atan(z) has E phi(G)^2<1. Gaussian normalization changes the coefficients so they no longer sum to one. Separately, for phi=az+e psi with psi Lipschitz constant D and a>eD, its best-affine variance fraction is at most [eD/(a-eD)]^2 for every nondegenerate finite-variance input law. A large affine slope can therefore conceal a small relative nonlinear fraction despite an order-one absolute nonlinear coefficient. [Normalization](../two_sample_odd_activation_theorem/INITIAL_MOTION_AND_NORMALIZATION.md:199), [relative bound](../two_sample_activation_design/RELATIVE_NONLINEARITY.md:6).

8. **Primal energy and ordinary operator bounds alone do not supply the missing stability theory.** The reviewed counterexamples show that bounded raw norms, second moments, or exchangeability alone do not control adaptive Gaussian-transpose square tails; combined covariance-weighted L2 responses do not control named coefficients or higher moments; the moderate sine loss is not weakly lower semicontinuous or locally semiconvex on the whole ambient Hilbert space, and its gradient is not locally Lipschitz there. The exact sine's trained conditional Gaussian damping need not retain its initialized exp(-2) factor. A scalar identity-activation controlled ascent can blow up under bounded arbitrary control, unlike the physical energy argument. None of these examples establishes that an actual initialized neural GF reaches the offending states or fails to have a global limit. [Detailed thread-2 inventory](THREAD2_AUDIT.md), [thread-3 inventory](THREAD3_AUDIT.md), [thread-4 inventory](THREAD4_AUDIT.md).


9. **Quadratic activation has a real initialized-continuity obstruction in the older order-one-readout model.** For L=2,m=1, phi(z)=z^2/sqrt(3), independent Gaussian connector and order-one stored Gaussian readout, width-first loss Euler has a nonvanishing initial layer and cannot converge uniformly from t=0 to a continuous initialized scalar trace. The **width limit** of actual finite GF has the same initialized-continuity obstruction, and sufficiently resolved joint GD meshes inherit it. Each finite GF remains global and continuous. This is not a theorem against every activation, initialization, or every under-resolved full-network mesh. The all-vanishing-mesh theorem belongs only to the auxiliary **frozen-bottom** model and has weaker independent-review evidence. The full model's arbitrary-diagonal claim remains open. [Final proof and scope](expanded_sources/OPERATOR_AUDIT.md).

10. **Hard ReLU exposes a distinction between classical GF and Euler limits.** In that earlier order-one-readout L2, one-sample model, any prescribed fixed derivative convention at zero can encounter an attracting gate with no absolutely continuous classical continuation. A finite-width positive-Gaussian-probability event is proved; no uniform macroscopic probability over widths is inferred. Conversely, exact Euler predictor/loss paths are proved tight in C([0,T0]) for **every** deterministic vanishing mesh, T0=1/(384*6^4). Their limits are continuous at the prescribed initialization. A unique deterministic generalized full-state/population limit is still unproved. The classical-gate negative has independent review; the later scalar compactness proof is a complete recovered primary argument without a separate final frozen-hash review certificate located in this pass. Do not call it an independently certified full joint theorem. [Proofs, review and qualification](expanded_sources/OPERATOR_AUDIT.md).

11. **Duplicate inputs with opposite labels prevent universal strict learning.** Every deterministic network gives the duplicates the same output; the common zero initial prediction can already minimize their summed loss. This rules out strict loss decrease/nontrivial learning for every arbitrary dataset, not existence of a stationary population flow. It is compatible with SG, whose existence hypotheses do not promise strict activity. [Audited history](expanded_sources/OPERATOR_AUDIT.md).

12. **A single bounded-multiplication Banach algebra cannot contain the required unbounded Gaussian powers with the prescribed norm properties.** A norm with positive zeroth-order weight and bounded multiplication would bound all powers geometrically, contradicting Gaussian moment growth. This invalidates that proposed all-source norm, including the normalized near-identity analytic setting. It does not exclude radius-losing scales, typed product spaces, or population GF. This is distinct from DL−ODE/PDE. [Final proof](../mfp_uniform_near_identity_l2/FINAL_RESOLUTION.md), [independent audit](../mfp_uniform_near_identity_l2/FINAL_RESOLUTION_AUDIT.md).

13. **Some global L3 proof shortcuts fail even though finite networks train globally.** The accepted one-sample atan investigations include: bounded-primal deterministic states with no width-uniform vector-field continuity; lack of a uniform local-Lipschitz neighborhood even near a natural Gaussian one-step state; a smooth L2 temporal path whose endpoint lacks the required spatial tails; bounded-primal deterministic zero-readout-reachable states violating the proposed signed material-Hessian bound; and, for the actual tiny-readout Gaussian flow at fixed small time, full-state relative entropy of order n^3 against initialized Gaussians. Broadening an independent readout reference still leaves n log n+O(n). These are precise obstructions to those stability/entropy methods. Deterministic witnesses are not counterexamples to the prescribed typical Gaussian global flow. [Exact statements, primary proofs and review qualifications](expanded_sources/EXPLAIN_AUDIT.md).

14. **Formal time expansions and primal bounds do not identify an analytic population flow.** A checked deterministic all-block L3, zero-readout atan family has initial hidden operator norms below 2 but feature-time fifth prediction derivative f^(5)(0)=-[9(pi/4)^7/16]sqrt(n)+O(1). Thus bounded primal norms do not provide width-uniform time analyticity; the family is not a typical independent-Gaussian counterexample. Separately, a full initialization jet, smoothness or a formal Gevrey bound does not by itself identify a Borel reconstruction with the actual solution. An ODE for the output along one already identified monotone trajectory is not a universal scalar state closure. This is a logical boundary on the reconstruction proposals, not a new neural nonexistence theorem. [Source reconciliation](expanded_sources/GENERALIZE_AUDIT.md), [historical counterexample scope](expanded_sources/EXPLAIN_AUDIT.md).

15. **High-probability finite fitting is not fixed-width almost-sure fitting.** In F2's actual Gaussian model there is a positive-probability event at every fixed width on which all first rows are same-sign saturated on both inputs. Their features then coincide forever, the two predictions are equal and summed loss stays at least 2. The event has exponentially small probability with width and is compatible with F2's high-probability theorem. A positive first Gram alone also does not replace its achieved strict loss margin: zero upper weights/readout give a stationary counterexample. [Exact witnesses and independent reviews](expanded_sources/L2_MIXED_FINITE_GF_RECOVERY_AUDIT.md).

16. **Ordinary raw-Hilbert local Lipschitzness fails at actual positive-time population states.** For L=3, two normalized inputs with -1<=rho<1, any binary labels, small-readout Gaussian initialization and phi(z)=1+atan(sinh z)/10, the checked local canonical flow has the following property at every sufficiently small fixed positive time: its actual middle reverse query has Gaussian-order lower tails of both signs even when the corresponding preactivation is restricted to any ordinary interval of positive length. The full loss has scalar second directional derivatives of both signs unbounded over individually bounded, rank-one raw-unit Hilbert-Schmidt directions. Thus its raw-Hilbert gradient is not locally Lipschitz at those actually reached states. This goes beyond arbitrary-state counterexamples. It does **not** refute the already constructed local flow, uniqueness, restart or global continuation; no fixed-positive-time finite-width Hessian limit is claimed. The final modular review discharges the imported local premises; a separate isolated review checks the new theorem conditional on those precise premises. [Complete source, imports and exact-hash review audit](expanded_sources/L3_POSITIVE_TIME_CURVATURE_RECOVERY_AUDIT.md).
