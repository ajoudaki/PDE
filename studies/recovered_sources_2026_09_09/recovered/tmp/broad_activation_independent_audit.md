## Checked mathematical points

1. **Scalar symmetry without odd activation.** The raw transformation
   `(w,A,B,C) -> (Q_x w,A,B,tau C)` sends predictions to `tau P f`,
   preserves the objective because `tau P y=y`, preserves the raw
   metric and Gaussian initialization law, and preserves the capped
   feature equations because the clip is odd in its backward argument.
   Forward slots transform by P, backward slots by tau P. Deterministic
   population contractions therefore have `f_i=y_i g`; no finite-width
   sample equality or prior uncut uniqueness is assumed.

2. **Typed source blocks.** With `Q_f=QY`, `Q_b=Q`, both transformed
   involutions are `tau diag(1,-1)`. Hence intertwiners
   `P A=A tau P` and `tau P B=B P` become sector-diagonal blocks under
   the two different bases. The learned Gram matrices commute with P;
   multiplying on the right by diag(y/2) gives exactly the required
   intertwiners. This also covers current derivative returns and
   nonzero feature means because the covariances are full second
   moments. Random coordinate gates do not become sector diagonal.

3. **Uniform affine variance interval.** The three polynomial upper
   expansions sum respectively to 6, 636, and 66780, as stated.
   The lower estimate `||Ap||^2 >= max{R(1+R),1/(100+R)} >=1/101`
   is valid by splitting at R=1. The radial inequality gives
   `||BAp||>=1`. Consequently every baseline marginal has standard
   deviation in `[1/sqrt(404),260]`, uniformly in delta. The argument
   concerns actual marginal variances, not large operator envelopes.

4. **Regression margin.** Bounded continuous nonconstant shapes are
   automatically nonaffine. For linearly growing shapes, the addendum
   correctly replaces nonconstancy by global nonaffinity; without that
   replacement `psi(z)=z` would defeat the conclusion. Full Gaussian
   support establishes positive residual at every variance, and the
   common compact variance interval yields a positive minimum.
   The regression optimizer has slope of absolute value at most one
   for a one-Lipschitz shape, giving the claimed two-Lipschitz bound
   for the square root of the optimal residual under L2 coupling.

5. **Linear-growth same-state forcing.** The forward constants
   `3/2,17/8,93/32`, forward difference constants `2,9/2,61/8`,
