## 6. Uncut construction, physical uniqueness, and reached restart

\[
 \|V_{R'}(\Theta_A)-V_R(\Theta_B)\|
 \le C_B(1+\epsilon R)\|\Theta_A-\Theta_B\|

                \|Q_B\mathbf1_{\{|Q_B|>R\}}\|_2.

\[

Identification of the limiting uncut field can itself use the asymmetric inequality with the limiting state in slot \(A\); it does not need an uncut local Lipschitz theorem.
