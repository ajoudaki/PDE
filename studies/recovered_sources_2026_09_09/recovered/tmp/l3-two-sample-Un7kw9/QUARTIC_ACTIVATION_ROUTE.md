# Exploratory activation surgery: flatter curvature near zero

Status: new main proposal; no full-network theorem. The angle-specific
arctangent proof under review is frozen and unchanged.

Consider phi(z)=1+z+e A(z), where

    A(z)=integral_0^z du/(1+u^4),  e>0 fixed.

This retains a bounded, odd, smooth, strictly nonlinear perturbation of
the affine comparator, a positive bounded gate, and bounded derivatives.
It therefore preserves the structural hypotheses of the near-affine
response strategy; applying that proof to this activation would still
require checking constants and the nonaffinity clauses explicitly.
No e depending on width or time is permitted.

The proposed advantage is precise. For arctangent, phi''(M) vanishes
linearly at zero. A large signed pure-contrast driver can give the common
initial-state response exp[e V0^2/(1+e)], and arbitrary late common
injections can see the inverse of a large preceding contraction. For
the quartic gate, phi''(M) vanishes cubically at zero. The corresponding
local invariant has an inverse-square singularity instead of a logarithm,
suggesting only polynomial response growth in the Gaussian initial data.

Main derivation for the pure local driver M_r=V phi''(M), V_r=phi'(M):

    a(M)=1+e/(1+M^4), b(M)=-4e M^3/(1+M^4)^2,
    F(M)=(1+M^4)(1+M^4+e),
    Lambda(M)=-(1+e)/(4e M^2)+(2+e)M^2/(4e)+M^6/(12e),
    V^2+Lambda(M)=V0^2+Lambda(M0)    (M0!=0).

At fixed Y=V^2, m_Y=-2e m^3/F(m). The radius satisfies
m_c^2<=M0^2+e V0^2, with m_c the common coordinate at V=0.
Integrating Lambda' gives

    (m_c/M0)^2 <=1+4e V0^2 m_c^2/(1+e).

The scalar orbit derivative J=f(m)/f(M0) should therefore have a
polynomial bound in 1+|M0|+sqrt(e)|V0| in BOTH directions of Y.
The envelope m(v)=O(1/|v|) is insufficient to integrate |m|, but the
clock derivative contains b(m)=O(m^3), whose integral does converge.
These observations are being checked in QUARTIC_FLAT_GATE_RESPONSE.md
by a fresh proof worker. None is promoted before that proof is read
and independently audited.

The actual network still contains the common incoming adjoint p,
generated by upper-layer curvature and even-sector operator updates.
Even a polynomial pure-driver theorem is not a bound for that coupled
response. The invariant defect would be Lambda'(M)phi'(M)p, singular
at M=0. A new proof must control the actual p channel or use a regular
coordinate formulation which does not divide by M. The route is not
an assertion that a flat gate solves the universal-angle theorem.
