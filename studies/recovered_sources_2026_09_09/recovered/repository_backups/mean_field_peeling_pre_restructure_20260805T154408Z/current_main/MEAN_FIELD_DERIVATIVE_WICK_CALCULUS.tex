\section*{Current high-level notes}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The goal is to consolidate the project's elaborate technique for
  computing expectations in mean-field neural-network limits into one
  comprehensive document.

  \item The intended starting point is a canonical multilayer perceptron,
  with canonical notation and a standard $\mu$P parameterization in the
  mean-field feature-learning regime.

  \item The technique to be consolidated involves higher-order derivatives,
  network depth, activation functions and their derivatives, and Gaussian or
  Wick expectation calculations.

  \item No concrete model convention, formula, theorem, derivation, or claim
  has yet been adopted for this document.

  \item Further content will be added only when explicitly requested.
\end{itemize}
