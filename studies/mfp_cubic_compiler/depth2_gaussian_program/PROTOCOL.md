# Depth-2 cubic Gaussian-program jet: frozen protocol

## Frozen object

Fix one input, two hidden layers of equal width \(n\), the raw activation
\(\phi(x)=x^3\) at both hidden layers, and independent standard-Gaussian
initialization.  With the input Gram normalized to one, write

\[
X_j=U_j^3,
\qquad
Z_i=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_jW_{ij}X_j,
\qquad
f_n=\frac1n\sum_i A_i Z_i^3.
\]

All three parameter blocks \((A,W,U)\) train with unit Euclidean metric,

\[
D_n=n\nabla f_n\mathbin\cdot\nabla,
\qquad
F^{(r)}(0)=\lim_{n\to\infty}D_n^r f_n.
\]

The limit is width first at each fixed derivative order.  This is the raw
cubic model: no variance normalization, unit-Gram quotient, or NTK-only
freezing is imposed.  Its initial hidden Gram is
\(E[U^6]=15\), hence \(Z_0\sim N(0,15)\).

## Exact finite-width flow and fixed-matrix reduction

Put

\[
B=A\odot Z^2,
\qquad
R=n^{-1/2}W^\top B.
\]

The feature-ascent flow generated by \(D_n\) is

\[
\dot A=Z^3,
\qquad
\dot W=\frac3{\sqrt n}BX^\top,
\qquad
\dot U=9U^2\odot R.
\]

Integrating the only matrix gives the exact identities

\[
Z(t)=n^{-1/2}W_0X(t)
 +3\int_0^t B(s)\langle X(s),X(t)\rangle_n\,ds,
\]

\[
R(t)=n^{-1/2}W_0^\top B(t)
 +3\int_0^t X(s)\langle B(s),B(t)\rangle_n\,ds.
\]

The width limit is represented by two exact scalar Gaussian polynomial laws:

1. a column law containing the base \(U_0\) and fresh transpose innovations
   \(\xi_0,\xi_1,\ldots\);
2. a row law containing the base \(A_0\) and fresh forward innovations
   \(\eta_0,\eta_1,\ldots\).

Their covariances and chronological Stein responses are

\[
E[\eta_k\eta_j]=E[X_kX_j],
\qquad
\widehat Z_k=\eta_k+
 \sum_{j<k}E[\partial_{\xi_j}X_k]B_j,
\]

\[
E[\xi_k\xi_j]=E[B_kB_j],
\qquad
\widehat R_k=\xi_k+
 \sum_{j\le k}E[\partial_{\eta_j}B_k]X_j.
\]

For ordinary Taylor coefficients, the frozen chronological recurrence is

\[
U_k=\frac9k\sum_{p+q+r=k-1}U_pU_qR_r,
\qquad
A_k=\frac1k\sum_{p+q+r=k-1}Z_pZ_qZ_r
\quad(k\ge1),
\]

\[
X_k=\sum_{p+q+r=k}U_pU_qU_r,
\]

\[
Z_k=\widehat Z_k+
3\sum_{p+q+r+1=k}
 \frac{B_pE[X_qX_r]}{p+q+1},
\]

\[
B_k=\sum_{p+q+r=k}A_pZ_qZ_r,
\]

\[
R_k=\widehat R_k+
3\sum_{p+q+r+1=k}
 \frac{X_pE[B_qB_r]}{p+q+1}.
\]

Finally,

\[
F^{(k)}(0)=k!\,E\!\left[
\sum_{p+q+r+s=k}A_pZ_qZ_rZ_s
\right].
\]

## Research contract and decision rule

- **Hypothesis H1.**  Replacing the quadratic activation by raw cubic leaves
  the one-matrix Gaussian detransposition method exact and computationally
  practical through \(F^{(9)}(0)\).
- **Null H0.**  A missing response or activation factor invalidates the
  recurrence, the two assemblers disagree, or the exact sparse state exceeds
  the frozen resource bound.
- **Primary output.**  The exact integer jet \(F^{(r)}(0)\), \(0\le r\le9\).
  The new orders seven and nine are accepted only after every gate below.

1. Independent, previously audited lower-order sources must be reproduced:

   \[
   F'(0)=305\,775,
   \]

   \[
   F^{(3)}(0)=154\,118\,008\,098\,000,
   \]

   \[

   \]

   The first two are frozen in
   `generic_first_stieltjes/compiler/AUDIT.md`; the fifth is the exact
   evaluation at coefficients `(0,0,0,1)` of the layer-separated order-five
   artifact whose SHA-256 is
   `5219b3558aec52a2065b93ba7d6ce0e350ee930c2048518fcd012ba61f605ec9`.

2. Readout reflection gives exact zeros at orders \(0,2,4,6,8\).
3. An ordinary-Taylor assembler and a derivative-normalized assembler with
   explicit multinomial rules must agree exactly at every order through nine.
   They may share sparse-polynomial and Wick primitives, but not recurrence
   assembly.
4. Every reported rational must have denominator one.
5. The initial gradient-block audit must equal

   \[
   50\,625+91\,125+164\,025=305\,775,
   \]

   for the \((A,W,U)\) blocks, respectively.

A lower-order mismatch is a failed computation, not evidence about the new
orders.  Exceeding the resource bound is inconclusive.

## Frozen resource and claim boundary

- Maximum order: nine.
- Per exact route: 10 minutes wall time and 4 GiB virtual memory.
- No claim is made about positive-time existence, convergence radius,
  all-order signs, Stieltjes structure, other activations, or arbitrary depth.
- “Efficient” means both exact order-nine routes finish inside this bound
  without enumerating parameter-index derivative forests.
