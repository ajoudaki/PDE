   its matching condition now uses the defined `L_g(tau(t))=L_f(t)`, and
   the lower-population innovation covariance in (C4.31) uses `E_1`.
   A blank line separates its ending from the certificate. The rest of the

   Lower population fields are `H_i^(1)` and upper fields are `H_i^(2)`;
   derivative factors appear explicitly as `phi'(Z_i)`, `phi'(Y_i)` and
   `phi''(Y_i)`. Population contractions are typed `E_1` or `E_2`.
   Generic Gaussian integrals specify their law. The covariance-difference
   functional is defined explicitly as a difference of the two upper laws.
3. Mathematical training indices remain `1,2,3` and the passive slot remains
   `x`. The bijection `iota(1)=0`, `iota(2)=1`, `iota(3)=2`, `iota(x)=3`
   identifies every C++ array slot and label token. The four-index tensor is
   `mathcal T` from (C4.27); `T_bits` remains its literal code key. Its
   row-major flat offset is stated explicitly.
4. The engine's former `E_4` denotes integration over its fourth independent
   Gaussian root. V2 writes the normalized conditional root integral
   explicitly, conditional on the first three roots. The corresponding outer
   expectations name those three roots. The text distinguishes the exact
   upper population covariance from the supplied dyadic factor's covariance,
   whose discrepancy remains charged by (C4.E6). All seventeen dynamic
   moments and both response derivatives are preserved. Literal JSON group
   names and input/output protocol fences are unchanged.
5. The cubature dimension is `q`, leaving network input dimension `d`
   unchanged. The operation-count example uses `k`, leaving width `n`
   unchanged. The two scalar angular contributions are `mathcal F(alpha)`
   and `mathcal B(alpha)`, distinct from the initialized population fields.
   Angular evaluation and node-index shorthand are defined. The reflection
   argument explicitly flips the second lower root and exchanges training
   indices 2 and 3.
6. `P=27/50` is a single rational upper bound throughout the certificate;
   the exact identity `sum_a|p_a|=(1+sqrt(5))/6<P` remains explicit.
   Both exact and dyadic label sums are bounded by this same P. This resolves
   the former reuse of P for an exact sum and later for its rational bound.
   All estimates are monotone in this positive majorant, and the numerical
   angular majorant (C4.A9) already used `27/50` in the original source.
   No signed coefficient or label is replaced by its absolute bound.
7. The arithmetic integrand paragraph now invokes the already proved
   `|phi|,|phi'|,|phi''|<=1` from (C4.E7), instead of repeating the looser
   intermediate `|dd|<=2`. Root explicitly requested this clarification:
   it states the unit bounds used by the existing product-error argument.
   This is a strengthening of a redundant intermediate estimate using an
   unchanged proved inequality, not a changed arithmetic budget or algorithm.
8. The concluding undefined `Delta(t)` is expanded as
   `R(g_tau(t))-R(f_t)`. Search-history language and a stale inspection
