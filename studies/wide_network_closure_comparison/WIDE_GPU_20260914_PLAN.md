## Observations and criteria

Save a common time grid: 0,0.005,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1, then every 0.2 through 40 (deduplicate); ensure original times 1,10,20,40 occur. Observe an interior point by affine interpolation between actual Heun endpoints, recomputing nonlinear fields, without feeding the interpolation back into training. Record loss, weighted mean prediction on the data, the 128-direction passive prediction panel, each hidden layer's data-averaged absolute activation RMS and paired RMS displacement from its own initialization. No neuron identity pairing between closure and network; compare matching scalar observables. Raw hidden initializations differ across the two representations and their errors are reported.

Primary prediction discrepancy is maximum over saved times of circle-panel RMS error (absolute output units; labels have magnitude one); also report maximum panel-coordinate error and time-integrated RMS. Hidden discrepancy is maximum saved-time absolute difference for each layer and each RMS statistic. Loss discrepancy is maximum saved-time absolute loss difference. Report each seed and width and the mean/spread without claiming three seeds yield a certified confidence interval. Report all closure orders, including unfavorable outcomes.

Descriptive agreement criterion per closure/data: primary prediction RMS <=0.05, both paired and raw hidden RMS errors <=0.03, and loss error <=0.05 for the largest-width mean, with numerical controls below 0.005 for predictions/RMS/loss. Values above 0.10 prediction RMS or 0.06 hidden RMS indicate material disagreement at this resolution; intermediate values or uncontrolled width/numerical effects are inconclusive. These are practical decision conventions, not theorem thresholds. Width sensitivity and seed spread are reported even if the criterion passes. Identical tiny terminal losses alone never establish accuracy.
