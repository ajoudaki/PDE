the full workflow; isolated reviewers must follow their neutral frozen input
scope and report missing dependencies. The two branches are not inadvertently
combined. The helper's generated study and round instructions preserve the
reviewer exception (`_workflow.py` 161, 284–324). The root file is only 4,635 bytes;
the longer workflow is explicitly a follow-through read. Existing sessions and
changed launch paths/overrides are addressed without assuming hot reload.

**Ownership and lifecycle: CLEAN.** The role table and source/data rules allocate
ordinary author work to one flat study, generated products to its private data
namespace, and maintained destinations to the assigned integration role. Review
scratch has an explicit writable location. Source, proof and unique dependencies
cannot exist only in ignored generated data. Historical outputs are preserved;
fresh run IDs and separate historical inputs are required. The data guide's
`data/established/` reproduction exception is explicit and consistent with the
workflow's maintained-recipe requirement. Administrative lifecycle labels do not
confer scientific acceptance. Adoption preserves existing records and requires
the author to reconcile them; it does not silently migrate or certify old work.

**Selection and promotion gates: CLEAN.** The workflow preserves the actual
nonlinear correlated-data objective and the scientific contract's model, metric,
clock, initialization, information, data/depth and limit-order scope. It requires
independent scientific value/duplication/placement screening, complete dependency
closure, two fresh complete adversarial reviews of identical frozen inputs,
applicable code and empirical validation, coordinator inspection of actual
evidence, and separate fresh final integration review. Corrected scientific
inputs reopen the paired complete-review gate. Incomplete or adverse reviews
block acceptance. Formal, conditional, empirical, exact and open statements are
distinguished, and useful finite or scoped results are not rejected merely for
failing to solve global dynamics. Empirical reproduction must run the producer
and analysis; archived arrays or a downstream table check are insufficient.

**Maintained dependencies and placement: CLEAN for this addition.** The gateway
explicitly sits outside the theorem book, and the helper remains shared study
infrastructure using only Python's standard library. The new policy does not
make maintained mathematics depend on study state, review packets or historical
verdicts. It instead requires accepted material to have standalone dependency
closure in the book/code. The unchanged accepted-library hashes establish that
this workflow installation did not alter those mathematical/runtime files; they
do not independently re-prove their prior boundary or correctness.

**Helper commands and schemas: CLEAN.** I traced every implementation line and
compared the documented commands with the parser and emitted schema. `start`,
`adopt`, `status`, `check`, `freeze` and `schema` agree with the documentation.
`start` refuses an existing folder; `adopt` writes only missing records. Naming,
traversal, special-file, symlink and hardlink checks are applied to the relevant
paths. A freeze requires a nonempty candidate, detects source changes during
copying, refuses an existing round, and records complete file fingerprints plus
the canonical digest. The frozen inputs are compared against both their manifest
and current candidate/dependencies. Review/selection receipts bind the digest,
distinct identities and distinct full report hashes. Code/reproduction receipts
require successful result/exit fields and nonempty matching logs under the same
study's data namespace. The selected implementation's tests exercise these
mechanical failure modes and preserve old candidate rounds.

The helper does not execute experiments, write Git, integrate material, or make
the scientific selection decision. Its explicitly stated limits are material:
identity strings, completion text, booleans and matching report bytes cannot

Undeclared relevant components/dependencies remain reviewer/coordinator duties.
