# Workflow maintenance acceptance

The permanent study lifecycle, automatic entry instructions and mechanical
promotion helper are accepted for repository use at the exact seven-file edition
in `reviews/r1/INPUTS.json`. This is process/code maintenance requested by the user,
not acceptance of a mathematical result or a new scientific library audit.

## Implemented scope

Root `AGENTS.md` introduces the complete `RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md` to new tasks.
The root gateway, study catalogue and data guide route to the same procedure.
Shared `studies/_workflow.py` initializes/adopts study records, freezes complete
selected review inputs, and checks their hashes and evidence structure. Its
deterministic tests belong to this maintenance study. It uses only the standard
library; its installed runtime does not depend on the study's drafts or reviews.

The procedure covers discovery/reuse of one research thrust, cooperating task
ownership, canonical scientific contracts, claim levels, complete persisted
arguments, code/data/reproduction records, independent value and duplication
screening, paired complete adversarial corrected-version reviews, and a separate
final integration audit. It preserves useful exact, conditional and negative
results without inflating their scopes or requiring a global-dynamics resolution.
The gate may refuse irrelevant, duplicative, invalid or scientifically weak work.

Generated products use the existing `data/generated/<study>/<run>/` convention;
maintained empirical reproductions use `data/established/<run>/`. The user's
affirmative answer authorized the private study-owned data arrangement. No
existing output namespace or historical command was migrated.
