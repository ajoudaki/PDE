# Quadratic L=2 GPU large-width ladder

**Frozen before implementation qualification or scientific execution:**
23 August 2026.

## Decision question

For the exact two-hidden-layer quadratic model already tested through width
2048, do resolved finite-width threshold times visibly shrink toward zero on
the GPU-accessible ladder through width 32768, or do they remain compatible
with a positive continuous-time transition scale?

This experiment can detect a visible boundary layer. It cannot prove or
disprove the asymptotic limit at widths beyond the ladder.

## Canonical model

The activation is phi(v) = v^2/2. For equal width n,

    h = x^2/2,
    z = W h,
    f = mean(a z^2)/2,
    loss = (1-f)^2,

with independent standard-Gaussian x and a and entries W_ji distributed as
N(0,1/n). Writing e=1-f and nu=a*z, one exact simultaneous metric-GD step is

    a_next = a + 2 Delta e (z^2/2),
    x_next = x + 2 Delta e [x * W^T nu],
    W_next = W + (2 Delta e/n) nu h^T.

Every increment uses the old state. There is no clipping, normalization,
regularization, planted extreme neuron, changed parameterization, or
autograd surrogate.

The exact tangent kernel is

    K = mean(z^4)/4
        + mean(h^2) mean((a z)^2)
        + mean([x * W^T(a z)]^2).

## Competing hypotheses

**H0: resolved positive-time scale.** Threshold-time medians and kernels
stabilize as width grows. The fast quadratic transition is genuine but does
not visibly collapse to t=0.

**H1: visible shrinking boundary layer.** Once larger Gaussian extremes are
present, threshold times decrease coherently with width after time-step
resolution, accompanied by a growing tangent kernel or tail concentration.

**Third outcome.** A weak, nonmonotone, solver-confounded, or statistically
unresolved trend is inconclusive.

## Frozen scientific design

- widths: 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768;
- keys: 9201, 9202, 9203, 9204, 9205, 9206;
- independent widths generated by a deterministic Torch-CUDA seed map from
  (key, n, salt=2026082302);
- float64 throughout;
- two RTX 3090 GPUs, one persistent worker per GPU;
- steps Delta_coarse=0.000625 and Delta_fine=0.0003125;
- physical horizon T=0.25;
- thresholds 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90, 0.95, 0.99;
- a trajectory may stop after its first 0.99 crossing;
- all six seeds are retained; there is no outlier exclusion.

Record every step through stopping: f, loss, the three exact kernel blocks,
max absolute a/x/z, largest-coordinate fraction of the readout kernel, flow
defect, and threshold times.

## Numerical validity gates

The scientific verdict is admissible only if:

1. all fine trajectories remain finite and reach f=0.95 by T=0.25;
2. every coarse trajectory remains finite and reaches f=0.95 by T=0.25;
3. at every width, the 95th percentile paired maximum predictor discrepancy
   through the common pre-0.99 trajectory is at most 0.01;
4. median paired hitting-time differences are at most 0.001 for thresholds
   0.25, 0.50, 0.75, and 0.90;
5. the fine loss has no increase above 1e-5 and the median normalized
   one-step identity defect is below 0.01;
6. deterministic replay and a NumPy-versus-GPU exact-field qualification pass
   before the scientific run;
7. peak Torch allocation stays below 18 GiB per GPU.

Any failed gate makes the result inconclusive. No failed seed is replaced.

## Frozen scientific interpretation

Use the fine trajectories and bootstrap width-wise seed medians with 5000
resamples.

Record **evidence for a visible shrinking boundary layer** only if, for each
q in {0.25, 0.50, 0.75}:

- tau_q(32768)/tau_q(2048) < 0.70;
- median tau_q is strictly decreasing over widths 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768;
- the central log-log slope is below -0.10 and the upper endpoint of its 95%
  bootstrap interval is below -0.03;

or if median f(0.005) at width 32768 exceeds 0.75 while increasing strictly
over widths 8192, 16384, 32768.

Record **evidence for a resolved positive-time scale** only if, for each q in
{0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90}:

- tau_q(32768)/tau_q(2048) lies in [0.80,1.20];
- the central log-log slope lies in [-0.08,0.08];
- its 95% bootstrap interval lies inside (-0.15,0.15);

and both median K(0) and median maximum K before f=0.75 change by a factor
below 1.5 from width 2048 to 32768 and have central log-log slope below 0.10.

All other valid outcomes are scientifically inconclusive. In every case,
report raw ratios, slopes, bootstrap intervals, early-time curves, kernel
scaling, and extreme-coordinate diagnostics.

## Qualification, budget, and stopping

Before science, a reserved non-scientific key may be used for:

- a small NumPy/GPU field and one-step identity comparison;
- deterministic GPU replay;
- at most three updates at n=32768 to verify the 18 GiB memory cap.

Qualification values are not scientific evidence. If qualification fails,
the main run does not launch.

Hard limits: 60 scientific trajectories, 45 minutes total scientific wall
time, 18 GiB peak Torch allocation per device, and 1 GiB output. There is no
post-result extension of widths, keys, steps, or thresholds in this family.
