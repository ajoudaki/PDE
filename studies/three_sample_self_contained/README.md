# Three separated inputs: self-contained theorem and proof

The full statement and proof are in [MANUSCRIPT.md](MANUSCRIPT.md). Theorem M.1 gives the result; (M.21) and (R.90) give the activation selection and complete finite constant chain.

The setting is three fixed inputs normalized by ||x_i||²=d, pairwise inner products x_iᵀx_j/d ≤ 1−δ, and labels in {−1,1}. One activation φ(z)=a_δ(1+z)+e arctan(z), with a_δ and a positive admissible upper bound on e selected from δ alone, works for every such fixed configuration. Singular input Gram matrices are included.

The theorem establishes global canonical strong raw gradient flow and its stated uniqueness/continuation; joint full-sequence compact-time limits for finite gradient flow and exact raw gradient descent; predictions, all four true kernel blocks, hidden path laws, velocity laws and integrated squared speeds; uniform all-time nonaffinity; nonzero initial motion in every hidden parameter block and every sample/layer field; and variation of the projected total kernel.

All required specialized Gaussian, response, cap-transfer and initialization arguments are proved inside the single manuscript. The remaining background is classical measure/probability, linear algebra and Hilbert-space analysis.
