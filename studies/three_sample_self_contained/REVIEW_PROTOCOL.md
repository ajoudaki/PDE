# Independent review protocol

Each reviewer is a fresh agent started without conversation history. Its only permitted reading input is one immutable copy of the complete manuscript. It may not read source notes, skills, previous manuscripts, previous reviews, author messages, project state, or external websites. It may write its own review. The procedural brief discloses no prior findings or verdicts.

Every reviewer must read the complete manuscript. Mathematical emphasis is diversified across Gaussian foundations and singular conditioning; nonlinear estimates and global continuation; finite algorithms, observation laws and initial motion; and a broad adversarial audit of quantifiers and unproved advanced invocations. The emphasis never limits whole-manuscript responsibility.

A PASS means the reviewer identified no remaining mathematical correctness or completeness objection after that audit. Any invalid step, missing substantial argument, unmet hypothesis, unjustified limit order or advanced external invocation is an objection. Reviewers record their actual reading coverage and permitted-input compliance, and cite precise manuscript locations for findings. They are asked to report uncertainty candidly, not to agree with a target verdict.

Every substantive repair requires a new manuscript snapshot and a new round of fresh agents. They receive no information about preceding reviews or revisions. Completion requires all reviewers in one final round to pass the identical manuscript hash without objections. Prior rounds remain preserved separately for provenance and are never shown to subsequent reviewers.
