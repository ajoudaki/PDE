## 3. Counted finite algorithm, conditional on Section 1

The selected metric, packets, scalar marks/history and coefficient
arrays contain O(R^2) p-bit words. The query coefficients have the same
size. At each passive stage retain all J block-mean vectors, each with
at most CR coordinates of p bits. Their CRJp<=CRp^2 bits already fit
the seed allowance. Process every sampled packet once, accumulate all
coordinates, and take their medians after the J blocks finish. Hold
incoming query context and prepared coefficients fixed until all medians
are complete, then discard the block vectors before the next stage.
Therefore retained and
post-initialization peak internal memory are

\[
                    C\{R^2p+(L+1)Rp^2\}.              \tag{2}
\]

The L factor is kept here; it is absorbed only into explicit beta powers
below, not an unnamed nonuniversal constant.

Exact metric preprocessing on the finite table uses

\[
 nR^5p^3\quad\text{work},\qquad nRp+R^3p\quad\text{peak bits}.
 \tag{3}
\]

These are the established finite-table counts with p in place of the old
word size R Theta. Finite source generation contributes at most
C(nR+R^2)p^4 for Gaussian marks, CR^5p^2+CR^4p^3 for the shared small
coefficient calculations, and CnR^2p^2 for row arithmetic. Retain these
terms before substituting the explicit envelopes (1); their substituted
parameter powers are covered by the initialization bound in (6).
Source generation uses the finite
iid packet algorithm; it does not construct n-by-n hidden matrices.
Its full field table occupies CnRp bits.
For the complete initialization peak add the retained coefficient and
seed arrays, C[R^2p+(L+1)Rp^2], to (3). They are covered explicitly by
the parameter envelope in (6); they are not treated as free later storage.

All compact acquisitions reuse conditioning coefficients by causal phase,
evaluate each selected packet's finite row program, and compute their
exact finite metric pair contractions. The core bounds give

\[
          C(R^5p^2+R^4p^3)                             \tag{4}
\]

total internal work. The CR^4 pair arithmetic and selected-row evaluation
are smaller. There is no unspecified ODE step number.

At query time a finite packet costs at most

\[
                  C(Rp^4+R^2p^3).
\]

The first term is finite Gaussian generation; the second covers the
finite vector row arithmetic, including whitening, and random-generator
block computation. The latter costs CA^2E<=CR^2p^3 per packet. Coefficients
are prepared once per stage at cost C(R^5p^2+R^4p^3). There are J<=Cp
blocks and s rows per block, with no coordinate restart. Thus query
work is bounded by

\[
 C(L+1)\{s(Rp^5+R^2p^4)+R^5p^2+R^4p^3\}.             \tag{5}
\]

Here s is the certified prior-block length. Set
K_plus=max{1,P log(1+B_F^2/eta^2)/(2 alpha)}, with B_F>=1 and explicit
confidence share alpha. The complete pair count and noise choice give
cR^2p/alpha <= K_plus <= CR^2p/alpha. Compute a dyadic certificate
K_plus<=K_hat<=2K_plus, set s=floor(n/(128 K_hat)), and retain
n>=512 K_hat. Consequently
n/(256 K_hat)<=s<=n/(128 K_hat)<=Cn/(R^2p).
The streaming part of (5) is therefore at most
C(L+1)n(p^4/R+p^3), and hence at most C(L+1)n(p^4+p^3).
No inequality p<=R is used. This is a bound on a sufficient information
certificate, not a lower bound on the true information.

The statistical price of this block choice must stay explicit. At a
good prefix, D(nu||mu)<=K_plus/n gives
TV(nu^s,mu^s)<=sqrt(sK_plus/(2n))<=1/16. For a passive test |G|<=B_pass
and whitened r-dimensional marks U with nu(UU^T)<=I and r<=CR, block
Chebyshev followed by coordinate medians gives

\[
 \|\widehat b-\nu(UG)\|_2
 \le C B_{\rm pass}\sqrt{(R+1)(\widehat K+1)/n},\qquad
 |\widehat c-\nu(G^2)|
 \le C B_{\rm pass}^2\sqrt{(\widehat K+1)/n}.            \tag{5a}
\]

The small physical passive cap B_pass differs from the numerical cap B_F.
The first radius contains the vector sampling factor sqrt(R+1); a
separate population-center radius sqrt((K_plus+R+1)/n) does not replace
it. Increasing P or decreasing eta requires this larger radius and the
sampling width gate in the passive comparison. Its propagated statistical
remainder must still fit the original dense-pair upper certificate at
the stated eventual width; the notation n^(-1/2+o(1)) alone is insufficient.
Confidence dependence through alpha is retained even though a smaller
alpha is favorable to the query-work upper bound.

The full-vector bad-median event is a one-pass finite-state test with
the state charged above. Independent stage seeds and the physical
external grid supply the same reachable-context union. No independence
of pseudorandom rows or repeated-read theorem is asserted. Sorting all
coordinate medians costs CRJ log(J+1)p<=CRp^3, below the preparation
term in (5).
