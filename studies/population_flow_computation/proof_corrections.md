The implementation factors a clean query covariance by eigenvectors times
square-root eigenvalues, after merging repeated columns. That factor can be
discontinuous under the same raw Gaussian query seed. The principal-square-root
continuity bound in the proof does not establish seedwise convergence of these
particular factors. This is separate from exact reached restart of the same

For convergence in law, condition on the training state and realize the same
full forward query covariance with its principal root and independent full-
coordinate Gaussian vectors. This gives exactly the empirical algorithm's
conditional distribution. Couple that realization to the ideal program. Include
every new query average in the ideal concentration list: current/old response
coefficients, rank contractions, reverse Gram and cross-Gram. Apply the causal
paired-error decomposition to those averages. Then condition on training plus
the forward arrays, and perform the same distribution-preserving principal-root
coupling for reverse queries. The reviewer gives the complete argument in
Section 4, including duplicate/singular cases and the fact that the ideal rows
remain iid in each population.

conservative fixed-graph rate for that row coupling is a polynomial in log P
times P^(-1/8), followed by the separate iid joint-W2 estimate. The training
P^(-1/2) rate with logarithmic factors does not apply unchanged to passive Q.
Only fixed finite query lists and polynomially controlled named observations
are covered. Arbitrary smooth functions with unrestricted growth are excluded.
