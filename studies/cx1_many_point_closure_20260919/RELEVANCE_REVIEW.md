# C-X1 relevance and scope review

Date: 2026-09-19. This is an isolated relevance assessment, not a proof-acceptance review, a code audit, or promotion approval. The question is whether the stated result would merit inclusion **if all its proof and implementation obligations were closed**.

## Verdict

**Yes, conditionally.** A theorem for every separately fixed `1 <= m <= d`, through physical time `5m`, on a fixed positive neighborhood of the distinct coordinate directions, would be a meaningful and nonredundant extension of the maintained computational branch. Its value lies in combining a many-point, dimension-general nonlinear learning episode with actual finite-network identification and a convergent, independently initialized autonomous closure. Merely deriving the orthogonal reference or making the finite solver accept more coordinates would not supply that contribution.

The appropriate description is **a complementary finite-data extension of H1–H4**, not a strict enlargement of all their scopes. The reviewed candidate is expressly unfinished. Its assembly assumes the perturbed-flow and closure interfaces; this review does not certify either interface or the missing continuation argument.

## Scientific contribution and comparison

The maintained H1–H4 computation uses two-dimensional inputs. H1/H2 give current-state sufficiency and finite population closure on a fixed positive law neighborhood and short nonlinear interval; H3 gives numerical realization for represented nonorthogonal atomic and nonatomic laws; H4 carries its explicit supported family through time 40 with substantial loss reduction and early paired motion. The general-dimension first-order implementation mentioned in the maintained guides expressly supplies no general-dimension trained-network convergence theorem.

The candidate adds an unbounded collection of **separately fixed** sample counts and dimensions, whole-sphere prediction, arbitrary binary label lists, and learning-horizon closure convergence for nearby configurations. For `m >= 3`, actual continuation on an open set of perturbed anchor configurations is outside the reviewed maintained H1–H4 domain. Retaining the whole first row matters when `d > m`: the passive Gaussian coordinates contribute to predictions away from the training span. This gives a real observation contract beyond a training-list calculation.

The architecture, Gaussian variances, mean-loss clock, physical mobilities, full initialized action and actual adjoint match the maintained conventions. The candidate does not obtain its scope by deleting the Gaussian bulk, freezing hidden layers, replacing the transpose, fitting coefficients to a learned trajectory, or identifying quadrature counts with width. The positive perturbation radius and activity margins are to be fixed before every approximation limit. These are the right non-vacuity requirements.

The loss conclusion is substantive: the population initialization has loss one, whereas the claimed final loss is below one quarter. The assembly even has a strict margin `9/64`, conditional on its inputs. Paired activation displacement in both layers supplies the stated early hidden-motion certificate on this same trajectory. It does not show that feature motion is necessary for fitting, persists to `5m`, or gives an advantage over frozen features.

## Boundaries and contract qualifications

1. **Continuation remains indispensable.** The reference file treats exactly orthogonal anchors. Its global reference flow, scalar clock, energy bounds and initial activity calculations do not construct nearby nonorthogonal flows through `5m`. Assembly interface (P) explicitly requires that construction, source/tail control, uniqueness, finite-network identification and a vanishing raw-distance modulus. Interface (C) also requires both small omitted-source errors and their propagation. The relevance verdict is conditional on these bridges, not evidence that they hold.

2. **This is a structured finite-data family.** It has `m <= d`, equal weights, binary labels, and points close to a labeled orthogonal configuration. It does not cover overcomplete training sets, unequal masses, arbitrary correlations, noisy labels, or nonatomic laws. H1/H2 and the represented H3/H4 families retain law directions absent here. The explicit nonorthogonal construction in the assembly establishes nonemptiness for `m >= 2`; it establishes no useful correlation range. For `m=1,d=1` the local sphere is discrete; for `m=1,d>1` there are no distinct pairs whose correlations could be learned.

3. **The radius is existential.** In contrast with H4's explicitly represented positive radius, the reviewed theorem only requests `rho_(m,d)>0` and the assembly chooses it through a continuity modulus. Rational stereographic inputs exist inside any such neighborhood, but that observation supplies neither an executable membership test nor a practically resolved supported nonorthogonal example. This is a limitation on constructivity and practical scope, not by itself a failure of the stated existential theorem. Do not describe the candidate as a quantitatively broad or explicitly usable correlation guarantee.

4. **Nonlinearity and activity have different strengths.** H1 part 8 and H2 part 7 also carry a positive best-affine-approximation error for reached preactivation laws. The reviewed C-X1 statement asks for paired motion and preserves tanh dynamics but does not state that extra margin. An explicit reached-law nonaffinity certificate would be a useful strengthening; it is not a missing requirement of the supplied C-X1 contract. In particular, a stronger claim of uniform relative nonlinear strength or persistent nonlinearity would need its own statement and proof.

5. **The approximation limits are appropriately separate.** Prediction uses a time-and-sphere supremum; hidden/action observations use each declared fixed same-population joint tuple in `W2`, including both initialized and current values. This avoids an unrequested whole-hidden-field path-law assertion. Width identification and numerical/closure-order convergence must identify the same target, but do not establish arbitrary simultaneous width/order growth. A raw-GD condition proved for the reference alone cannot be applied to perturbed configurations without the corresponding bridge.

6. **Fixed storage during evolution is not efficient scaling.** A feature-indexed action matrix can be a legitimate population compression without being small in `m,d`, order, precision or requested accuracy. Complete joint mark laws, initialized source programs, quadrature, arithmetic bits, data and stage workspace must remain in the accounting. A result for each fixed `m,d` gives no uniform radius, activity margin, order bound or practical complexity as `m,d` grow. The clock `T=5m` follows the mean-loss normalization and a reference estimate; it is not a computational speedup theorem.

7. **The operational evidence now has a bounded, relevant role.** The added validation plan and driver prescribe five dimension-three runs, two closure orders, time and joint integration refinements, and serialized midpoint continuation. The record reports finite losses, paired RMS, unchanged state shapes, resource use and exact own-state restart. This addresses the lack of operational deliverables in THEOREM item 5 alone. I did not rerun the driver, inspect its imported implementation or tests, or verify the generated records; the reported outcomes remain attributed to VALIDATION.md. The explicitly exploratory nonorthogonal example is correctly not claimed to lie inside the unknown radius.

   The reviewed driver records per-configuration elapsed time, retained bytes and peak RSS, but does not explicitly split initialization/evolution costs or define a conditioning diagnostic. Such information matters if the final addition claims the full operational-efficiency scope described by H3. It should accompany the implementation accounting or the claim should stay at bounded declared operation. Jointly doubling initialization and population nodes does not separate those two errors. The reported passive-prediction difference of approximately `0.061` also prevents treating the small training losses as evidence of population prediction accuracy. None of these diagnostic limitations contradicts a qualitative iterated convergence theorem.

## Placement and permissible conclusion

If the complete mathematical and executable addition later passes its required independent reviews, place it after the existing H4 computational result, as a distinct **many-point finite-data extension** with its own model/family/horizon table and links to the reusable implementation. Retain H4's nonatomic-law and explicit-radius statements separately. A suitable scientific conclusion would be:

> For each separately fixed sample count and input dimension with `1 <= m <= d`, canonical two-hidden-layer tanh training on an admitted near-orthogonal binary dataset has a substantially learning finite episode that is captured by an autonomous observable closure, with the stated qualitative limits and fixed storage at a chosen resolution.

Do not attach arbitrary-data, growing-dimension, efficient-learning, generalization, depth-advantage, all-time perturbed-flow, finite-run accuracy, or literature-priority conclusions. Nonredundancy here is relative to the assigned maintained material; no external novelty survey was authorized or performed. No maintained placement is recommended **now**, before the candidate's open interfaces are closed and the complete addition is reviewed.
