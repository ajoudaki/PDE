# Normalized quadratic activation at the exact fifth jet

Date: 25 August 2026.

Use only the already established width-first (q=1,L=2) OMFP network.
Write the normalized mixed quadratic activation as

\[
 \psi_e(x)=\frac{x+e x^2}{\sqrt{1+3e^2}}.
\]

This resolves the ambiguity in the coefficients
\(a x+b x^2\): after RMS normalization and a harmless common scale/sign,
the nonlinear amplitude is the ratio (e=b/a) when (a\ne0).

Determine rigorously:

1. the time degree of \([h^5]F_{N,2}(h)\);
2. the time degree of
   \([h^5]\{F_{2t,2}(h)-F_{t,2}(2h)\}\);
3. whether small fixed nonzero (e) changes either degree;
4. exact activation-defined formulas or terminating rational recursions;
5. the distinction between an exact fifth Taylor coefficient and a
   uniform nonzero-step fifth-order remainder.

Every positive claim must receive an independent symbolic or algebraic
audit.  Quadratic growth falls outside the bounded-derivative/linear-growth
activation class used by some earlier interval theorems, so fixed-order
polynomial integrability must be justified directly.
