## Complete proof/dependency chain used

1. Constant-metric tensor product differentiation gives A. No probabilistic
   theorem enters the weighted-tree identity.
2. Scalar Gaussian integration by parts gives the raw Gaussian moments and
   edge pairings. Equality partitions give B2 exactly at finite width.
   The elementary connected-graph edge/vertex inequality proves the leading
   forest factorization. Disjoint-union multiplication proves B6.
3. Direct differentiation of the specified finite raw-square network gives
   C1 and the row/column/bridge rewrites, including every n and block factor.
   Product differentiation and B supply the connected recursion and all
   fixed hidden/output coefficient limits.
4. Simultaneously substituting each original primitive factor by its Euler
   update attaches trees or deletes original bridges and attaches leaves.
   The exponent e/2+r rises by one per selected update factor. Binomial
   coefficients account for repeated powers. Residual powers are ordinary
   disjoint-union products. This proves D independently of a generic
   detransposition theorem.
5. Positive raw polynomial coefficients compare the expected prescribed
   feature program with its frozen-first-layer branch. Conditional Gaussian
   rows and elementary iid-moment counting identify that branch. A single
   recursively selected top-degree monomial proves D5. Adaptive loss
   schedules are identified first by D2, then compared only before the
   delta hit. No sign claim is made after residual reversal.
6. Finite Taylor composition, product rules and raw gradient equations give
   E. E9 is a literal finite product/Bell algebra with supplied linear
   response rules; Gaussian integration by parts gives E11, with a direct
   proof covering singular covariance.
7. The ordinary second chain rule proves F, including the derivative
   variable and local source. Repeated substitution gives every typed word.
8. Exact shallow coordinate multiplication and differentiation give G1/G2.
   Gaussian iid moment counting gives their deterministic initial scalars.
   A compact-box first-exit Euler estimate gives G5 without width-dependent
   vector-field constants. The identity feature solution supplies fitting,
   mark-space endpoints and the elementary beta-density moment calculation.
9. The scalar second-order equation for the shallow quadratic denominator
   supplies its characteristic and pole behavior. Gaussian monomial moments
   and formal inversion specify the finite certificate; accepted boundary
   overlap is explicitly recorded below.
10. Formal triangular coefficient comparison, completing the square and
    the binomial theorem prove all H identities. No specialized external
    theorem is imported.

Elementary local ODE existence used in E and G is supported by the stated
finite locally Lipschitz field and the integral-equation contraction

graph counting, finite-dimensional algebra, finite Taylor differentiation
and a discrete Gronwall sum are all exposed in the candidate. No external
