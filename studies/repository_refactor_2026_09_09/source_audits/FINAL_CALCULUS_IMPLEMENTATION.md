# Finite calculus: implementation, corrections and acceptance scope

The complete primary proofs and correction chain were read by the source
assembler and recorded, with hashes and exact ranges, in
FINAL_CALCULUS_ASSEMBLY.md. The coordinator read the complete assembled proof.
The first full isolated mathematical review, FINAL_CALCULUS_A1.md, supported
the substance but required three local qualifications: decorations must be
nonnegative integers; convergence in probability of random scalar steps does
not itself supply Lp convergence; and the Euler API must declare its activation
scale. Its original complete candidate and input manifest are preserved.
The corrected proof explicitly gives the moment condition, constant-step
specialization, and raw-square c=1 API. No conclusion is broadened.

The coordinator implemented the finite APIs in the existing exact_calculus
and finite_jets modules. These are finite deterministic operations, with no
training runner, implicit data, retained arrays or generator campaign:

- Canonical decorated forests, finite-width equality-partition evaluation,
  leading paired quotients, weighted raw-square derivatives, simultaneous
  raw-square loss pullback, and constant-metric contraction-tree weights.
- General supplied-state moving full-mean-loss GF coefficients through order
  five at any fixed finite depth and batch, same-layer activation/preactivation
  Gram coefficients, typed curvature words and numeric held-fixed downstream
  preactivation Hessians.
- A complete rational polynomial Gaussian head from supplied full 9-variable
  covariance and ten response constants. The actual-Gaussian polynomial mode
  is implemented; formal atoms and nonpolynomial integration are not promised.
- Exact shallow identity scalar GD, singular/positive Hankel thresholds, and
  rational Bernstein interval/degree conversion.

All old production definitions are preserved. Count/type, covariance PSD,
forest/domain, mobility, activation-callback and ownership requirements are
explicit. Float64 functions make no exact-certificate or extreme-range promise.
Exact combinatorial operations have caller-controlled finite sizes and may
be expensive. The APIs do not resolve high-order neural covariance provenance,
analytic Taylor convergence, positive-time Gaussian identification, the fully
trained quadratic GF/adaptive diagonal, or cap removal.

Sixteen added deterministic tests supplement the existing 90 scientific tests.
They compare with unrestricted finite index sums, direct rational raw gradients
and simultaneous updates, independent polynomial contraction and composition,
exact interpolatory Gaussian quadrature, finite raw variations, closed order-five
flows, typed-word evaluation and singular matrix examples. All 106 selected
tests pass, as do both guide examples. The standalone receipt records the exact
run and complete scientific inputs without studies/exporter/data dependencies.

Fresh independent complete reviews FINAL_CALCULUS_COMPLETE_A2.md and B2.md
received only the full corrected proof, guide, notation, five production files
and both complete test modules. Each read all 3986 lines, checked unchanged
hashes, ran all 41 relevant tests and both examples, and performed independent
bounded algebra checks. Both are clean without required corrections. Optional
identity-block wording and frozen-update clarity suggestions are recorded;
no input changed after either review. This is full acceptance of these finite
APIs and stated proofs, not a fresh proof audit of unrelated older chapters.
