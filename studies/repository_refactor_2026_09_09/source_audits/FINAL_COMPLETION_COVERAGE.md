for the stated missing discrete proof/recovery and coefficient-generation chains.

from unaccepted stronger producer, numerical or limit claims. The other rows
route to actual complete maintained proofs at their stated scopes. This count
is a headline map, not a count of independent theorems.

all study directories. Complete isolated mathematical audits certify the imported
proof groups, and final integration acceptance has a separate read scope.
