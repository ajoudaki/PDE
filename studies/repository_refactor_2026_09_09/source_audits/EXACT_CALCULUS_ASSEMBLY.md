## What was imported, rewritten and excluded

The forest source overview supplies the fixed decorated-forest normalization
and leading component-factorization mechanism. The addition gives a complete
free-index proof, including extra accidental equalities of decoration labels,
odd components, isolated vertices, and the exact isomorphism-key proof. It
does not invoke or certify any historical packed graph engine, pruning rule,
enumerator, weighted forest generator or large-order table. The new small
all-roots key is a transparent specification-level reference, not a claimed
replacement of the optimized historical implementations.

The Stieltjes axis donor gives the exact finite reduction with first-block
mobility zero, order-one stored Gaussian readout, and feature ascent. The
addition derives that reduction in canonical stored notation and gives an
elementary fixed-order annealed initialization-derivative limit. Its uniform
moment step is written out. This is not a positive-time trajectory theorem.

The historical generator contains a Campaign-4 artifact/hash read before
certificate construction. That wrapper was read but never executed. The new
module has no such input or authorization dependency. It computes the small
fixed order-thirteen certificate directly, with Python rational arithmetic.
Instead of importing the donor's Lagrange-inversion formula, the new proof
and code derive inverse coefficients by recursive composition matching and
compute K=F' composed with F-inverse. Thus no specialized inversion theorem
or unprovided proof is used. The displayed six fractions, determinant and
polynomial witness are regenerated and independently checked by small tests.

The source's strictly positive first-mobility extension, parameter interval
and transition certificate, and canonical order-seventeen successor were
read as statements in the report only; their separate recurrence/generator
proofs are not claimed as read or imported. They remain preserved, not
rejected. The zero-mobility diagnostic does not settle those questions or
the canonical unit-metric all-order problem. The retained canonical output
order is seventeen, not nineteen. No historical campaign was resumed.
