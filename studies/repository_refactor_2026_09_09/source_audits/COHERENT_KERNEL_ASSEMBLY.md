## Corrections retained instead of earlier overclaims

1. The domain is Bochner `L^2` in depth of the intersection of uniformly
   bounded row and column `L^2` kernels. Scalar joint measurability plus
   a finite mixed-norm expression is not silently substituted for strong
   measurability into a possibly nonseparable carrier.
2. The sentence in 12732 saying the mixed norm gives a jointly bounded
   preactivation is too strong. The assembled proof uses precisely
   `Z in L^2_s L^infinity_u`, with bounded forward states and adjoints.
   No depth maximum of the local field is needed.
3. For bounded `phi'`, the gate difference times an `L^2` adjoint does
   belong to `L^2`. The earlier phrase “need only lie in `L^1`, not
   `L^2`” is inaccurate. The displayed concentration example in the
   primary source instead obstructs a proposed **local Lipschitz
   estimate**. The addition does not infer nonexistence, discontinuity,
   or failure of uniqueness of every possible bare-`L^2` formulation.
4. The representer is the gradient in the `H=L^2(ds du dv)` pairing for
   the functional differentiated on the strong carrier. No open-domain
   Fréchet calculus or gradient-flow well-posedness on all of `H` is
   inferred from that representation.
5. Finite energy controls `W(t)-W(0)`. Subtracting only microscopic
   Gaussian noise would leave the coherent initial kernel in place.
   The assembly uses the actual initial state throughout.
6. The earlier unrestricted noisy joint width/depth rate is not part of
   this theorem. The final source itself did not complete its uniform
   nonlinear stability and discretization chain. No raw Gaussian-kernel
   convergence, maximum-over-depth local-field convergence, or genuinely
   surviving Gaussian `W/sqrt(n)` bulk is imported.
7. The strong-carrier result is a distinct dense kernel model, not a
   proof about the scalar particle system or a replacement for a missing
   Gaussian construction. Its fixed bounded endpoints, bounded activation,
   `C^2` lower-bounded loss, finite data set, and physical clock are kept.
