### 4.1 Deliberate exclusion of the assumed tagged quadratic effective model

The decisive primary source was read at §§2 and 4–9, not merely at its verdict.
It assumes a continuous Gaussian cavity process independent of the entire
initial Gaussian readout, a deterministic continuous retarded memory with
`M(0,0)=3/2+2 gamma>0`, and the self-consistent dissipation inequality
`f'=2(1-f) kappa`, `kappa>=E z^4/4`. On an event of positive probability,
its cooperative comparison is

`b'=c v²`, `v'=c m b v`, `b(0)=A`, `v(0)=z_*`.

The invariant `v²-z_*²=m(b²-A²)` yields the stated finite blow-up time
`[2cm alpha]^-1 log((A+alpha)/(A-alpha))=O(log(A)/A)`. Its divergent fourth
moment integral contradicts positive subtarget delay for a classical output
continuous at initialization. This is valid algebra and comparison **under
those postulated effective equations**. The source explicitly does not derive
the representation, complete-path independence, memory, self-consistency,
positive-time existence or network-to-model identification. Assigning the
instantaneous diagonal coefficient to the whole retarded law is an additional
assumption, not a derivation. Its discontinuous fitting step additionally
requires a monotone/no-overshoot relaxed-selection axiom. That selected trace
has the elementary continuous-approximation lower bound 1/2 (or 1 with exact
initialization), but has not been shown to be the network's trace.

This package is deliberately left as a conditional consistency test of a
postulated effective model. It provides no additional theorem about any
maintained network or its actual Gaussian source, beyond the independently
included finite quadratic/Taylor restrictions. There is no new proof search to
perform for its Riccati calculation; its missing network identification is a
real mathematical premise. This exclusion is different from B, which proves a
conditional construction for the actual raw field on its stated common spaces.
