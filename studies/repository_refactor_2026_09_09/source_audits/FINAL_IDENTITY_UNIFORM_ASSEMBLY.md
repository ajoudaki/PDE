## Scope exclusions and genuine remaining obligations

No established result within this bounded identity family was left for
later editorial incorporation. The positive theorem and the requested
deterministic boundary are fully assembled. The following conclusions
are outside it for specific mathematical reasons:

- **General nonlinear uniform remainder:** the abstract transported
  estimate is proved, but nonlinear application requires uniform
  generated-direction derivative/product/adjoint-response estimates.
  An \(L^2\) source bound does not provide all the products or higher
  moments. Bounded activation slopes alone do not verify that premise.
- **Generic nonlinear \(I+J^*\) or compact nine-moment promotion:**
  neither is used. The fixed-word Fock proof suffices for identity;
  its specialization does not imply the general nonlinear extension.
- **Bounded-slope diagonal counterexample promotion:** the witness
  refutes the deterministic state majorant, not the annealed
  output-continuity lemma or the final single-activation no-go.
  The missing probabilistic continuity bridge is a real obligation.
  The long ladder/super-remainder manuscripts and their full marked-term
  generator are not imported or newly certified.
- **Physical-loss energy-ball witness:** not asserted. Section 8 is
  exactly feature Euler; it does not omit a physical residual factor.
- **Gaussian-typical or rare-event conclusions:** the deterministic
  witness gives no typical Gaussian instability. The identity UI proof
  is for actual Gaussian initialization and a fixed program, with no
  growing-program rare-event estimate.
- **Global feature time, nonlinear continuation, or a joint depth
  limit:** these do not follow from the unit-ball polynomial argument.
  The maintained global physical linear theorem has its own loss
  dissipation proof and is not relabeled here.
- **A joint finite-width continuous-time limit:** the width limit is
  taken at each fixed program. Simultaneous or reversed limits require
  additional width-uniform flow/Euler control.
- **Scalar sewing as a state theorem:** summable scalar discrepancies
  alone do not identify a state or generator. Here the complete Banach
  flow proof supplies them separately.
