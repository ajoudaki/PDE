## Arguments imported and dependencies resolved inside the candidate

The frozen reduction is derived directly by multiplying the exact connector
update by the frozen feature vector. The raw kernel is computed from both
trained gradient blocks. The negative-row exponential envelope is uniform over
all pre-hit steps without a growing-time union bound. Conditional second moments
and a fixed positive Gaussian tail mass give a single high-probability event.
The favorable rows satisfy a discrete reciprocal/Riccati growth argument; no
Euler-to-ODE or finite-program theorem is used. Initial second moments are
computed explicitly, and the crossing argument uses actual raw-parameter
interpolation.

The ReLU normal velocity is recomputed using both connector and lower-feature
motion in the stored-matrix convention. Strict inequalities persist in adjacent
sign cells and on the gate. The absolute-continuity level-set argument is proved,
and an open upstream set is constructed directly from bounded speed and strict
normal motion. This avoids importing a transversality theorem or treating a
measure-zero contact as positive-probability initialization.

The local compactness proof gives the deterministic `R^+ <= R+6 eta R^5`
recursion, its explicit short-time bootstrap, the Gaussian matrix bound by a
finite sphere net, and output increments along the actual linear parameter
interpolant. It proves compactness of bounded equi-Lipschitz function sets by a
dense-set argument. The basic probability fact used is the stated weak
compactness theorem for tight laws on the complete separable continuous-path
space; no specialized neural, tensor-program, random-matrix-limit or Filippov
existence/uniqueness theorem is used. Gaussian integration, conditional Chebyshev,
and local finite-dimensional smooth ODE existence are the other foundational
facts, with the hypotheses and the needed estimates supplied.

The scalar occupation result is an exact telescoping identity over every window.
Its second and joint moments are kept distinct from its first marginal moment.

## Corrections and supersession retained

1. Historical files often write `r=1-f`; the candidate uses canonical
   `r_n=f_n-1` and exposes every minus sign. Unscaled historical connector arrays
   are converted to stored matrices with entries `N(0,1/n)` and mobility one.
2. The independent mesh audit requires adjacent-cell velocities, not globally
   frozen side velocities; the candidate proves neighborhood bounds and reaches
   the gate from an open set. Every fixed kink convention has its own permitted
   choice of the witness parameter. No uniform lower bound in width is asserted.
3. The same audit rejects replacing finite initialization norms by their limits.
   The candidate's local theorem is proved on an explicit event of probability
   tending to one; finite exceptional widths are handled separately for tightness.
4. The frozen rotation must specify its zero convention. With `v(0)=p`, the
   invariant strip is taken as `(eta q, eta p]`, preserving the exact identity even
   when an orbit lands on zero. This repairs the endpoint imprecision in the
   historical open-strip wording.
5. The principal local compactness source states a grid-predictor interpolation.
   The candidate also derives the cellwise Lipschitz estimate for recomputed raw
   parameters, then bounds its difference from grid-predictor and grid-loss
   interpolation. This is a supplied finite Lipschitz calculation, not an
   unproved identification of interpolation conventions.
6. The older joint-scaling and mesh verdicts treat the canonical full-quadratic
   covariant-Schur initial layer as settled. The later master report identifies
   the adaptive differentiated-word bridge gap. The candidate uses none of that
   theorem, none of its rates, and no ultra-fine full-network Euler consequence.
7. The pure frozen theorem is not transferred to all-trained dynamics: the
   exact counterexample disproves the proposed pathwise deletion comparison.
   Conversely, classical ReLU failure is not transferred to Euler nonexistence;
   the positive scalar compactness theorem is proved beside it.
