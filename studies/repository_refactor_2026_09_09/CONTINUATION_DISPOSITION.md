## Review and preservation rules used

All new maintained arguments are internal to docs/code. Reviewers receive only
complete candidate material and explicitly supplied dependencies, without
project history or earlier verdicts. Required corrections trigger new complete
reviews. In the first six packages, the required proof corrections were a missing C3
predictor assumption and forbidden finite RMS shorthand. The resumed assembly
also corrected mixed-boolean API validation. All unsuccessful full reports and
original inputs are retained, with fresh complete reviews of corrected versions.

The checkout is shared, not cloned or migrated. The coordinator alone stages
explicit file lists and checks the current HEAD/index before committing.
The eight inherited modified artifacts and the subsequently appearing
TASK_NAME_INDEX.md remain outside this work. The PDF exporter remains outside scientific acceptance. Its new concurrent
changes, together with .gitignore changes, were observed and preserved without
being edited or staged by the scientific coordinator. Historical preservation receipts
are not falsely retaken or relabeled for the current source state.
