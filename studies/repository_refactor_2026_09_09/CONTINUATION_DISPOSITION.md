# Remaining-library disposition after ae43aa4

This is a bounded incorporation decision, not a new audit of all 105 studies.
The book keeps genuinely nonlinear deep feature learning on correlated data
as its objective. Exact identities, computation primitives and conditional
or negative results are included when their complete argument can be contained.
A proof gap is not repaired by changing the architecture or running a campaign.
Final promotion status and exact accepted versions are in
[INCORPORATION_ACCEPTANCE.md](INCORPORATION_ACCEPTANCE.md).

six matching assembly records under `source_audits/`. Only clean complete

These exclusions distinguish a useful closed finite lemma from a generator,
external theorem, empirical pipeline or unresolved analytic bridge. They do not
assert that every excluded source theorem is false or that no additional
established theorem exists. Earlier accepted packages are not re-reviewed or
strengthened by this disposition. The historical master and completeness audit
remain locators, with superseded missing-coverage rows read against this edition.

## Review and preservation rules used

All new maintained arguments are internal to docs/code. Reviewers receive only
complete candidate material and explicitly supplied dependencies, without
project history or earlier verdicts. Required corrections trigger new complete
reviews. The two actual corrections in the first candidates were a missing C3
predictor assumption and forbidden finite RMS shorthand; both are preserved in
the unsuccessful reports and repaired in later versions.

The checkout is shared, not cloned or migrated. The coordinator alone stages
explicit file lists and checks the current HEAD/index before committing.
The eight inherited modified artifacts and the subsequently appearing
TASK_NAME_INDEX.md remain outside this work. The six PDF-exporter files remain
unchanged and outside scientific acceptance. Historical preservation receipts
are not falsely retaken or relabeled for the current source state.
