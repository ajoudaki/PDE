## Audit evidence and its limits

[Source audits](source_audits/) record actual source reading, code baselines,
dependency closure and remaining qualifications.
[Isolated reviews](reviews/) identify the exact input hashes they checked.
A clean verdict applies only to that version and scope. A NOT CLEAN review is
kept when a later revision fixes its findings; it is not silently relabeled.
The [validation record](VALIDATION.md) identifies completed scoped checks and
the limits of historical workflow reproducibility.

The [promotion completeness audit](source_audits/PROMOTION_COMPLETENESS.md)
distinguishes proved results awaiting editorial assembly, genuinely open
premises, import-qualified results and finite/auxiliary theorems. An omitted
result is not automatically false or unproved. Historical studies and their
frozen source hashes are preserved even when their broad scientific claims
are not promoted.

The [promotion ledger](PROMOTION_LEDGER.md) supersedes the earlier candidate's
coverage list: it records added complete chapters and preserves the distinction
between an open theorem, a qualified import and an editorial promotion backlog.
The [advisory disposition](ADVISORY_COVERAGE_DISPOSITION.md) revisits ten wider
coverage families against the actual maintained book. The scientific narrative
is expanded; computational machinery and narrower proved results remain
explicitly distinguished from unclosed imports, rather than being called
speculative merely because they are not full global-limit theorems.

## Rollback without losing work

Inspect the desired checkpoint in a separate checkout before deciding on a
rollback. Do not reset the current tree over subsequent user work. Git retains
source snapshots, not newly excluded generated data: keep `data/` in place or
back it up separately. Old campaign commands may embed old locations or seals;
consult the move and repair records before running them. No old scientific
hash seal has been regenerated to disguise a source relocation.
