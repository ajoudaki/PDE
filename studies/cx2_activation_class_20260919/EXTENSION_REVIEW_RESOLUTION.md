  result includes supported half-mass binary Borel laws and represented
  nonatomic near-orthogonal arcs. Their support radius is fixed before all
  limits. No global modulus of the second derivative is needed.
- These targets have the stated strong uniqueness, law continuity, early
  nonlazy subfamilies, same-population joint observations, actual finite
  GF/GD capture and autonomous dense closure. The executable adapter has
  complete generic Gaussian initialization, actual adjunction and restart.
  Its literal numerical theorem requires consistent activation evaluators
