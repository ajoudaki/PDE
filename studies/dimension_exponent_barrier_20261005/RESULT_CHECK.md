**Verdict: PASS for synthesis scope and the four structural arguments.** No
conditional spatial count, finite cover, or low-memory integration proposal
is represented as an autonomous neural compressor or an actual neural
impossibility theorem.

The relevant checks are:

1. The spherical polynomial dimension formula counts a coefficient table.
   Polynomial division proves its stated kernel, including the separate
   degree-zero and degree-one cases. The sine example illustrates a smaller
   nonlinear description without being presented as a trained network.
2. The conditional coefficient-ball lower bound now explicitly concerns
   uniform worst-case approximation. It retains a positive decay constant
   independent of width and polynomial bounds on both descriptor range and
   decoder sensitivity. These are the hypotheses needed to turn the
   lower-bound route's logarithmic denominator into $O(\log n)$. A Gaussian
   high-probability result is correctly said to need a separate mass bound.
3. The deterministic reference trajectory is defined before the tightness
   assumption. Compact containment of the normalized complete trajectories
   yields a finite fixed-tolerance cover at fixed confidence. The summary
   does not confuse these function-valued centers with finite stored state.
   The conditional degree $O(\log\log n)$ and spatial count
   $O((1+\log\log(en))^{d-1})$ agree with the scale audit; no coefficient
   evolution or actual-network fluctuation theorem is inferred from them.
4. The symmetry argument uses the orthogonal stabilizer of the training
   span. Equality of projected query coordinates characterizes its sphere
   orbits, because the perpendicular norms then agree. Right multiplication
   of the first matrix by an orthogonal transpose preserves its Gaussian
   law and commutes with its scalar block mobility. Thus the argument gives
   equivariance of the flow and invariance in law, not invariance of each
   initialized predictor. At full input rank the stabilizer is the identity.
5. The finite-dimensional Lipschitz-family covering bound follows from the
   stated maximum-norm grid. A data-parametrized all-time solution map is
   explicitly conditional; the information count does not establish an
   inexpensive population evaluator.
6. For the pairwise-variability statement, product measurability permits
   conditioning on the independent copy. If every possible center had ball
   probability strictly below $1-\delta$, averaging those probabilities
   would be strictly below $1-\delta$, contradicting the hypothesis. Hence
   the asserted center exists, at the same tolerance and confidence. This
   is selection by averaging ball probabilities, not a claim about the mean
   trajectory or an initialization-only way to compute a cheap center.
7. The summary keeps fixed-dimension-first limits separate from a uniform
   dimension-width theorem, and distinguishes root-width tolerances from
   logarithmic variants and the observed discrepancy of a pair of runs.
   Its warning about a generic $n^{-1/2+o(1)}$ bound is valid: for example,
   the extra factor $\exp(\sqrt{\log n})$ is subpolynomial in $n$ but exceeds
   every fixed power of $\log n$.
