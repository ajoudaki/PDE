# Primary-source screening; no external theorem invoked

The new proof is internal: elementary Gaussian conditioning, integration by parts, Hermite generating functions and Hilbert-space arguments are supplied in the report. No advanced result from the paper below is used as a premise.

A scope check examined Zixiang Chen, Greg Yang, Qingyue Zhao and Quanquan Gu, *Global Convergence and Rich Feature Learning in L-Layer Infinite-Width Neural Networks under μP Parametrization*, arXiv:2503.09565v2 (22 July 2025), especially Section 3, Assumption 4.1, Theorem 4.5 and Corollary 4.6. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.09565v2).

Its data assumption excludes repeated absolute pairwise inner products and zero inner products; the present target includes such triples. Its stated iteration results and conditional stationary-point corollary do not themselves provide the required raw GF/vanishing-step joint path-and-velocity limit for every separated triple. Accordingly it was not invoked. This is a scope screen, not a claim to have audited that paper's complete proof or a claim that the full literature contains no relevant method. No external-source correctness claim supports the new theorem.
