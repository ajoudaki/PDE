# Isolated adversarial review protocol

Each final reviewer is a fresh agent spawned with no inherited conversation. The only mathematical input is one immutable copy of REPORT.md in a neutral directory. No project notes, skills, source drafts, previous reviews, conversation history, websites, experiments, or other agents' analysis are allowed. Every reviewer must read the entire report and record its exact hash and full reading coverage.

The reviewers receive different emphases: Gaussian foundations and quantitative constants; functional analysis and conditional continuation; counterexamples, sharpness and quantifiers. Each still checks the entire report, including advanced invocations and missing hypotheses.

A PASS concerns only the initialization theorem and the conditional continuation theorem in their stated scopes. It cannot certify the explicitly unresolved global trained conjecture. Any substantive finding must be repaired; substantive revisions require fresh isolated reviews of the exact new report bytes. Full reports are read by the author and preserved verbatim.

The earlier isolated initialization-only review is preparatory and concerns a different input hash. Its contextual qualifications were not used as proof evidence and were not shown to final reviewers. The delivered report supplies its finite model and all definitions and does not rely on external-document endorsements.
