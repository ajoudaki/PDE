# Campaign 5 result: three equicorrelated inputs

## Final outcome

Campaign 5 is **inconclusive at its intended Stieltjes test**.  It found no
counterexample, but it also did not complete the order-seven derivative
needed to test the first nontrivial Hankel determinant.

What completed exactly is still useful:

- the three-input equicorrelated parameter is genuinely nontrivial rather
  than a removable input, output, or time scaling;
- the exact mean-field feature derivatives through order five were computed
  as polynomials over the full PSD interval $-1/2\le\rho\le1$;
- a transparent labelled-Wick calculation and the connected compiler agree
  through order three;
- the first genuine three-color cycle contraction survives and produces odd
  powers of $\rho$;
- every canonical, two-color, normalization, parity, and lower-order
  sectorization gate passed;
- the preregistered order-seven projection gate failed because the
  final-source zero-W-hit pilot did not finish within 1,800 seconds.

The failure was treated as a resource failure, not as mathematical evidence
for or against Stieltjes positivity.  Stage-C production is disabled and no
order-seven coefficient from an incomplete pilot was interpreted.

## 1. Exact training reduction and formal object

For input color $\alpha\in\{1,2,3\}$, the two-hidden-layer quadratic model
at initialization can be represented as

$$
z_i^\alpha=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{j=1}^n
W_{ij}(u_j^\alpha)^2,
\qquad
f_\alpha=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n a_i(z_i^\alpha)^2.
$$

The readout weights $a_i$ and middle weights $W_{ij}$ are independent
standard Gaussians.  For each $j$, the first-hidden preactivations have the
equicorrelated Gaussian law

$$
(u_j^1,u_j^2,u_j^3)\sim\mathcal N(0,G(\rho)),
\qquad
G(\rho)=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&\rho&\rho\\
\rho&1&\rho\\
\rho&\rho&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The admissible interval is exactly

$$
-\frac12\le\rho\le1,
$$

because the eigenvalues of $G(\rho)$ are $1+2\rho,1-\rho,1-\rho$.
The same matrix $G(\rho)$ is used both in the initialization law and in the
first-layer gradient metric.  This second occurrence is essential: omitting
it would compute a different training problem.

The labels are $(1,1,1)$ and the average squared loss is

$$
L=\frac13\sum_{\alpha=1}^3(f_\alpha-1)^2.
$$

Define the permutation-invariant output

$$
g=\frac{f_1+f_2+f_3}{3}.
$$

On the invariant symmetric channel $f_1=f_2=f_3=g$,

$$
L=(g-1)^2
$$

and the full squared-loss gradient is exactly parallel to $\nabla g$:

$$
\nabla L
=\frac23\sum_{\alpha=1}^3(f_\alpha-1)\nabla f_\alpha
=2(g-1)\nabla g.
$$

Consequently, under the mean-field metric convention,

$$
\dot g=2(1-g)K_3(g;\rho),
$$

where $K_3$ is the scalar output kernel generated by feature ascent of $g$.
Thus this campaign tests a faithful scalar reduction of natural squared-loss
training, not an auxiliary objective unrelated to training.

Let

$$
D=n\nabla g\mathbin\cdot\nabla,
\qquad
F_3^{(k)}(0;\rho)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb E[D^k g].
$$

The compiler removes factors of three by setting

$$
A=f_1+f_2+f_3,
\qquad
\widetilde D=n\nabla A\mathbin\cdot\nabla=3D,
$$

and computing

$$
J_k(\rho)=\mathbb E[\widetilde D^kA].
$$

The desired derivative is then recovered exactly by

$$
F_3^{(k)}(0;\rho)=\frac{J_k(\rho)}{3^{k+1}}.
$$

These are fixed-order formal mean-field limits.  No positive-time or global
trajectory is inferred from their existence.

## 2. Why this parameter adds new information

For two inputs, every invariant depends only on the squared correlation.
Three inputs introduce a cycle invariant.  The smallest example is

$$
\mathbb E[(u^1)^2(u^2)^2(u^3)^2]
=1+6\rho^2+8\rho^3.
$$

The $8\rho^3$ term records a three-color triangle and changes sign when
$\rho$ changes sign.  It cannot be reconstructed from separate two-input
tests.

There is also an empirical algebraic check that the entire family is not a
rescaled copy of one curve.  The scale-invariant ratio

$$
\mathcal I(\rho)
=\frac{F_3'(0;\rho)F_3^{(5)}(0;\rho)}
{F_3^{(3)}(0;\rho)^2}
$$

takes the three distinct exact values

$$
\mathcal I\!\left(-\frac12\right)
=\frac{329279908}{107729503},
$$

$$
\mathcal I(0)
=\frac{791075688939}{261115847168},
$$

$$
\mathcal I(1)
=\frac{29831494015}{9860573312}.
$$

Therefore $\rho$ survives after all separate input, output, and time scalings
have been removed.

## 3. Exact MFP implementation

The connected mean-field-peeling state is a decorated bipartite tree:

- a row vertex stores the exponent of one readout Gaussian $a_i$;
- a column vertex stores the exponent triple of
  $(u_j^1,u_j^2,u_j^3)$;
- an edge represents a middle-weight factor $W_{ij}$.

Differentiation uses three exact local rewrites, corresponding to a hit on
$a$, $u$, or $W$.  A $W$ hit removes a bridge and splits the tree; the
remaining derivative order is distributed between the two components by the
exact binomial Leibniz convolution.  An off-diagonal $u$-metric hit inserts
one factor of $\rho$.  At derivative order zero, the remaining $W$ factors
are contracted exactly and each column receives its exact trivariate
Gaussian moment polynomial.

The production compiler adds two exact quotients:

1. graph isomorphisms are canonicalized;
2. the six permutations of the three input colors are canonicalized.

Terminal Wick contractions are evaluated through an equivalent exact
vertex-partition recursion using checked signed 1024-bit integers.  Overflow
throws instead of wrapping.

For Stage C only, a separate immutable compiler graded every history by its
number of $W$ hits.  The sum of its six order-five sectors reproduces every
coefficient of the independent dense order-five result.  This proves that
the grading is an exact partition rather than an approximation.

## 4. Stage A: exact order-three feasibility

The raw polynomials are

$$
J_1(\rho)=423+240\rho^2+336\rho^4
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}
J_3(\rho)={}&8671872
+24671424\rho^2
+2582784\rho^3\\
&+52017024\rho^4
+10037760\rho^5
+23721216\rho^6\\
&+7507968\rho^7
+7289856\rho^8.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
F_3'(0;\rho)=\frac{141+80\rho^2+112\rho^4}{3},
\qquad
F_3^{(3)}(0;\rho)=\frac{J_3(\rho)}{81}.
$$

All even derivatives through order two vanish exactly.  The odd powers in
$J_3$ are not unexplained aggregate coefficients: the transparent oracle
records, among other terms, a terminal column class with exponent pattern
$(3,1,2)$ and moment polynomial

$$
3\rho+6\rho^2+6\rho^3.
$$

Its support uses all three colors, giving an explicit surviving cyclic
sector.

The independent labelled-edge Wick oracle and connected compiler agree on
every coefficient through order three.  At $\rho=1$, the normalized values
are

$$
F_3'(0;1)=111,
\qquad
F_3^{(3)}(0;1)=1\,685\,184,
$$

matching the accepted canonical one-input derivatives.

The accepted S3-quotiented connected run used 294 derivative states and 255
terminal states.  Its order-three recursion took $0.0122699$ seconds, with
$0.02$ seconds wall time and $4{,}504$ KiB peak RSS.  The independent Python
oracle took $5.43$ seconds and $87{,}528$ KiB.  Both were below the frozen
10-minute and 2-GiB caps.

Orders three and five were preregistered as feasibility and novelty gates,
not as Stieltjes successes.

### Post-hoc lower necessary moment signs

After Stage C had closed, a cheap exact consequence of the already accepted
jets through order five was certified.  Set

$$
A=J_1,\qquad B=J_3,\qquad C=J_5,
\qquad P=4B^2-AC.
$$

Because $a=A/9$, $b=B/81$, and $c=C/729$, the two available moment
candidates simplify to

$$
\mu_0=\frac{B}{2A^2},
\qquad
\mu_1=\frac{3P}{8A^5}.
$$

Exact Sturm counts on both $[-1/2,0]$ and $[0,1]$ show that $A$, $B$, and
$P$ have no real root in either half-interval.  Their exact endpoint values
are all positive:

| polynomial | at $-1/2$ | at $0$ | at $1$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $A$ | $504$ | $423$ | $999$ |
| $B$ | $17\,794\,728$ | $8\,671\,872$ | $136\,499\,904$ |
| $P$ | $298\,747\,724\,954\,112$ | $72\,975\,657\,523\,104$ | $18\,160\,258\,685\,101\,344$ |

Consequently,

$$
\mu_0(\rho)>0,
\qquad
\mu_1(\rho)>0
\quad\text{for every }-\frac12\le\rho\le1.
$$

This is explicitly a **post-hoc lower necessary Stieltjes check**.  It is not
the preregistered Campaign-5 success criterion, contains no Hankel
determinant, and does not repair the missing $F_3^{(7)}$, $\mu_2$, or
$\Delta_1$.

## 6. Why order seven was necessary

Write

$$
a=F_3'(0;\rho),\quad
b=F_3^{(3)}(0;\rho),\quad
c=F_3^{(5)}(0;\rho),\quad
d=F_3^{(7)}(0;\rho).
$$

Formal inversion gives

$$
K_3(y;\rho)
=a+\mu_0y^2-\mu_1y^4+\mu_2y^6+O(y^8),
$$

where

$$
\mu_0=\frac{b}{2a^2},
\qquad
\mu_1=\frac{4b^2-ac}{24a^5},
$$

and

$$
\mu_2=\frac{a^2d-26abc+70b^3}{720a^8}.
$$

The first ordinary nontrivial Hankel determinant is

$$
\Delta_1
=\det\begin{pmatrix}\mu_0&\mu_1\\\mu_1&\mu_2\end{pmatrix}
=\mu_0\mu_2-\mu_1^2.
$$

Both $\mu_2$ and $\Delta_1$ require $d=F_3^{(7)}(0;\rho)$.  Consequently,
F3 and F5 alone cannot answer the campaign's discriminating Stieltjes
question.

Had order seven completed, the reduced numerators and denominators of
$\mu_0,\mu_1,\mu_2,\Delta_1$ would have been certified separately on
$[-1/2,0]$ and $[0,1]$ by exact Sturm root counts and endpoint signs.  No
grid test or positive-coefficient heuristic was designated as the primary
certificate.

## 7. Stage-C resource gate and terminal stop

The final sector source and reproducible binary were frozen at SHA-256

```text
source  f1912e81b2f25bdef04bcef9c490a0975757a64deda4cb55f74c7c50abfe64ce
binary  59d949b0808d92b946ec55a856764f43ed4ccbcc922c5849561c9ba73e175fbf
```

Before any order-seven production, three representative pilot sectors were
fixed:

- zero W hits, probing the terminal-contraction-heavy extreme;
- three W hits, probing the mixed interior regime;
- seven W hits, probing maximal bridge splitting.

The written projection rule was frozen at 19:58 CEST, after the final-source
zero-W-hit pilot had already launched at 19:44:48 CEST and had remained
unfinished for roughly thirteen minutes.  It was nevertheless frozen before
any pilot completed, before any order-seven coefficient was available, and
before the 1,800-second failure boundary.  This chronology weakens the fully
prospective purity of the engineering projection design and must be retained
when weighing the campaign.  It cannot favorably bias the actual conclusion:
the observed run failed the newly written cutoff and the campaign was closed
rather than granted more time.

Each was run under a 4-GiB address-space cap.  Authorization required all
three to finish within 1,800 seconds, all three to remain below the memory
cap, and the conservative projection

$$
C_{\rm pilot}+8\max(C_0,C_3,C_7)
\le21{,}600\text{ CPU seconds}.
$$

The final-source zero-W-hit pilot produced no complete order-seven result by
its 1,800-second cutoff.  It therefore failed the first authorization
condition.  The other two pilots were stopped safely and no production
sector was launched.

The timing wrappers were interrupted before emitting exact CPU and peak-RSS
records.  The provenance therefore does not invent those measurements.  It
debits the conservative elapsed-wall upper bounds instead:

| sector | final status | conservative budget debit |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | failed to complete by cutoff | 1,826.050744 s |
| 3 | stopped after common gate failed | 1,470.081830 s |
| 7 | stopped after common gate failed | 1,470.037829 s |
| total | Stage C unauthorized | 4,766.170403 s |

The zero-sector process was terminated at the next polling boundary, at most
$26.050744$ seconds after its cutoff.  No post-cutoff output was accepted,
and the entire polling tail is included in the conservative debit.  This
late termination is recorded as a procedural imperfection; it cannot change
the fail-closed conclusion.

Two older diagnostic logs are explicitly excluded: they predate the final
source and binary, invoke an obsolete temporary executable, and in one case
contain embedded NUL bytes.  They are not projection evidence.

The durable runner now refuses unconditionally to launch Stage C.  There was
no complete order-seven polynomial, no accepted partial coefficient, no
Sturm calculation, and no order-nine, B=4, non-equicorrelated, or mixed-label
branch.

## 8. What this campaign establishes—and does not

Established exactly:

1. the B=3 equicorrelated family is a faithful natural-loss symmetry
   reduction;
2. the same input Gram matrix is retained in initialization and gradient
   geometry;
3. a genuine three-color cycle invariant survives MFP;
4. exact derivatives through order five are available over the entire PSD
   interval;
5. $\rho$ changes scale-free jet shape nontrivially;
6. all applicable independent-oracle, parity, normalization, canonical,
   two-color, and sector-partition gates pass.

Not established:

1. $F_3^{(7)}(0;\rho)$;
2. $\mu_2(\rho)$ or $\Delta_1(\rho)$;
3. any new three-input Hankel PSD theorem or counterexample;
4. all-order Stieltjes positivity;
5. existence or uniqueness of a representing measure;
6. identification of the formal resolvent with a global mean-field training
   trajectory.

The scientifically correct classification is therefore: **new exact
three-input structure and a successful low-order MFP extension, but an
inconclusive Stieltjes experiment because the preregistered order-seven
resource gate failed.**
