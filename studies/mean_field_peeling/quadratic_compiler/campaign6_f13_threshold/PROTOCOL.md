# Campaign 6: bounded canonical F13 threshold certification

Protocol frozen before implementation or execution on 13 August 2026.

## Decision target

For the canonical one-input, two-hidden-layer quadratic network, let

$$
D_{13}=F^{(13)}(0).
$$

The next shifted Hankel determinant is an affine decreasing function of
$D_{13}$.  Its unique zero is the exact rational threshold $T$ generated by
the accepted `exact_d13_threshold.py`; numerically,

$$
T=1.300194546159283\times 10^{32}.
$$

The campaign seeks a rigorous interval

$$
L\le D_{13}\le U
$$

with either $L>T$ or $U\le T$.  Computing the full integer $D_{13}$ is not
the goal.  A certified $L>T$ falsifies the canonical Stieltjes conjecture;
a certified $U\le T$ proves the next shifted Hankel condition.  Any interval
containing $T$ is inconclusive.

All comparisons with $T$ use exact rational arithmetic.  The decimal above
is descriptive only.

## Admissible certificates

An accepted lower bound may retain an explicitly described subset of the
nonnegative MFP derivative/Wick histories.  Every retained multiplicity must
be exact and no history may be counted twice.

An accepted upper bound must cover every omitted positive history by a proved
inequality.  Empirical ratios, low-order extrapolations, fitted tails, and the
previously falsified local two-hit charging inequality are not upper-bound
certificates.

Permitted research directions are:

1. threshold-aware positive lower subsums;
2. sectorwise exact or interval bounds;
3. branch-and-bound over vertex partitions or Wick pairings;
4. proved upper bounds on omitted partition families;
5. exact reuse of canonical subproblems across sectors.

The following are forbidden:

- the existing multi-day full P14 base enumeration;
- resuming or relying on untracked `/tmp` checkpoints;
- any uncheckpointed long sector enumeration;
- the three eight-hour root-class branch calculations;
- treating a non-crossing positive lower bound as evidence for positivity;
- using the unrestricted GF(2)/matroid gate or the retracted singleton-row
  shortcut;
- using factor nine or another aggregate multiplier without a new proof.

## Mandatory validity gates

No new bound is interpreted until the implementation reproduces:

1. every exact D9 sector $P=1,\ldots,10$;
2. the accepted D11 total;
3. every D11 sector used directly by the proposed bound, including the exact
   high-sector values when applicable;
4. checked-integer or arbitrary-precision arithmetic with no silent overflow;
5. disjointness and coverage invariants for every retained/omitted branch
   partition.

If a shortcut is scoped to a structural family, tests must compare it
base-by-base with the accepted exact evaluator on every reachable D9 base in
that family and on an independently selected D11 sample.  Regression is
necessary but never substitutes for a proof of the shortcut.

## Initial bounded probe

The total initial budget is at most two CPU-hours.  Memory is capped at
4 GiB per process.  No individual benchmark may exceed 15 wall-clock minutes.
Every benchmark records command, source hash, wall time, CPU time, peak RSS,
exit status, and durable output or checkpoint hash.

The initial probe proceeds in this order:

1. reproduce the exact threshold and freeze its rational value;
2. inventory sector sizes and the contribution required to cross $T$;
3. test only bounded candidate lemmas or threshold-aware evaluators on D9 and
   D11;
4. benchmark a representative D13 subset for at most 15 minutes;
5. derive a certified global interval if the proved bounds permit it.

The probe stops immediately when threshold separation is certified, when the
two-CPU-hour budget is exhausted, or when every surviving method lacks a
proved global omitted-mass bound and a credible separation projection.

## Extension gate

No production run beyond the initial two CPU-hours is authorized by this
protocol.  An extension may be proposed only if a preregistered benchmark:

1. has passed all D9 and D11 gates;
2. uses a proved certificate, not an empirical estimator;
3. checkpoints every reusable completed unit;
4. demonstrates a quantitative projection that separates $T$ within at most
   eight additional CPU-hours and 4 GiB per process;
5. states a fixed terminal stopping rule before the extension begins.

Absent all five conditions, the campaign terminates with a no-go report or a
precise missing algorithmic lemma.

## Claim levels

- Exact threshold algebra and completed exact sector values are exact
  computational certificates.
- Retained positive subsums are lower bounds only.
- A proved omitted-family domination gives an upper bound only over its stated
  structural scope.
- Timeout, memory failure, incomplete coverage, or a failed regression gate is
  inconclusive.
- Failure of this campaign does not count against the Stieltjes conjecture; it
  only shows that the tested threshold-certification mechanisms did not
  resolve the next determinant under the resource bound.

## Durable artifacts

All new source, protocols, compact outputs, logs, and provenance are confined
to this directory.  Ordinary binaries, caches, and large transient
checkpoints are ignored.  Existing parent compiler sources are read-only
dependencies and are identified by SHA-256 in the final provenance record.
