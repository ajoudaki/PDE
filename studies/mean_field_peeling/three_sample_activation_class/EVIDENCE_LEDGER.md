| Claim | Scope | Evidence | Current classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Every normalized nonconstant C² bounded perturbation admits a sufficient gain and positive amplitude cutoff | Three inputs, three hidden layers, binary labels, original raw metric; data-independent activation selection | Complete manuscript M.1 and M.21, all internal Parts F/R/G/V/N | Proved in the manuscript; four fresh isolated full-report reviews PASS |
| Persistent nonaffinity for general bounded shapes | Uniform over samples, layers, training time and admissible data; coefficient may depend on shape and separation | G.6–G.8, explicit interval residual, finite initial variance range, controlled displacement | Derived directly |
| One common constant recipe over an infinite-dimensional class | Norm bounds plus a fixed positive interval regression margin | A.1–A.2 | Derived directly |
| An infinite-dimensional class with a separation-independent nonaffinity coefficient | Small C_b² ball around scaled arctangent | A.3–A.7 and the direct Gaussian saturation calculation | Derived directly |
| Two-sided cosine separation is sufficient | Requested class is contained in the stronger one-sided separated class; singular Gram allowed | G.1–G.5a | Derived directly |
| Same theorem without the gain, or for arbitrary bounded activations alone | Not established by this manuscript | No asserted bridge | Not claimed |
| Arbitrary depth or arbitrary sample count | Not the target of this extension | Three-input geometry, three-layer dynamics and binary-label initial-motion argument explicitly retained | Not claimed |

The interval density bound replaces the inapplicable assumption that every bounded nonconstant shape has a positive Gaussian regression residual at arbitrarily large scales. The Gaussian projection now includes the mean of a nonodd shape. Lower gate bounds use a/2. No former result is invalidated; the earlier arctangent manuscript remains unchanged.
