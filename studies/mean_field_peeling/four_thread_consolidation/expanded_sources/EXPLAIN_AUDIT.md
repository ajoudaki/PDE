## Exact common contract

Here L is the number of hidden layers, m the sample count, and n hidden width. Every canonical trained result in this task has **m=1, input exactly 1, target exactly 1, phi(z)=arctan(z)**. It does not state arbitrary real-label or multi-input extensions.

The first preactivation has iid N(0,1) coordinates; hidden matrices have iid N(0,1/n) entries; the rescaled output vector C has iid N(0,n^-2) coordinates, independent of all other blocks. Prediction is f=<C,h^L>/n, loss is (f-1)^2. Thus if raw output is c=C/n, its coordinate variance is n^-4. Population C0=0 is a limit, not a replacement of the actual prescribed finite initialization. Raw GF has vector rates -2(f-1) times the relevant gradient field and matrix rate -2(f-1) delta^l(h^(l-1))^T/n. Vector metric is normalized Euclidean, matrix metric ordinary Frobenius. Actual GD is simultaneous raw Euler with eta=n^-2 and physical t=k eta; raw parameters interpolate linearly and hidden fields are recomputed.

The limiting state has finitely many **fields and bounded operators**, not finitely many real coordinates. At L=3 it is (F(Z1),W2,W3,C), F(z)=z+z^3/3, on three generated probability spaces. Both matrix orientations and actual adjoints survive. The affine HS increments of the operators, rather than their initial actions, have finite HS norm. Across widths the topology is a joint empirical action law, not operator-norm convergence between different ambient spaces. Same-width GD/GF comparison does use the displayed operator norm.

“Global joint” means every fixed finite physical [0,T], not a width limit uniform over [0,infinity). “Absolute positive-time joint” means one explicit T0>0 independent of n, with full joint conclusions on that interval; it meets the requested semi-global convention. Restart uniqueness within that interval does not prove continuation beyond it.

## Distinct global finite optimization theorems — missing from the earlier inventory

### E-P3 — L=3 GF globally fits the sample, with uniform conditioning and parameter convergence

**Status: proved finite-width theorem, not a population theorem.** [Complete proof](EXPLAIN_PRIMARY/l3-full-resolution-9nbD4z/READOUT_COERCIVITY.md), hash `0bd062da74fbde03d41a125561beee7345915ea6aa0578ed8e7b81d2c9f08c53`, two independent full-file PASSs (one fresh-context).

The stronger deterministic result assumes bounded initial hidden operator norms, a positive initial top feature norm ||h3(0)||/sqrt(n)>=b>0, and sufficiently small initial readout **RMS** depending only on b and the hidden operator bounds. No readout coordinate maximum hypothesis is needed. In feature time the readout obeys C''=P(s)C with P(s)>=0; convexity of ||C||/sqrt(n) yields ||h3(s)||/sqrt(n)>=b/2 and total kernel K(s)>=b^2/4 for all s>=0. For exact zero readout the stronger floor is the actual initial m3,n=||h3(0)||^2/n; the degenerate m3,n=0 case is stationary.

For the prescribed independent Gaussian initialization define m1=E atan(G)^2, m2=E atan(sqrt(m1)G)^2, m3=E atan(sqrt(m2)G)^2>0. Taking b=sqrt(m3)/2 and hidden operator bound 10 yields, with probability tending to one, **simultaneously for every physical t>=0**:

- K_n(t)>=||h3(t)||^2/n>=m3/16;
- 0<1-f_n(t)<=1-f_n(0) (strict decrease for t>0), and 1-f_n(t)<=(1-f_n(0))exp(-m3 t/8);
- loss decays at least as (1-f_n(0))^2 exp(-m3 t/4);
- feature clock has finite total length, at most 16(1-f_n(0))/m3;
- every parameter converges to the finite-width interpolating endpoint, with exponentially small remaining path length in the normalized-vector/Frobenius-matrix metric.

The same theorem supplies all-time uniform trained Frobenius displacements, positive lower hidden **second moments** (not automatically variances), and integrated backward energy. The endpoint is unique for the given initial condition; uniqueness among all minimizers is not claimed. Global flow at finite width was known earlier; uniform fitting, coercivity and exponential parameter convergence are genuinely additional conclusions.

### E-P4 — L=3 exact GD globally fits without overshoot

**Status: proved finite-width theorem, distinct from E-P3.** [Complete proof](EXPLAIN_PRIMARY/l3-full-resolution-9nbD4z/EXACT_GD_COERCIVITY.md), hash `fcac71917f0a0c60c4368d7c86167aae0689ae87778622ab8745ac213fa9c35c`, two independent full-file PASSs on the final canonical rewrite including the Gaussian corollary and explicit width threshold. Under the same kind of deterministic bounded-hidden/positive-feature/small-readout-RMS hypotheses and n>=N(M,b), exact eta=n^-2 GD satisfies f_k<f_(k+1)<1, top feature RMS>=b/2, and 1-f_k<=(1-f0)(1-eta b^2/4)^k, for **every** k. Every parameter converges to a finite interpolating endpoint. N(M,b) is explicit in equations (6)–(9); there is no all-small-width guarantee.

For the prescribed Gaussian initialization, with probability tending to one, **simultaneously over all k>=0**:

- ||h3_k||^2/n>=m3/16;
- 0<1-f_k<=(1-f0)(1-eta m3/16)^k <=(1-f0)exp(-m3 k eta/16);
- with alpha_k=2eta(1-f_k), sum_(j>=k) alpha_j<=32(1-f_k)/m3;
- d(theta_k,theta_infinity)<=32V(1-f_k)/m3 in the displayed normalized-vector/operator-matrix distance.

The computational feature clock is summable; sum eta is infinite. This proof handles the actual raw Euler remainder and avoids invoking an unproved global GF/GD stability bridge. **Neither E-P3 nor E-P4 implies that their endpoints converge with n, that their population limit is global, that GF and GD are globally close, or that width and infinite-time limits interchange.**
