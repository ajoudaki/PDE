# Limited primary-source screen

2026-09-08. This was a search for an applicable missing continuation
theorem, not an exhaustive literature review or a premise for claiming
that the mathematical problem is open worldwide.

- [Chen, Vanden-Eijnden and Bruna, JMLR27(52),2026](https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v27/22-1232.html): the primary abstract explicitly fixes the first-layer weights. Its stated model therefore cannot establish the requested jointly trained theorem without a new transfer argument.
- [Nishiyama and Imaizumi, arXiv2602.06320v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06320v2): Section2 fixes the number of output coordinates m while data count and dimension grow proportionally, and evolves X theta with fixed Gaussian data X. It does not directly cover two jointly trained hidden layers with three fixed samples and the present initialization.

Neither result is used as a proof of the requested target or a no-go
theorem. The partial mathematical conclusions here are derived in the
local companions from their explicit hypotheses.
