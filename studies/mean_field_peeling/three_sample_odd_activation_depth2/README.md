# Three-input odd activation at two hidden layers

[REPORT.md](REPORT.md) gives the result and its exact scope.

**The full polynomial activation-threshold theorem remains unproved.**
The proved initialization estimate is lambda_min(Q2)>=c0 theta²delta²,
with a matching worst-case upper bound through the second hidden layer.
The admitted equilateral triple rules out a theta-independent loss rate.
Successful fitting requires raw excursion Omega(theta^-1/2) and time
Omega(theta^-1). This is not a counterexample to the qualitative
positive-theta population/GF/GD theorem.
