# Arbitrary-polynomial uniform-tail question

For the same width-first q=1, L=2 ascent network, let

\[
 \phi(x)=\sum_{k=0}^d c_kx^k,\qquad d\ge2,\qquad c_d>0,
 \qquad \mathbb E\phi(G)^2=1.
\]

Determine whether, for every such fixed activation and every \(\rho>0\),
the effective paired remainder

\[
 \sup_{0<h\le\rho/t}
 \frac{|F_{2t}(h)-F_t(2h)-\kappa_th^3|}{h^5}
\]

grows faster than the polynomial required by the uniform gradient-flow
result.  Lower coefficients may have arbitrary signs.  A proof may not use
the positive-coefficient deletion inequality unless its hypotheses are
actually recovered; the quadratic argument does not automatically extend.
