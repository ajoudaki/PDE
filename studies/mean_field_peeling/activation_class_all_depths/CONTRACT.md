# Depth-uniform activation proof contract

The target is the complete three-input theorem for phi(z)=a(1+z)+e psi(z), where psi is a fixed nonconstant C² function with sup norms of psi, psi′ and psi″ at most one. The pair a,e may depend on the required angular separation and the function, but must be independent of hidden depth L>=2, width, training time, and the particular admissible dataset. Input norms are sqrt(d), labels are binary, and the requested cosine interval is (-1+delta,1-delta), 0<delta<1. A theorem under the stronger one-sided upper-separation condition is acceptable.

Preserve the original Gaussian initialization, raw metric, simultaneous gradient descent with step n^-2 and raw interpolation, and true gradient flow. The proof may normalize fields by exact deterministic layer scalings but may not change the trained parameters, raw metric or physical algorithm. Required conclusions are global canonical strong population GF with the stated uniqueness/restart, full-sequence fixed-depth compact-time finite GF/GD limits, true kernels, paths, velocities, integrated speeds, persistent nonaffinity, every hidden block/sample/layer initial motion, and projected-kernel variation.

The activation pair must be common to every separately fixed finite L. Constants in the width-convergence estimates and the width required for a given accuracy may depend on L. No simultaneous L(n)-width limit is claimed. For the broad bounded function class, the positive nonaffinity coefficient may depend on L; a separate theorem will address a uniform coefficient under a uniform Gaussian regression hypothesis. A compactly supported perturbation already prevents a uniform positive initialization margin as depth grows.

Authorized work: new theoretical proofs, local manuscript artifacts, full proof/dependency examination and independent adversarial agents. No numerical experiments. Do not treat another study's earlier no-gain restriction as applying to this request, which explicitly asks for constants a,e in the just-established gain family. No hard runtime or token budget was supplied.

All specialized ingredients used must be proved inside the final report or fully audited from complete primary sources. Prefer the existing elementary Gaussian norm bound 10 so no nonclassical operator-norm theorem is needed. Fresh final reviewers receive only the complete frozen manuscript, no preparation notes, conversations or previous reviews. Record all substantive objections; repairs require a new isolated round. Do not promote a candidate proof until its complete chain survives review.
