## D5-5: annealed coefficient theorem

- Statement: for each separately fixed \(H\), the terminal map equals
  \(\lim_n\mathbb E[D_n^k f_n]\), \(k=1,3,5\).
- Status: **theorem-level under the stated polynomial-smooth envelope**:
  \(\phi\in C^\infty\) and every derivative has polynomial growth.  The exact
  finite jet is one fixed NETSOR-transpose-plus program, and the cited
  all-finite-\(L^p\) tensor-program theorem supplies expectation convergence.
- Weaker tier: a separately established probability/almost-sure limit plus
  \(\sup_n\mathbb E|D_n^k f_n|^{1+\epsilon}<\infty\) gives uniform
  integrability and expectation convergence.
- Nonclaims: growing depth, positive-time convergence of the untruncated
  network trajectory, and activation assumptions limited only to \(C^5\).
