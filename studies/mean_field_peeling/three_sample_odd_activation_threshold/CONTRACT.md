# Three-input odd-mixture threshold: research contract

2026-09-08. Determine the strongest rigorously sufficient activation
coefficient theta_delta, with emphasis on small delta, for the original
three-hidden-layer raw model and all three-input datasets satisfying
||x_i||^2=d and |<x_i,x_j>/d|<=1-delta, with arbitrary binary labels.
The same activation (1-theta)z+theta arctan(z) is used at each layer.
The amplitude is selected from delta alone, independently of the
dataset, width, auxiliary cap/mesh, and physical horizon.

The target retains every conclusion and convention of
../two_sample_odd_activation_theorem/PROOF.md, extended to three samples:
global strong autonomous population flow; nonsymmetric bounded-primal
uniqueness and reached-state restart; joint full-width GF/raw-GD limits
on each fixed finite interval; original initialization and raw metric;
all full kernels, both action orientations, same-layer path/velocity
laws, moments, nonaffinity and initial-motion conclusions. Width
convergence is for each fixed dataset, not a supremum over datasets.

Singular input Grams are admissible. Neither an additional input
spectral-gap hypothesis, an activation offset/large gain, two hidden
layers, a fixed horizon, nor initialization positivity may substitute
for the target. A sharp initialization eigenvalue is not a sharp
sufficient activation threshold. Failure of a proof method is not a
counterexample to the positive-amplitude theorem.

Authorized work is theory, local artifacts, independent route search
and adversarial checking. No trajectory experiments, prior-proof edits,
commits, external publication or messages to third parties are needed.
Existing unrelated workspace changes must be preserved.

Routes: direct energy/source construction; nonlinear-reference/kernel
coercivity; sharp geometry and necessary excursions. Root restores the
source state and checks whether the proposed quantifiers can follow
from the available results. Each route must state exact proved facts,
unresolved implications, and the evidence required to reopen a stalled
route. No affirmative threshold is assumed to exist or be presently
provable.
