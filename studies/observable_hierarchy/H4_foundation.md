## Valid reuse and new obligations

The prescribed population state, initialized action and its actual adjoint are
unchanged. Finite-network identification must retain the actual independent
finite Gaussian readout and its vanishing contribution. The model uses stored
variances `(1,1/n,1/n²)`, mobilities `(n,1,n)`, unhalved mean squared loss,
and physical GF time.

H3's dictionary, positive ridge, full Gaussian source compiler and joint mark
initialization do not depend on the training horizon. Its fixed-order energy
bounds continue to every fixed finite time. The new closure comparison must
use the constructed time-40 target's reverse-field tails, preserve both action
errors and the filtered HS source, and establish the full target's membership
in the generated observable spaces.

The old H3 rotated-arc law theorem is short-time. C.4.7 gives a time-40
existence neighborhood with some constants left unevaluated, while C.4.5 and
C.4.7.7 give a separate explicit radius for the learning conclusions.
Merely choosing a very small positive number does not prove it lies inside
the unspecified existence neighborhood. H4 therefore needs an explicit
supported family, with an explicit support proof or an executable parameter
condition justified by complete estimates. This remains a new obligation.

No finite run can settle that obligation or certify the limiting risk/activity
thresholds. Exact law descriptions, joint same-population pairs, all numerical
limit orders, total resource accounting and own-state restart are part of the
new computation contract.
