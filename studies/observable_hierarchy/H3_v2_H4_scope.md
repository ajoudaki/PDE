# H4 scope remaining after the revised H3 candidate

This is a scope assessment of frozen edition v2, conditional on its scientific
acceptance. It is not a time-40 theorem or authorization to start that extension.
No long-horizon computation or finite-network training was performed.

The candidate proves population/closure/numerical agreement on the fixed
rational two-arc family through T=1/200. The implemented vector field itself
is autonomous, and a fixed resolution has no elapsed-time-dependent storage.
The fixed-order numerical comparison uses bounds depending on a finite horizon;
its structure is not tied to a particular count of steps. This explains why
reusing the numerical method for a longer horizon is plausible, but does not
extend the outer population theorem for these represented laws.

The uncompleted implication is a supported represented law family, independent
of order, on which the exact target and this compatible closure converge
through physical time 40, together with feasible declared computations over
that interval. The current direct source-tail/population construction proves
only the shorter interval. Membership of its explicit two-arc family in the
older existential time-40 neighborhood has not been proved. Existence of some
population flow through time 40 would not alone establish the approximation
claim or practical numerical operation there.

The existing complete H1/H2 dependencies and the new filter comparison provide
material for a subsequent assessment of that implication; this note does not
supply a new extension proof. Any such extension must carry both approximation
and numerical errors from initialization to the final time and retain the same
paired observations and own-state restart. It must not reset to exact population
states. A qualitative extension does not require true-error certificates,
convergence rates, automatic tolerance selection or a cost-to-tolerance bound.

Therefore H4 is not merged into this H3 package. Its remaining scientific and
computational scope should be assessed after the present candidate's reviews,
before launching any new work. The present note neither asserts impossibility
nor requires a broad experiment or a new certification campaign.
