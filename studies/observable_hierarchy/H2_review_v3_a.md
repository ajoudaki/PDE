### Well-posedness, metric, energy and own-state restart

For fixed N, each feature is a finite bounded linear combination of bounded words. On the fixed mark carriers, use `(w-g,c,M)` in `L-infinity x L-infinity x finite Euclidean space`. The fixed unbounded g causes no Lipschitz difficulty: differences of tanh and its first derivative are bounded by the differences of the current row, and all feature evaluations are bounded by their finite envelopes. The RHS is locally Lipschitz on bounded sets, so its integral map is a contraction on a sufficiently short path ball. The proof supplies both ball preservation and contraction rather than using a bare formal ODE assertion on L2.

I re-derived the M gradient. A variation gives `delta z2=b2^T delta M a`, hence `delta f=d^T delta M a`; therefore the unhalved loss gradient is `2 integral r d a^T`. Back-propagating the same scalar variation to w gives `q=b1^T M^T d` with the lower gate, and variation in c gives h2. These are exactly H2.5. The M metric is ordinary Euclidean coefficient metric, not the Hilbert–Schmidt metric of its filtered represented operator. Accordingly the dissipated norm is

`L_N' = -E1|w_N'|^2 - ||M'||_F^2 - E2|c_N'|^2`.

This distinction is essential and correctly stated. From the energy bound `integral |r|<=Y`, the readout bound is `||c||_infinity<=2Yt`. Contraction of U gives `||a||<=1`, `||d||<=2Yt`, and hence `||M'||_F<=4Y^2 t`. Integration gives `||M-D||_F<=2Y^2 t^2`. The row L2 speed is at most `4Y^2 t(2+2Y^2t^2)`, yielding the displayed row bound. The finite feature envelope converts this to a bound on `||w-g||_infinity` for each fixed N. These bounds prevent finite-time escape in the actual local-existence norms. They do not incorrectly infer L-infinity control from a raw L2 bound alone.

At a reached state, the saved joint laws retain every conditional dependence needed by the drift. In particular, g need not reconstruct w from its initial value; w itself is saved jointly with the marks. The row drift is Lipschitz in its current individual w coordinate, and the individual readout drift is independent of its c coordinate. Characteristic uniqueness therefore identifies continuation from the saved laws with the original restriction. Fixed domains and fixed matrices suffice; neither source programs nor elapsed time nor earlier population snapshots are required.

### Numerical limitations and reusable API

The producer computes source covariance extensions with a rectangular source factor and reduced QR solve. It retains all positive innovations, however small; exactly zero innovations retain a named derivative slot without a new independent Gaussian coordinate. A small negative Schur correction is disclosed in diagnostics; an unresolved range or larger negative value is rejected. Ridge normalization keeps every dictionary column and every computed positive regularized mode, uses the prescribed ridge, and rejects unresolved positivity or ridge underflow rather than changing the mathematical order.

This is floating quadrature, not the exact Gaussian integral theorem. Source factors, covariance corrections and normalizations are not interval certificates. Default order five is explicitly coarse. The numerical D norm is reported without forcing the exact bound by rescaling. Source, tensor-dimension, node and word-prefix limits are explicit, and partial failed compiler objects are not represented as valid complete initializations. The readout gate `1-tanh^2` and all contractions use ordinary float64; the text disclaims an extreme-range or certified error guarantee. The finite weighted data-law API and rational word scalings are correctly declared narrower than the mathematical law and real-mark observation interfaces.

`fields`, `rhs`, `apply_action`, `observe`, `joint_observe`, and save/load expose a compact autonomous interface. Current evolution never depends on the returned GaussianProgram. The fixed D and g suffice for frozen observations; current M and complete joint populations suffice for current actions. Archives use no pickle, enforce the exact schema, preserve probabilities and data, and write their reserved format tag without requiring private caller metadata. The assembled package imports and runs independently of study files.
