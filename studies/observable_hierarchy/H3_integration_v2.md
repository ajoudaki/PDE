neither a half-loss clock nor feature-time substitution enters the executable.

two-atom reference these six coordinates reduce to the stored `b,beta` with
the stated signs. The extra stored initialization contractions and kernel
coefficients are counted as fixed numerical inputs. No growing neuron matrix,

RMS estimates include nonzero Duhamel motion and their additional spatial
remainders. There is no claim that a frozen readout alone establishes hidden
learning. The completed-action formula is justified through saturation and
the norm-two action/square-root limit, not by silently admitting arbitrary
unbounded programs.
