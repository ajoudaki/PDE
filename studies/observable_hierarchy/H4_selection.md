## Exact selected scope and assumptions

Use the bias-free two-hidden-layer tanh model, normalized direction
u=x/sqrt(2), independent stored Gaussian variances (1,1/n,1/n^2), mobilities
(n,1,n), residual f-y, unhalved squared loss, and physical GF. The population
readout starts at zero as the limit of the actual finite random readout.
Retain one initialized Gaussian action and its actual adjoint; only the
learned increment is Hilbert–Schmidt. Preserve the entire H3 dictionary,
including the exhaustive bounded-word prefix, its ordering, and
eta_N=1/[1024(N+1)^2]. The polynomial core alone is not the proved dense
hierarchy.

The smallest common family suited to the proposed executable interface is
the equal-mass endpoint family described by H4_laws.py. Fix one positive
represented radius rho before order, precision, quadrature, or accuracy is
selected. With U(s)=((1-s^2)/(1+s^2),2s/(1+s^2)) and J the quarter-turn,
take one half of Law(sqrt(2) U(rho S),+1) and one half of
Law(sqrt(2) J U(rho V),-1), where S and V are uniform on rational intervals
[a,b] and [c,d] inside [-1,1], with degenerate intervals interpreted as
atoms. The final exact radius must be proved no larger than the radius r
of H4_family_explicit_cap.md (E30). Then |U(rho s)-e1| and
|J U(rho s)-e2| are at most 2rho, which is the stated supported-domain
condition.

This family contains nonorthogonal atomic members and nonatomic arc members.
Its mathematical nonorthogonality can be extraordinarily small. It has
equal binary-label masses and a strong support restriction; it is not an
open neighborhood among all bounded-label laws, a useful-size correlation
regime, or the original H3 ArcLaw family with its 3/5,4/5 rotation and
variable mixture weight. Preserve H3's original short-time guarantee.

H4_family_explicit_cap.md §7 also describes atom/arc mixtures with two
parameters. These are not literally the same family as H4_laws.py's two
uniform intervals. The broader supported-domain proposition can support
either, after radius compatibility is proved. For the smallest maintained
addition, select the executable endpoint family explicitly rather than
maintaining two unnecessary public families or calling their descriptions
identical.

State convergence for every separately fixed selected law; do not upgrade
it to uniformity over all laws. State the iterated order explicitly:
arithmetic precision, time mesh, input quadrature, population cubature,
initializer cubature, generic source regularization, then closure order.
Resource allowances may increase between refinements. No arbitrary diagonal,
simultaneous closure-order/width theorem, time-40 raw-GD theorem, all-time
flow, finite-run trajectory certificate, tolerance selector, or
cost-to-accuracy bound is selected.

The inherited activity threshold concerns time 1/200. It does not establish
activity at 40, persistent motion throughout the interval, feature-learning
superiority, generalization, or endpoint selection. Risk convergence and an
inherited target risk threshold do not certify that a particular finite run
has reached that threshold.

## Non-vacuity and practical-computation boundary

The proposed horizon proof keeps the exact trajectory only as a comparison
object in vanishing-source estimates. It is not supplied to initialization,
the step rule, or restart. Features and their Gaussian joint initialization
are fixed before training; both orientations are finite contractions using
one matrix and its transpose. This is the appropriate information contract
for selection, subject to complete scientific checking.

At exact closure order the two joint populations remain continuum laws.
Finite storage comes only after their specified numerical discretization.
Retained dimensions and node counts may grow with the chosen resolution,
but not with elapsed steps. The final cost statement must count exact law
metadata, arithmetic bits, initialization, temporary workspace, observations,
and checkpoint storage as well as the moving state.

H4_laws.py preserves a positive symbolic dyadic radius and explicitly records
both deliberate radius replacement and collapse of a nonreference midpoint
rule after arithmetic rounding. Its tests include eventual resolution at
increased precision for a manageable fixed exponent. These are useful
representation semantics, not tests of the final time-40 radius or
trajectory convergence. The source expressly disclaims theorem verification,
and caller-supplied scope tags are not proofs of domain membership.

Feasible runs whose working inputs have collapsed to the reference may show
that the fixed-memory solver operates through 40 at those declared numerical
resolutions. They do not show practical resolution of the family's nonzero
angles or arc width. Retain that limitation next to the operational results.
The tiny fixed exact radius is not itself a reason to reject the qualitative
theorem; silently presenting collapsed runs as numerically resolved
nonorthogonal/nonatomic dynamics would exceed the selected scope. No new
experiment to resolve astronomically small perturbations is requested here.
