## Distinct value and duplication

The candidate adds a substantive, nonvacuous result at the stated C-H1 level:
an explicit finite instruction alphabet, finitely many marked joint-law types
at each order, and a finite reverse compiler that expresses each order-n
characteristic-function derivative using order
`N = 10^6(n+1)^6`, current contractions, one training-law integral, and the
specified `R -> infinity` limit inside that fixed higher order. The proof
uses only bounded coordinate multipliers, bounded actions on L2, and quadratic
joint statistics. It does not require an unrestricted same-space product
algebra or moments of every order for arbitrary action outputs.

This is useful because it turns an implicitly generated observable state into
a typed hierarchy with a concrete upward dependency rule. In particular,
reverse differentiation can generate an unbounded L2 covector as an input to
another action. The saturation compilation in candidate Section 4 gives a
specified way to read that action using the admitted bounded-query alphabet,
with level independent of the cutoff. Merely invoking a chain rule for the
original operator state would not specify that interface. The construction is
still useful if no convergent finite closure is subsequently found: it states
exactly what current information the derivative needs and where a closure
would have to control missing information.

The current-state sufficiency conclusion has substantial established overlap.
`global_nonlinear.md` C.4.2, especially its final subsection, already says that
equal current generated joint laws intertwine the equation and determine
future laws. It also supplies the generated-space Euler invariance argument,
comparison against the existing tail-bearing solution, and restart without
response history. C.4.7.1–5 already provide the long-interval nonlinear flow,
reached uniqueness, law continuity, passive-query tails, and fixed finite
observation-program interpretation in W2. The candidate must not describe
these foundations as newly established by C-H1.

Candidate Sections 5–6 give a useful explicit realization of that existing
sufficiency mechanism for this particular alphabet: characteristic tests are
dense, equality of joint laws gives coordinate-preserving isometries, the two
observable spaces reduce the action, and arbitrary matching bounded-action
realizations obtain a transported continuation whose complement cannot feed
the observations. The more explicit matching-realization statement and its
proof support the new dictionary. They do not independently justify presenting
the whole addition as a new population existence or restart theorem.

The Gaussian initialization rule is a specialization of the existing finite
Gaussian-program construction, not a new Gaussian action theorem. Section 8's
positive hidden motion and nonaffinity argument supplies a qualitative witness
for the hierarchy's fixed law family and interval; it should be retained as a
supporting lemma, not advertised as a separate architectural advantage.

## Useful scope, assumptions, and information audit

The useful scope is the actual two-hidden-layer tanh model with the prescribed
small finite Gaussian readout, unhalved mean-square physical GF, block
mobilities `(n,1,n)`, both orientations of the same middle action, bounded labels,
and a fixed open Wasserstein neighborhood of the opposite-label reference.
The neighborhood admits nonorthogonal and nonatomic laws. The declared
positive interval and neighborhood do not shrink with hierarchy order.
The existing `[0,40]` results supply identities and continuation bounds; the
candidate's C-H1 contract uses `[0,1/200]` and an existential common positive
time within it for paired motion and nonaffinity.

These restrictions preserve the intended nonlinear hidden-learning mechanism.
The positive radius and activity constants need not have practical numerical
size. The result does not establish broader depth, arbitrary-law global
existence, a new actual-GD limit, useful risk improvement, or computational
efficiency.

The hierarchy is nonvacuous under the book's explicit separation of C-H1 from
C-H2 and C-H3:

- Each type has finitely many coordinates and finitely many real/input marks.
  Marks do not accept an arbitrary vector, operator, function, or encoded
  parameter distribution. More simultaneous query arguments require more nodes.
- Same-population finite unions retain the required joint correlations.
  Separate marginal populations would not support the contraction formulas or
  the isometry argument. No artificial cross-layer neuron pairing is used.
- Gaussian initialization is prescribed from the original model. Learned
  coefficients in equation (8) are recomputed from the current hierarchy and
  the fixed training law. No future trajectory is supplied.
- Finite order counts law types, coordinate dimensions, and syntax. A law and
  its continuum mark dependence remain infinite information objects. That is
  expressly admitted at C-H1 and does not establish finite scalar storage.
- The `R -> infinity` operation is an exact information interface at one
  higher fixed order. Its absence of a computable uniform error bound is a
  genuine unresolved numerical obligation. It must remain explicit in every
  summary of the evolution statement.
- Complete-hierarchy sufficiency is for realizable states matching a reached
  canonical state, under the continuation law specified in the theorem. It
  does not prove well-posedness of arbitrary formal characteristic-function
  sequences or unrestricted switching of training laws.

The obstruction results in `gaussian_calculus.md` Sections 6 and 10 do not
invalidate this scoped claim. Section 6 excludes the specified unrestricted
same-norm algebra and jet-shift bounds; the candidate instead excludes
unbounded coordinate products and uses bounded saturation with L2 limits.
Section 10 treats a different quadratic model with order-one readout and a
specified positive Taylor family. C-H1 is neither that family nor an assertion
that its temporal Taylor series converges. These distinctions leave C-H2 open;
they are not evidence for convergence of any proposed closure.
