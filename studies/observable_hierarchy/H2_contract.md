## Fixed target

Construct one increasing family of finite autonomous current-population systems
converging to the canonical bias-free two-hidden-layer tanh population physical
GF. Stored finite Gaussian variances are `(1,1/n,1/n^2)`, mobilities `(n,1,n)`,
residual `f-y`, and loss is unhalved mean square. Preserve the actual finite
random readout whenever using a finite-network theorem and both orientations
of the same reused middle Gaussian action.

For each fixed `Y>=1`, choose one fixed positive radius within C-H1's supported
law neighborhood, with normalized-input-plus-label transport metric on
`sqrt(2) S^1 × [-Y,Y]`, and one fixed `0<T<=1/200`. Neither shrinks with order.
The family includes nonorthogonal and nonatomic laws; the interval contains a
common positive time at which paired displacements of both hidden activations
are nonzero. Prefer the already certified C-H1 family and interval if feasible.

At each order count every retained joint law, auxiliary coordinate, coefficient,
field type and finite-dimensional mark domain. State size is independent of
elapsed time, update count and the numerical time mesh. Initialize using the
canonical model, training law and declared fixed information only. Specify a
closed autonomous vector field, observation maps, well-posedness through T and
restart from its own current reached state.

Finite population fields and explicitly defined integrals are allowed. No
operational omitted-level query, unresolved cutoff limit, arbitrary-vector
Gaussian action oracle, exact trajectory query, representative dense network,
full parameter-space density, stored history, preallocated replay or fitted
surrogate is admissible. Finite observable coefficient/contraction matrices
require explicit mathematical roles and provenance; renaming a raw trained
middle matrix is insufficient.

For every separately fixed admitted law prove uniform-in-time and whole-circle
prediction convergence, and uniform-in-time W2 convergence of every separately
fixed admissible finite C-H1 joint tuple, including paired initial/current
hidden observations, both action directions and quadratic contractions. One
common construction across the law family is required; a uniform rate is not.
Identify the limit with actual canonical nonlinear GF. Static realizability or
uniqueness only for matching reached hierarchies does not identify an arbitrary
formal hierarchy solution.

No temporal analyticity, moment determinacy, positive Stieltjes representation,
or unrestricted same-norm multiplication/derivative estimate is assumed.
Regularization/cutoff schedules must use admissible information. Conditional
small-tail estimates and correct finite coefficients do not complete C-H2.
