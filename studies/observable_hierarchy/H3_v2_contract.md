## Target and claim ladder

The target is bias-free two-hidden-layer tanh population physical GF, with
stored Gaussian variances (1,1/n,1/n²), mobilities (n,1,n), residual f-y and
unhalved squared loss. Both orientations are the same reused Gaussian action.
Finite-network identifications retain the actual finite random readout.
The fixed horizon is T=1/200. A fixed executable represented law family must
include nonorthogonal and nonatomic members. Its supported scope will be
proved, not inferred from an unspecified positive neighborhood radius.

Required separate claims are: finite construction and own-state restart;
numerical consistency at separately fixed closure order; closure convergence
to the identical GF; paired internal-observation convergence; and practical
operation at declared resolutions. All are currently open in this revision.
The probability mode and the order of every initialization, integration,
time, arithmetic and closure limit will be stated explicitly. An iterated
limit is allowed; an arbitrary diagonal is not assumed.

The numerical state must retain joint frozen/current marks, both action
directions, whole-circle prediction, and training-averaged initial/current
activation pairs and RMS motions for each layer. State and workspace at a
fixed resolution must be bounded independently of elapsed steps. No raw
neuron middle matrix, fitted trajectory, growing history or operational
arbitrary-action oracle is allowed. No error rate, finite-run certificate,
automatic tolerance rule or old numerical signal threshold is required.
