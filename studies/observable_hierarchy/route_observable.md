# Current observable algebra: reconstruction theorem and closure obstruction

Status: independent scoped route, frozen 2026-09-12. This is a partial route, not a proof of C-H1. Scientific inputs were the supervisor's prompt only. No other study, route, book, history, or chat was read. Required mathematical and conjecture-investigation skills and the three specified process references were read. No numerical experiment or Git mutation was performed.

## 1. Contract and conclusion

Use the prompt notation: \(Q=(Q_1,Q_2)\) is the first row, \(W:H_1\to H_2\) the middle operator, and \(A\in H_2\) the readout. Thus the notation map to the repository's customary \(w,A,c\) is \(Q\leftrightarrow w\), \(W\leftrightarrow A\), \(A\leftrightarrow c\). Both \(H_i=L^2(\Omega_i,\mathbb P_i)\) are real probability-space Hilbert spaces. The actual Hilbert adjoint is used throughout.

The target is an exact current-observable hierarchy, with finitely many coordinate templates and input replicas at every order, a finite higher-order dependency for each evolution equation, and unconditional predictive sufficiency on the canonical reached states. No elapsed history, future trajectory, raw operator coordinate, full parameter-distribution coordinate, moment-determinacy assumption, or temporal-analyticity argument is permitted.

**What this route establishes:** a fixed finite-alphabet hierarchy of bounded current observable populations determines, at all orders, the two probability algebras generated by the observables and the action of \(W,W^*\) on them, up to compatible probability-algebra isometries. These are precisely closed subspaces on which the current network and its current vector field live. This is a static state-reconstruction theorem, stronger than determination of a tangent kernel alone.

**What remains open:** under the stated \(L^2\)/bounded-operator hypotheses, I do not obtain an admissible finite higher-order dependency for evolution of every population in that hierarchy. Adding unbounded response words introduces a real integrability obstruction. Continuous auxiliary probe marks give formal repairs but risk storing the entire relevant operator action at one finite level. Future equivalence additionally needs uniqueness/equivariance of the canonical continuation, or another invariance argument; existence of strong trajectories by itself does not supply that assertion.

These limitations concern this witness/proof route. The example below is not asserted to be a canonical reached state and does not disprove C-H1.

## 2. Exact low-order causal skeleton

Write
\[
v(x)=x/\sqrt2,\qquad c(x,x')=v(x)\cdot v(x'),\qquad r(x,y)=f(x)-y,
\]
\[
s_1(x)=1-h_1(x)^2,\qquad s_2(x)=1-h_2(x)^2,\qquad
b_1(x)=W^*\delta_2(x).
\]
Thus \(\delta_1(x)=s_1(x)b_1(x)\). The fields \(h_i,s_i\) are bounded, \(\delta_2=A s_2\) is bounded on the assumed readout ball, and \(b_1,\delta_1\in H_1\). Products and inner products in the following first-order identities are therefore well defined.

For the prediction, differentiation in the three parameter directions gives
\[
\dot f(x)=-2\int r(x',y')K(x,x')\,d\nu(x',y'),
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
K(x,x')={}&\langle h_2(x),h_2(x')\rangle_2\\
&+\langle\delta_2(x),\delta_2(x')\rangle_2
  \langle h_1(x),h_1(x')\rangle_1\\
&+c(x,x')\langle s_1(x)b_1(x),s_1(x')b_1(x')\rangle_1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, the readout direction contributes the first term. The rank-one middle-layer direction contributes the product of the two displayed inner products. The row direction contributes \(c(x,x')\) times the inner product of the two row sensitivities. This retains nonlinear motion of both hidden layers; no frozen-feature replacement has been made.

The first hidden output obeys
\[
\dot h_1(x)=-2\int r(x',y')c(x,x')s_1(x)s_1(x')b_1(x')\,d\nu.
\]
Define the response field
\[
C(x,x')=W\big[s_1(x)s_1(x')b_1(x')\big]\in H_2.
\]
The operand belongs to \(H_1\) because its multipliers are bounded. Then
\[
\dot z_2(x)=-2\int r(x',y')\left[
\delta_2(x')\langle h_1(x'),h_1(x)\rangle_1+c(x,x')C(x,x')
\right]d\nu.
\]
Also
\[
\dot\delta_2(x)=s_2(x)\dot A+A\,s_2'(z_2(x))\dot z_2(x),
\qquad s_2'(z)=-2\tanh(z)\operatorname{sech}^2(z),
\]
and
\[
\dot b_1(x)=\dot W^*\delta_2(x)+W^*\dot\delta_2(x).
\]
These identities are legitimate strong \(L^2\) identities along the stipulated strong trajectories when the parameter derivatives in the prompt exist. Bounded first derivatives of the scalar nonlinearities, bounded \(A\), and bounded operators justify the chain rules along such curves by dominated convergence. The rank-one increment of \(W\) is bounded in operator norm by its trace norm, so the supplied trace-class evolution also provides the operator derivative used here.

This displays the first new dependency explicitly. A law containing \(h_1,b_1,h_2,\delta_2\) determines \(K\), but its evolution asks for \(C\) and adjoint actions on bounded multiples of \(C\). Merely naming these new fields does not prove an indefinitely iterable hierarchy.

## 3. A finite-alphabet bounded-word hierarchy

Fix a readout bound \(R\ge1\). Define primitive bounded observable fields
\[
\mathcal B_{1,0}=\{1,h_1(x)\},\qquad
\mathcal B_{2,0}=\{1,h_2(x),d(x),o(x)\},
\]
where \(d(x)=\delta_2(x)/R\) and \(o(x)=A h_2(x)/R\). The last two are current response and output-contribution populations. They are not a distribution of all model parameters.

Use a finite typed alphabet to form further bounded fields:

1. the two constants \(0,1\) on either population;
2. \(b\mapsto-b\), \((b,c)\mapsto(b+c)/2\), and \((b,c)\mapsto bc\), within one population;
3. \(b\mapsto\tanh(Wb)\), from population 1 to population 2;
4. \(b\mapsto\tanh(W^*b)\), from population 2 to population 1.

Every resulting field has absolute value at most one. An action symbol here defines an observable of the current model; it is not an instruction allowing the finite-state evolution to query \(W\). No action symbol has a free arbitrary function as its operand: its operand is another finite word from this fixed alphabet.

Give a word its tree size, counting both operation and primitive nodes. Let level \(n\) contain all typed words of tree size at most \(n\), with at most \(n\) input slots, and every joint law of at most \(n\) such word evaluations on the same population. Input slots may be filled by arbitrary circle inputs and repeated. This includes cross-input joints and shared-neuron correlations. It does not replace them by independently sampled marginal coordinates.

For an explicit finite bound, the alphabet can be encoded using fewer than 16 node labels, arity at most two, and at most \(n\) input-slot labels. The number of ordered tree encodings of size at most \(n\) is at most \((64(n+1))^{n+1}\); hence the number of joint templates at level \(n\) is at most
\[
N_n=\big(1+(64(n+1))^{n+1}\big)^{n+1}.
\]
The bound is intentionally crude. Its role is to make finiteness explicit. No auxiliary continuous scalar probe marks occur in this hierarchy. The only continuous labels are the original circle inputs. The population laws are determined by bounded continuous tests on the resulting finite-dimensional cubes.

This construction uses no all-moment assumption. In particular, its laws distinguish distributions even when unbounded observables do not have higher moments.

The finite words are well defined: every operand of \(W\) or \(W^*\) is bounded and therefore belongs to \(L^2\); the outer hyperbolic tangent returns a bounded field. Each fixed word is strongly continuous in its input labels, by induction using boundedness of the operators and the Lipschitz property of hyperbolic tangent. Multiplication of two uniformly bounded strongly convergent \(L^2\) fields preserves strong convergence. The primitive fields have the same continuity. Consequently it suffices, for the reconstruction proof, to use a fixed countable dense set of circle inputs containing \(\sqrt2e_1,\sqrt2e_2\); arbitrary inputs are recovered by continuity.

## 4. Static reconstruction theorem

Consider two legitimate current parameter states, possibly represented on different population probability spaces. Suppose all same-population laws in the hierarchy above agree, for every finite order and all input labels. For each state let \(\Sigma_i\) be the completed sigma algebra generated by all its bounded words, and set
\[
K_i=L^2(\Sigma_i)\subseteq H_i.
\]
Then there are surjective linear isometries \(U_i:K_i\to\widetilde K_i\) with the following properties:

* They map each named observable word to its counterpart, preserve constants, and preserve bounded measurable functional calculus and products whenever defined.
* They recover and map the row and readout: \(U_1Q_j=\widetilde Q_j\), \(U_2A=\widetilde A\).
* \(W K_1\subseteq K_2\), \(W^*K_2\subseteq K_1\), and
  \[
  U_2 W|_{K_1}=\widetilde W U_1,\qquad
  U_1 W^*|_{K_2}=\widetilde W^*U_2.
  \]
* The current network observables and every term of its current parameter vector field are carried into their counterparts. In particular the current \(f\), risk, and \(\dot f\) agree.

### Proof

First take a bounded cylinder function \(F(b_1,\ldots,b_m)\) of finitely many word fields on one population. Send it to the same function of the counterpart words. Equality of the finite joint laws gives equality of its \(L^2\) norm and all pairwise inner products. If two cylinder representations agree almost surely in the original state, the squared norm of their difference vanishes and the corresponding difference vanishes in the second state as well. The map is therefore well defined and isometric on bounded cylinder functions.

Such functions are dense in \(L^2(\Sigma_i)\). One direct justification is that the bounded functions obtainable by \(L^2\) limits of cylinder functions contain indicators of the finite-coordinate cylinder sets, are closed under bounded monotone limits, and therefore contain indicators of their generated sigma algebra. Approximation of any \(L^2\) function by bounded simple functions finishes the argument. The isometry extends to \(U_i\), and its image is all of \(\widetilde K_i\) by the same density argument. Preservation of bounded measurable functional calculus follows first for cylinders and then by almost-sure subsequences and bounded convergence. Preservation for integrable unbounded functions follows by truncation whenever the relevant norm is finite.

The first-row coordinates are recovered from current forward observables:
\[
Q_j=\operatorname{arctanh}\big(h_1(\sqrt2e_j)\big)\quad\text{a.s.}
\]
The inverse has finite values almost surely because \(Q_j\) is finite almost surely, and belongs to \(L^2\) by the hypothesis \(Q_j\in H_1\). Bounded truncations justify the equality under \(U_1\). For any fixed input \(x_0\), the readout is recovered by the bounded identity
\[
A=R\left[d(x_0)+o(x_0)h_2(x_0)\right],
\]
because \(\operatorname{sech}^2 z+\tanh^2z=1\). Thus \(Q_j\in K_1\), \(A\in K_2\), and both map as claimed.

For every population-1 bounded word \(b\), its image word \(\tanh(Wb)\) lies in \(\Sigma_2\). Applying truncated inverse hyperbolic tangents and passing in \(L^2\) shows \(Wb\in K_2\) and
\[
U_2 Wb=\widetilde W U_1b.
\]
The real linear span of the bounded words is dense in \(K_1\). To check this rather than assume it, note that it is a unital algebra: multiplication is an allowed operation, and scalar linear combinations are allowed in its real span. It separates all finite word-coordinate vectors. On the compact cube of finitely many coordinates, polynomials approximate continuous functions, and bounded continuous functions approximate indicators in \(L^2\) of any finite Borel probability law. Equivalently, the bounded monotone-class argument applied to this algebra gives the same density conclusion. Together with the cylinder density above, this establishes the claim.

The boundedness of \(W,\widetilde W\) and the isometry of \(U_i\) now extend the intertwining identity from the word span to all of \(K_1\), and show \(WK_1\subseteq K_2\). Repeating the same argument with \(W^*\) gives the adjoint identity and \(W^*K_2\subseteq K_1\).

The network formulas use only these operator restrictions, constants, bounded functional calculus, multiplication by bounded fields, and the recovered \(Q,A\). All are preserved by the isometries. Population inner products are preserved, hence so are scalar residuals and the integrals against the fixed data law. The rank-one operator direction is preserved because for \(u\in K_1,v\in K_2\),
\[
U_2(v\otimes u)U_1^{-1}=(U_2v)\otimes(U_1u).
\]
This proves the current-vector-field assertion. No temporal derivative series has been used. \(\square\)

### Irrelevant complement and the continuation qualification

The two invariances imply a block decomposition. For \(u\in K_1\), \(v\in K_2^\perp\),
\(<v,Wu>=0\). For \(u\in K_1^\perp\), \(v\in K_2\),
\(<v,Wu>=<W^*v,u>=0\). Thus the off-diagonal blocks vanish:
\[
W=W_K\oplus W_\perp.
\]
At the current state, all rank-one update factors belong to the \(K\) blocks and the current \(Q,A\) belong to them. The restricted equations are the same nonlinear equations on the two generated probability algebras.

If the canonical strong continuation is unique in a class stable under these probability-algebra isometries, the restricted solution lifts to a full solution with fixed \(W_\perp\). Uniqueness then forces the canonical continuation to be this lifted solution. Applying \(U_i\) to the restricted solution produces the counterpart restricted solution, and uniqueness gives equality of all future predictions on their common existence horizon. This supplies predictive sufficiency without analyticity or moment determinacy.

Existence alone does not justify the uniqueness step or prevent a nonunique trajectory from departing an invariant subspace. The prompt states existence and legitimate strong trajectories but does not explicitly supply the needed uniqueness/equivariance theorem. I have therefore recorded this implication conditionally rather than treating it as unconditional C-H1 predictive sufficiency.

## 5. Why finite evolution is not yet proved

For a bounded-word field \(b\), the formal derivative of a forward action word has the legitimate first-order form
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\tanh(Wb)
=\operatorname{sech}^2(Wb)\,[\dot Wb+W\dot b].
\]
The derivative \(\dot b\) is generally an unbounded \(L^2\) field. It is not an operand from the bounded-word grammar. Its truncations can be approximated using higher and higher words, but that gives an all-orders limit, not a fixed finite higher-level dependency.

Enlarging the grammar to admit every \(W(bu)\), with \(b\) bounded and \(u\in L^2\) a response field, does not solve the problem. Differentiation yields \(W(\dot b\,u+b\dot u)\). The first product may only belong to \(L^1\). A bounded operator on \(L^2\) has no stipulated action on such an operand. Saturating the output of this action does not define the missing action on the input.

This is an obstruction to a proposed proof step, not merely concern about convergence of moments. It arises before one has a well-defined indefinitely iterable evolution hierarchy.

### Explicit Gaussian-coordinate integrability example

Let \(\Omega_1=\mathbb R^2\) with standard Gaussian measure and write \(q=Q_1\), \(Q_2\) for the independent second Gaussian coordinate. Let
\[
u(q)=e^{q^2/5}\mathbf1_{\{q>0\}}.
\]
Then \(u\in L^2\), since the exponent in its squared Gaussian-density integral is \((2/5-1/2)q^2=-q^2/10\). But \(u^2\notin L^2\), since the corresponding exponent is \((4/5-1/2)q^2=3q^2/10\).

For \(s(q)=\operatorname{sech}^2q\), consider the legitimate \(L^2\) perturbation \(q_\epsilon=q+\epsilon s(q)u(q)\) and the \(L^2\)-valued response
\[
R_\epsilon=s(q_\epsilon)u(q).
\]
It is continuous in \(L^2\) by bounded convergence dominated by a multiple of \(u^2\). Its pointwise derivative at zero is
\[
\left.\partial_\epsilon R_\epsilon\right|_0=s'(q)s(q)u(q)^2.
\]
This is not in \(L^2\). On the positive tail, \(|s'(q)s(q)|\) is comparable to \(e^{-4q}\), so its squared Gaussian-density integral has exponent \(3q^2/10-8q\), and diverges. If a strong \(L^2\) derivative existed, an almost-sure subsequence of the \(L^2\)-convergent difference quotients would identify it with this pointwise derivative, a contradiction.

Let \(H_2=L^2(\Omega_2)\) be any probability-space Hilbert space and define the bounded rank-one operator
\[
Wv=\langle u,v\rangle_1\,1,\qquad W^*1=u.
\]
Applying the formal response derivative as an input to this \(W\) would require the integral of \(s'(q)s(q)u(q)^3\). Its positive-tail absolute-value integral has exponent \((3/5-1/2)q^2-4q=q^2/10-4q\), so it diverges as well. Thus even the informal scalar extension is unavailable in this example.

The example has a Gaussian first row and a bounded middle operator. It demonstrates that those two facts alone do not repair the response hierarchy. Its middle operator is **not** the stipulated reused-Gaussian initialization, and it is **not claimed** to be a canonical reached operator. To rule it out for the canonical program one needs a proved reachable-state regularity estimate, not the generic operator-ball bound.

## 6. Continuous probe marks: precise possible repair and precise risk

One can formally introduce a soft truncation
\[
T_\alpha(u)=\frac{\tanh(\alpha u)}{\alpha},\qquad\alpha\in(0,1].
\]
For each fixed \(\alpha>0\), the operand \(bT_\alpha(u)\) is bounded if \(b\) is bounded. For \(u\in L^2\),
\[
bT_\alpha(u)\longrightarrow bu\quad\text{in }L^2\quad(\alpha\downarrow0),
\]
because \(|T_\alpha(u)|\le|u|\), pointwise convergence holds, and dominated convergence applies. Hence
\[
W(bu)=L^2\!\lim_{\alpha\downarrow0}W[bT_\alpha(u)].
\]
This can package a response action as a boundary limit of a family of bounded current observables with a one-dimensional continuous mark. Directional derivatives can similarly be encoded by small marked perturbations of current fields. These are spatial/current-state probe limits, not all-order temporal jets.

The exact marked domain would be a finite product of \(\(0,1]\) scale parameters and compact directional parameters, one per tree node. At a fixed tree size this has finitely many real coordinates. If all values of these marked families count as a single finite observable type, first-variation expressions can be encoded at bounded tree depth. However, this is not automatically an admissible finite state: the entire function of the marks may contain unrestricted operator information.

The risk already appears at a Gaussian first row. The families
\[
\tanh(\alpha\,v\cdot Q),\qquad
\tanh(\alpha\,v\cdot Q)\tanh(\beta\,w\cdot Q)
\]
with varying scales and directions are much richer than a fixed-scale finite-word family. Their closed linear span contains all Gaussian polynomials, hence is dense in \(L^2(\sigma(Q))\): derivatives at zero in the scale parameters recover odd powers of linear forms, polarization recovers odd homogeneous polynomials, and products recover the even polynomials as well. These derivatives are legitimate in Gaussian \(L^2\) because every polynomial moment is finite and scalar hyperbolic-tangent derivatives are bounded. The nonzero odd coefficients of hyperbolic tangent can also be checked inductively from \(\tanh'=1-\tanh^2\): their signs alternate, and the recurrence expresses each new absolute coefficient as a strictly positive sum of products of earlier ones. Gaussian polynomial density follows, for example, by noting that an \(L^2\) function orthogonal to all polynomials has zero Gaussian-weighted exponential transform; its entire extension has all Taylor coefficients zero, hence its Fourier transform is zero and the function vanishes almost surely.

Thus a finite-depth marked type containing \(W\) applied to all those probes can determine \(W\) on the entire \(Q\)-measurable population space. When that is the relevant population space, the proposed finite coordinate has packaged the relevant raw operator action. The full marked-family construction needs an explicit non-vacuity decision before it can be called a solution. Counting the dimension of its mark domain alone is insufficient.

I do not adopt this repair as a proved C-H1 witness. A discrete finite set of scale marks at each level avoids that packaging concern but returns to an unbounded-order limit in the exact evolution formula.

## 7. Canonical initialization and claim ledger

The hierarchy uses the actual \(W\) and its actual adjoint only in the definition of current observable words. It makes no iid-kernel substitution. At canonical time zero, \(Q\) has the stated Gaussian row law, \(A=0\), and therefore \(d=o=0\). Higher action-word laws must be initialized from the finite-dimensional action laws of the reused-Gaussian construction. Those laws were not specified in the scoped prompt, so no additional initialization formula is invented here.

The route should be kept as a reconstruction lemma and marked **blocked on finite evolution**, rather than accepted as a complete candidate proof. The most useful reopening condition is a proved canonical reachable-state class closed under the necessary bounded-multiplier response actions with sufficient integrability, together with an explicit finite template recursion. A separately supplied uniqueness theorem would resolve the continuation qualification, but would not repair the finite-evolution gap.
