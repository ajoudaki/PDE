The exact three velocity blocks are
`-2 integral r (phi'(w.u) A* [c phi'(Z2)] u,

`b'_a=-2 p_a(fF_a-y_a)`; at the reference this reduces to

finite-gradient and Hilbert-adjoint construction.

For `D_ab=H_b s_a`, differentiation along the initial forward calls gives
`E partial_(z_b)D_ab=E s_a s_b` and
`E partial_(z_a)D_ab=E H_b phi''(z_a)`, with both paths counted when indices

through PSD square roots. No Gram inverse is used by the executed reference.

For S5, the bounded word `l_u l_a R tanh(P_ab/R)` has exactly the claimed
reverse-source derivative. Its L2 limit follows from `|R tanh(P/R)|<=|P|`;
