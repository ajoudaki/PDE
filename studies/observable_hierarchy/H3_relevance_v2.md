the actual finite numerical certificate. Merge overlapping preliminary and

do not maintain historical bounds as competing headline results. Reference
the already established Gaussian/action and energy proofs instead of copying

retaining the full C-H3 open requirements.

observable-closure material is suitable. It should expose initialization,
saved-state continuation and whole-circle observation separately from the
reproduction driver, following the existing fixed-certificate pattern. Standard
library Decimal/Fraction arithmetic and streamed one-dimensional integrals
make the maintenance burden reasonable for this restricted scope. The existing
H2 prototype remains a distinct approximation.

Standalone validation should establish ordinary usability of that concrete
edition: package-local imports and canonical documentation references,
documented precision and supported state provenance, examples using both
rational directions and represented coordinate boxes, saved-state reuse, and
the maintained producer/analysis recipe without studies or historical outputs.
The current study-named imports and source comments are assembly details to
resolve, not permission to broaden the scientific contract. Supplied serialized
coefficients must remain descendants of a certified initialization; syntactic

This recommendation permits preparation and validation of the bounded edition.
