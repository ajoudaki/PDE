backward-query tail, and one-reference quantitative comparison in

effective arbitrary-accuracy actual-GF solver, supported fixed effective law
family, complete numerical error chain, or useful executed demonstration.
The principal named gap is an implemented and proved terminating refinement
and certification procedure whose computable source defects connect the finite
saved state to canonical GF, with all requested observations and error budgets.

**Hold the six-scalar short-time reference as a separate bounded numerical
addition** pending complete numerical certificates and evidence of useful
accuracy and cost. Its frozen theoretical scope is relevant, but it cannot
substitute for the full milestone. Keep the residual and effective routes as
study candidates until their proposed pipelines have complete definitions,
proofs, and implementations. Do not promote their research outlines wholesale.

## Target and comparison baseline

The contract preserves the canonical bias-free two-hidden-layer tanh model,
stored Gaussian variances `(1,1/n,1/n^2)`, mobilities `(n,1,n)`, residual `f-y`,
and unhalved squared-loss physical GF. Both action orientations belong to the
same initialized action. At finite width the original random readout remains;
its zero population limit is not a modified finite initialization.

The full C-H3 request is stronger than accurate output at one reference law.
It requires a represented fixed law family inside the H2 neighborhood,
including nonorthogonal and nonatomic laws, a terminating construction for
each admitted effective law and every positive tolerance on `[0,1/200]`,
whole-circle prediction, and every separately fixed admissible same-population
joint observation tuple in W2. Its state, including frozen marks, numerical
buffers, matrices, precision and carried restart error, must have accounted
fixed storage at chosen accuracy. Closure, initialization/Gaussian,
population/input, time and arithmetic errors have separate obligations.

The demonstration additionally fixes prediction change above `1e-4`, both
paired hidden RMS motions above `1e-7`, prediction error at most `1e-5`, and
each paired RMS error at most `1e-7`, with certified positive lower endpoints
and the stated resource budget. Qualitative positive activity at an unspecified
time does not establish those assertions at `T=1/200`.

Existing C.4.7.8 already supplies a current observable hierarchy, finite joint
Gaussian initialization, both action directions, initial/current pairings,
exact weak identities, and reached-state information sufficiency. Existing
C.4.7.9 already supplies autonomous finite population equations, fixed
dictionary/ridge initialization, qualitative convergence to canonical GF,
all separately fixed observation tuples, and own-state restart. In particular,
(H2.10) tends to zero but is a proof quantity formed from the unknown target;
it is not a computable stopping certificate.

The maintained `pde.observable_closure` interface already exposes finite
feature/action contractions, the actual transpose, a finite-law interface,
joint observations, and saved-state serialization. Its guide explicitly
excludes a trajectory solver, numerical accuracy certification and an
arbitrary-accuracy implementation of the exact-real theorem. Repeating those
interfaces, equations or qualitative conclusions is not a new C-H3 result.

## Why the narrow stability addition is useful but limited

At `Y=1,T=1/200`, the candidate chooses explicit overestimates
`C=101/10000`, `R=10101/10000`, and `B=1/32`. Its exact scalar code is intended
to certify the causal cap and the displayed marginal tail at cutoff `1/5`.
This is a concrete specialization that the currently read canonical material
does not evaluate. The modest script and proof can be maintained at much lower
cost than a full joint-Gaussian solver.

The useful interpretation of its last estimate is conditional: for a
same-base represented path in the declared bounds, a certified full RHS defect
at most `1e-5` and zero initial error would give the stated raw sum error below
`5.2e-8`. This report has neither executed that verifier nor audited all proof
steps; these figures describe the frozen candidate's exact claims.

The full defect premise is not itself vacuous: a separately implemented
Gaussian/source calculation could verify it, and a comparison theorem can
be useful before that producer exists. It becomes an oracle substitution if
the user is asked to supply the unknown residual, if a certificate simply
assumes it small, or if this propagation routine is presented as the solver.

The same-base qualification is material. H2 evolves with `B_N+K_N`, whereas
the canonical path uses `A0+K`. Their initialized-action discrepancy is not an
HS state difference. The frozen stability note correctly requires a lift to
`A0+K_N` or separate action-defect control. Assembly must retain that warning
at the comparison's hypotheses, alongside candidate norm verification and
the individual observation factors. A small sum-state error is not already
the separate W2, prediction and paired-motion certificate requested by C-H3.

The result also does not numerically specify H2's admissible radius. A cap
that is uniform over finite laws is not, by itself, a completed broader-law
canonical existence/identification theorem or a certificate that a chosen
numerical arc rectangle lies inside the original ball. The residual route's
proposed short-time all-law extension is a separate scientific claim and
must remain outside the narrow assembly recommendation unless fully supplied
and reviewed as such.

## Full-milestone gaps and the effect of later evidence

1. **Effective family.** H2's radius and activity time are qualitative. The
   effective route leaves a uniform activity remainder and a certified fixed
   parameter rectangle unfinished. The residual route proposes an enlarged
   short-time existence scope and a two-atom family; that is neither a reviewed
   completion of the original in-ball family contract nor a supplied nonatomic
   solver implementation. The short-time route restricts its target to the
   admitted ball without providing effective numerical membership.
2. **Arbitrary accuracy.** The residual route proposes an eventual-acceptance
   argument and the effective route an a priori selection mechanism. Neither
   frozen route includes a complete tested implementation of the finite
   representation, recursive Gaussian enclosures, source defects, stopping
   procedure and all observation-error maps. The six-scalar reference has a
   fixed approximation error floor. Increasing numerical precision or observing
   agreement of successive resolutions cannot remove that floor or replace
   effective closure control.
3. **Useful certified accuracy.** No frozen input supplies a numerical
   trajectory with certified positive learning signals and the full numerical
   error chain. The short-time route's frozen analytic upper hidden error is
   about `1.299e-7`, already above the contract's `1e-7` upper-motion error
   tolerance before quadrature, time and arithmetic errors. This bound does
   not prove the actual error is too large, but it cannot certify the stated
   tolerance without a new valid bound or a changed approximation. The
   prediction and first-hidden analytic bounds are potentially useful.
4. **Saved-state and cost coverage.** Six evolving scalars do not count the
   initialized contractions, correlated Gaussian coordinates, observable
   quadrature and certification buffers. Likewise two population types do
   not imply small scalar storage. Full coverage must include initialization,
   Gaussian/covariance refinement, whole-circle/time verification, stored
   error at restart and all necessary working precision. The frozen routes
   provide no measured end-to-end bound or tested error-carrying restart.

Later empirical evidence alone cannot resolve items 1 or 2. A successful
reference-law run cannot establish convergence at every requested tolerance,
extend the observation language, certify a nonatomic family, or prove that
refinement terminates. A stable but uncertified trajectory would not even
resolve item 3.

A later implementation with complete analytic and arithmetic enclosures,
fresh reproduction, successful restart and recorded costs could change the
bounded reference component's disposition from hold to narrow assembly.
That evidence would be a useful separate result even if the full solver stayed
open. It would still not complete C-H3 while items 1 and 2 remain unresolved.
A genuinely completed general certification pipeline and its theory would be
new scientific input requiring a fresh assessment of that concrete package;
this report neither anticipates its correctness nor blocks such a later gate.

## Smallest suitable destinations and maintenance cost

For the accepted narrow assembly scope, prefer one short companion proof unit
at the end of C.4.7.9, following its present limitations, with direct references
to C.4.7.3 (N9)–(N19). This keeps the quantitative result beside the finite
closure it is meant to assess while avoiding a new chapter or duplicated
source-recursion proof. Title it as an explicit short-time tail/comparison
result, not a certified solver theorem. Keep C.4.7.9's qualitative convergence
claim and its stated open computational obligations unchanged in substance.

The supporting scalar calculation is best a small opt-in exact certificate
tool associated with the observable-closure material, following the repository's
existing fixed-certificate pattern. Its public contract should return scalar
enclosures and conditional propagation bounds; source/configuration provenance
and file-writing belong in a thin command driver. A new general solver API or
broad export from `pde` is not justified by this component alone. The current
140-line script is small, but assembly still needs suitable domain checks,
meaningful verification, supported arithmetic semantics and a maintained
reproduction recipe. Those are gate-2/gate-3 tasks, not acceptance established
by this selector.

If the short-time reference later qualifies, merge its complete formulas,
remainder theorem and compact reusable implementation into that same closure
area as a separately named finite-horizon approximation. Keep its finite-atom
scope, fixed error floor and supported observations explicit. Its numerical
producer and analysis must be maintained together; arrays or a study-only
driver would not suffice. Do not replace the existing H2 closure silently.

If the full residual or effective pipeline later qualifies, extend the existing
observable-closure implementation with reusable numerical representations and
certificate interfaces. The substantial maintenance costs are joint Gaussian
initialization, covariance-error propagation, high-dimensional integration,
precision/refinement semantics, autonomous state serialization and reproducible
cost evidence. Their expense should be justified by an executed useful solver,
not by a nominal count of population types or scalar state coordinates.

No independent literature-priority claim is made. Distinctness here is relative
to the assigned current canonical coverage and maintained API guide.
