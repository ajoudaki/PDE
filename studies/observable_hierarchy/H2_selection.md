## Distinct value and scope

The comparison is unusually direct. C-H1 part 9 expressly leaves finite autonomous
closure and control of omitted information open. Its exact weak evolution reads
a higher hierarchy level and takes a cutoff limit. C-H2 instead supplies two
current joint populations, a counted finite matrix, explicit initial laws and
closed current-state equations (4)–(6), with no higher-level request or evolving
cutoff. The asserted conclusion is convergence to the existing canonical GF,
including predictions and the C-H1 joint observation language. If it survives
complete scientific review, this resolves a substantial obligation that C-H1
does not resolve. It is not a second presentation of predictive sufficiency.

The useful new mathematical content is concentrated in the deterministic finite
initialized dictionary with ridge normalization, the autonomous action-coefficient
system and its energy/restart argument, and the direct approximation argument.
In particular, (10) is a target-dependent proof residual whose vanishing is
argued from strong approximation on compact sets; it is not an assumed small
tail or a coefficient read by the evolution. The action itself need not converge
in operator norm. These distinctions explain the candidate's relevance without
endorsing every proof step.

Accept only the stated model: two tanh hidden layers, normalized two-dimensional
inputs on the circle, bounded labels with separately fixed Y >= 1, canonical
Gaussian variances and small finite readout, mobilities (n,1,n), unhalved-loss
physical GF, and the actual reverse of the same action. The fixed law ball has
radius rho = delta/2 from C-H1; the fixed horizon is T = 1/200. Neither shrinks
with closure order. The candidate invokes C-H1's activity family, including
nonorthogonal and nonatomic laws and positive paired motion/nonaffinity at a
common positive time. This is scientifically relevant early nonlinear learning,
not substantial fitting through time 40.

The limiting statement is for every **separately fixed** admitted law and every
**separately fixed** finite observation tuple. Prediction convergence additionally
covers the whole input circle uniformly in time. Do not change these quantifiers
to a uniform convergence rate over the law ball, growing observation programs,
or a joint closure-order/width limit. The construction claims exact population
integration, characteristic well-posedness and own-state restart; it supplies no
certified numerical accuracy, practical complexity, GD theorem, generalization
result or C-H3/C-H4 completion.

## Finite-state and representative-network screen

The state is finite in the C-H2 population-field sense, not in scalar-storage
sense. Its two laws live on R^(d_1+4) and R^(d_2+1), and the real coefficient
matrix has d_1*d_2 entries. The fixed dictionary and joint initial laws must also
remain part of the declared input/storage account. These dimensions grow with
order but are explicit, independent of neuron width and fixed during a run.
The populations retain the correlations of frozen marks with current w or c;
replacing them by separately sampled marginals would change the construction.

On the supplied definition, M is an admissible finite action-coefficient block.
The following concrete distinctions support that selection:

- Its indices name fixed initialized observable functions, not neurons. D_N is
  obtained from canonical limiting Gaussian joint programs rather than a sampled
  n-by-n Gaussian parameter array. The initial construction includes both action
  orientations and their response corrections.
- Its action is fully declared by finite matrix multiplication and the two
  current-population contractions. The transpose is the reverse of that same
  finite action. No arbitrary hidden action or latent original middle row is a
  coordinate of either law.
- The nonlinear w and c populations remain continuum populations; fixed N does
  not select a representative finite neuron count. Their currents are evolved
  from their own state, without training-trajectory samples or growing history.

Thus this is not, on its face, a representative dense network renamed as a
population solver. The fact that M is trainable and dense does not alone defeat
this theoretical construction. Conversely, the feature interpretation does not
establish manageable computation: the coefficient storage is quadratic in feature
counts, and the exact joint laws still require representation or integration.
The roadmap's warning about a renamed trainable matrix and a hidden large density
must remain active at C-H3. A later implementation that merely draws a dense
network or silently discards these joint laws would need a new assessment.

No trajectory-playback, noncausal-initialization or width-scaling loophole is
apparent in the candidate's specified interface. The ridge schedule is explicit
and independent of the target law/path. The exact Gaussian integrals and real
matrix operations are exposed assumptions of the theoretical representation;
finite precision and computable error bounds remain separate obligations. This
screen does not replace the detailed information-flow and dependency audits by
the two scientific reviewers.

Necessary accompanying edits are small: replace C-H1 part 9's open-C-H2 summary
with a precise pointer, and update the C-H2 roadmap/coverage text in

useful computation. Preserve C-H1's exact hierarchy and reached-state sufficiency
statements: C-H2's approximate finite state does not replace them. No broader
chapter reorganization or new general Gaussian compiler is justified by this
selection.

Maintenance cost is moderate for the theorem: one local construction with an
explicit dictionary, finite filters and a direct comparison, but tight dependency
on the initialized generated spaces and the canonical target's tail estimates.
Keep N for closure order distinct from n for neuron width; label lowercase
population aliases, the finite transpose, the operator adjoint, and HS versus
Frobenius bounds consistently. The proof-only Q operators and eps_N should never
appear as required runtime inputs.

Potential numerical maintenance is substantially less determined. Gaussian
program unions grow; ridge inversion can be poorly conditioned; feature bounds
and joint population dimensions can become large. Repeated/zero-variance words,
the coupled reverse initialization and exact ridge semantics are consequential
interfaces. An unsupported broad API would impose avoidable maintenance cost.

Therefore **no concrete maintained module or code-review readiness is accepted
here**. If the prototype supports a compact reusable state/initialization/RHS/

and numerical-limits paragraph in `code/README.md`. Final naming and supported
scope should follow assessment of that actual code. Keep campaign drivers and
exploratory outputs in the study. The roadmap explicitly requires a small
prototype during C-H2, so do not call the full milestone complete on the theory
selection alone. This hold asks for the already planned artifact, not new
research to rescue the candidate.
