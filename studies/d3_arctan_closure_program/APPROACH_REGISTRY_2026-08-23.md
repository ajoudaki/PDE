## Proof frontier before the final shutdown audit

The narrowest remaining assertion is not “apply TP” and not “prove DMFT.”
After the endpoint Abel reduction and the failure of quadratic BMO, it is one
of three equivalent-looking signed estimates:

1. a uniform-in-Euler-mesh exponential tail for the limiting TP middle query,
   obtained by controlling its signed endpoint predictor; or
2. a finite-width two-sided block-cavity estimate that keeps the exact
   divided-difference increment unsquared through \(p\asymp\log n\); or
3. a chronological conditional-moment estimate proving that each canonical
   innovation has only one effective fresh Gaussian carrier, with every
   Stein/Onsager compensator bounded by the lower action.

The signed first-chaos estimate now proves that the complete response
correction is uniformly bounded in \(L^2\).  It does **not** prove the needed
tail: a covariance-energy-to-\(\psi_1\) implication is false even for bounded
arctan histories.  Any surviving proof must use a genuinely dynamical
restriction on the reachable response row.

The first preregistered cavity computation supports the predicted
\(n^{-1/2}\) state-response scale and stable middle-query moments, but its
clustered response confidence intervals were too wide for its frozen
formal-support verdict.  The later direct high-moment experiment formally
passes its separate frozen support rule: moment-growth and raw-kernel slopes
remain width-stable through \(n=2048\), with solver and arithmetic audits well
inside tolerance.  Neither experiment changes a theorem rung.

The middle-saturation discriminator also passes through width \(4096\): no
sampled primary trajectory exhibited a large open-gate query held by a
75-percent opposing-bath cancellation, and all sampled \(|R_2|\ge2\)
coordinates at horizon four had closed gates.  The exact inequality
\(|\kappa(z,w)|\le d(z)d(w)\) independently shows that the learned two-time
leaf has only one ungated query factor.  The surviving obligation is now a
canonical annealed slow-tube/anti-cancellation estimate; global PSD and action
cannot provide its coordinatewise form.

The second preregistered computation rejects the stronger hypothesis that the
column response remains row-delocalized.  It simultaneously finds a small
width slope for the total Frobenius norm.  Thus A20 remains a valid
conditional reduction, but its proof cannot pass through bounded IPR,
small top-percent leverage, or a uniformly diffuse row profile.

The direct growing-iteration theorem search is now closed: its missing
statement is exactly the same new leave-out estimate already isolated above,
not an overlooked Tensor Program or AMP corollary.  A naive row probe and the
first passive multi-cavity proof are also closed.  The refined row route must
resum every same-row diagonal return and use the arctangent cancellation in
A19, but that is only preprocessing.  The sharp frontier is a
signed-resolvent/nonalignment estimate excluding coherent concentration of
the off-diagonal bath along the characteristic tangent; deterministic PSD,
normalized energy, and own-row independence cannot supply it.

A23 further excludes a deterministic or entropy-contractive closure.  The
surviving estimate is intrinsically annealed: its proof must keep the signed
Gaussian cancellations coupled to the active response, rather than first
bounding a pathwise tangent operator.

A24 locates the exact nonuniformity inside the strongest recent rigorous
cavity proof.  Time-simplex factors control causal memories only after the
instantaneous field estimate; they cannot compensate for its repeated
\(p\mapsto p^2\) and derivative-order escalation.

A38 is now closed in its ordinary quadratic form.  Conditional integration
over an independent Gaussian output mark has a finite radius determined by
the largest row leverage, so total action or annealed leverage tails cannot
control the bracket.  A39 avoids that artificial loss by using the exact
signed divided difference before squaring.  Its decisive audit is whether the
full chronological filtration preserves the one-fresh-carrier structure once
all static Onsager compensators and lower-path feedback are retained.

The local carrier audit answers that last question positively but not
sufficiently: no degree-three atom survives, yet C-61 constructs a lognormal
causal product from repeated degree-one arctan gates.  The construction uses
quadratic innovation of order one.  A41 now tests the genuinely stronger
network fact that the total new-direction quadratic mass is \(O(h)\).  In
parallel, A40 is the finite-width route: it reduces the claim to a marked
row--entry two-cavity estimate rather than a generic covariance-isometry
inequality.

## Paused proof frontier — 23 August 2026

This section supersedes the strategic frontier above.

The first unresolved contract rung remains C-13: a compact-time,
cutoff-uniform \(\psi_1\) or equivalent moderate-moment bound for
\[
R_{2,i}(t)=\Gamma_{2,:i}^{\mathsf T}B_3(t)
+\int_0^tX_{2,i}(s)\langle B_3(s),B_3(t)\rangle_n\,ds .
\]
The learned integral is bounded; the trained static-column projection is the

> Prove joint dynamic low influence for one or finitely many paired Gaussian
> row/column blocks after every order-one same-block return has been retained
> as a causal Volterra self-kernel.

1. **Value/crossing formulation (A73--A74).**  Prove the conditional carrier,
   bath, causal-kernel, and remainder estimates needed to apply (27.3).

   \(\|\|D_gB_3\|_{\rm HS}\|_{2q}\le C_T\sqrt q\) through
   \(q\lesssim\log n\).
