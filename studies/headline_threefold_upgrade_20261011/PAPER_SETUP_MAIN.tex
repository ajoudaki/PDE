\begin{proposition}[Four efficient initializers]
\label{cp:initialization}

Assume the setup of \Cref{cp:headline}. In exact real arithmetic,
Harmonic and Taylor each admit both a supplied dense initializer and a fresh implicit Gaussian initializer. For
every separately fixed admissible problem and confidence, eventually their
work and peak setup storage obey the following bounds.

\begin{center}\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.13\linewidth}p{.24\linewidth}p{.29\linewidth}p{.25\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Construction & Reference initialization & Arithmetic work & Peak setup real coordinates\\
\midrule
Harmonic & Supplied dense & $O(n^2[\log(en)]^{3d/2+1})$ & $O(n^2)$\\
Taylor & Supplied dense & $O(n^2[\log(en)]^{5/2})$ & $O(n^2)$\\
Harmonic & Fresh implicit Gaussian & $O(n[\log(en)]^{9d/2+3})$ & $O(n[\log(en)]^{3d/2+1})$\\
Taylor & Fresh implicit Gaussian & $O(n[\log(en)]^5)$ & $O(n[\log(en)]^{5/2})$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The constants in this table may depend on the separately fixed
$m,d,p,L,\beta,\gamma,Y$ and confidence. They are not the
activation-and-depth-only constant $C$ in the retained-storage formulas.

All four produce the retained inventories in
\eqref{cp:harmonic-size} and \eqref{cp:panel-size} and satisfy
\eqref{cp:absolute} on their original query domains and all physical
times, including the fitted endpoint. Consequently they preserve
\eqref{cp:relative}. The implicit execution produces exactly the
same final arrays as the corresponding supplied-matrix numerical initializer
under a coupling with its fresh Gaussian reference. A numerical initializer
need not select the same arrays as the initialized-jet compiler.

The operation model charges exact scalar arithmetic, comparisons, square
roots, elementary scalar functions, real activation values, and Gaussian
samples as primitives. Nonprimitive evaluators have their actual work,
description and scratch added separately. No high activation derivatives or
trained-reference values are inputs. Activation envelopes and the positive
population gap are supplied mathematical certificates. Their computation
is not included. Taylor uses the polynomial source-width event of
\Cref{cp:poly:source} and the accuracy and simplified-storage
gates of \Cref{cp:poly:width}. No extra eventual source event
is needed for its numerical initialization. Harmonic retains its eventual
source-width qualification. Full parameter and finite-order costs are given
in \hyperref[cp:init:sec:costs]{the detailed cost ledger}. The complete proof is in \Cref{cp:init:proof}.
\end{proposition}
