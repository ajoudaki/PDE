## Why the main bridges work

bound for exact population Euler predictions. It differentiates residuals,
both Gaussian covariance families and both response-coefficient arrays.
The short-interval proof subtracts a lower feature expression depending only
on old sources. Covariance derivatives involving a new source annihilate that
boundary expression; the remaining feature increment supplies the interval
length needed for absorption. The absorption coefficient uses base moments,
while derivative history enters only an additive bound. The same linear
part controls mixed second responses. No arbitrary ambient-L2 smoothness,
inverse mass or covariance inverse is substituted for this estimate.

The uniform quadratic finite-program remainder then justifies actual influence
limits rather than differentiating value convergence without a bound. Common
quantization and uniform forward-quotient convergence pass the construction to

The sampling proof uses small single and double replacements, not convergence
of nonatomic empirical laws in total variation. Its exact Hilbert Hoeffding
decomposition separates bias, higher-order interactions and first-projection
error. Their scaled mean-square sum tends to zero. A finite-test cutoff gives
exponentially rare exceptional sample laws, which justifies the specified
zero extension. The width-first bridge uses bounded tests outside the good
