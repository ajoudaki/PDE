# Exploratory probe report

**Program:** Renormalized Causal Gaussian Calculus (RCGC)  
**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Terminal status:** validation ladder passed; depth-three arctangent not
resolved; strict probe success criterion not met.

## 1. Bottom line

The proposed calculus became a real reusable machine on the resolved
ladder. It proved, without importing the forbidden model-specific solution
devices:

- identity activation at every separately fixed hidden depth, hence
  \(H=1,2,3\), by one physical-time pointed-action theorem;
- the declared generic \(H=1\) activation class by one population-flow rule;
  and
- an infinite tame-natural-gate class at \(H=2\), including the exact
  arctangent model, by one projective-source/invariant-envelope/two-mesh
  theorem.

Those are substantive positive results. They show that the exact compiler,
source split, projective Gaussian action, trace-class current state,
natural-coordinate transform, and quantitative cutoff/UI rules are not
merely new notation for the old proofs.

The calculus did **not** decide arctangent \(H=3\). At the first genuinely
two-action nonlinear multiplier it lacks a quantitative orbit-specific
Orlicz/response theorem. Consequently it cannot currently certify limiting
IDE well-posedness, uniform exact/Euler comparison, the two bottom raw
kernel terms, or the compact-time width limit. Under the user's stipulated
criterion—properly designed calculus should make D3 routine and decisive—the
probe therefore fails at its crux.

## 3. Reusable machinery actually established

1. **Exact typed compiler.** It emits all forward/backward fields, mixed
   metric block velocities, raw kernel terms, and curvature/return words for
   arbitrary fixed depth.
2. **Immutable source plus current action.** Every trained matrix is
   \(G=\Gamma+P\), with initialization-only bounded Gaussian action
   \(\Gamma\) and current trace-class \(P\). Rank-one velocities have the
   exact nuclear norm needed for compact-time control.
3. **Projective fixed-program source.** A countable language closed under
   parallel joins yields one probability algebra per type and true bounded
   adjoint actions, rather than unrelated scalar limits.
4. **Physical residual continuation.** For the linear ladder,
   \(\int_0^T e^2K\le e(0)^2/(4\eta)\) bounds the path length of every
   parameter block and gives global physical-time continuation.
5. **Natural-coordinate rule.** Positive gates with
   \(\Theta'=1/\phi'\) transform the trained bottom coordinate generically;
   arctangent is only the \(m=1\) instance of an infinite family.
6. **Invariant-envelope well-posedness.** Current-multiplier saturation is
   introduced only on a fixed cutoff slab and proved inactive. Gaussian
   cutoff removal uses a correctly bounded Osgood multiplier lemma.
7. **Two-mesh raw-square rule.** One auxiliary mesh proves exact-orbit
   square UI; after fixing a tail level, a distinct mesh transfers the
   saturated raw observable. This avoids a hidden growing-program diagonal.

## 4. New negative results and rollbacks

The probe falsified several tempting general rules.

### 4.1 Global feature time is false for deep linear ascent

For identity activation and \(H\ge2\), the unit feature-gradient Fock flow
blows up at finite positive and negative feature times. The global theorem
survives only because it is formulated directly in residual-weighted
physical time. Any future scalar-clock rule must prove trapping inside the
maximal feature interval rather than assume feature-globality.

### 4.2 Bare Hilbert stability is false

The map \((A,Z)\mapsto A d(Z)\) is not locally Lipschitz
\(L^2\times L^2\to L^2\). At H2, the immutable Gaussian mark plus bounded
current increment repairs this with an Osgood modulus. At H3 the multiplier
is instead a dynamically generated field, so that repair does not transfer
automatically.

### 4.3 One fixed \(L^p\) envelope is not enough

For the D3 middle field,

\[
 \Delta B_2=d(Z_2)\Delta Q_2+widetilde Q_2
 \{d(Z_2)-d(\widetilde Z_2)\}.                        \tag{4.1}
\]

On normalized \(L^2\) balls, a shrinking-support spike makes the second
term order one while \(\Delta Z_2\to0\). Even a fixed \(L^p\) bound yields
only a Hölder modulus \(s^\alpha\), \(\alpha<1\), which is non-Osgood. An
unrestricted source-dependent \(L^p\) state space also fails because a
Gaussian row reused as its own input can amplify the normalized \(L^p\)
norm by \(n^{1/2-1/p}\).

### 4.4 The first D3 two-colour return is real

The compiler and an independent reconstruction both produce

\[
 N=D_1G_2^{0*}D_2G_3^{0*}E_3G_3^0D_2G_2^0D_1.
\]

Its normalized trace may center, while its normalized Hilbert--Schmidt
gluing remains generically order one. It cannot be discarded. A two-copy
first-tangent susceptibility represents its first gluing, but this does not
prove either finite-response closure or the necessity of an all-word traffic
state.

### 4.5 The proposed global second-order shortcut was circular

Requiring the state and its first two variations to be uniformly Cauchy
across meshes already supplies the missing width/time interchange for a
stronger augmented state. Fixed-grid identification plus that hypothesis
almost immediately proves the target. It was therefore rejected as the
original theorem wearing the name of a machinery lemma.

## 5. Exact D3 frontier

The source split sharpens (4.1):

\[
 Q_2=\Gamma_3^*B_3+P_3^*B_3.                         \tag{5.1}
\]

The learned-history term is coordinatewise bounded on compact time by its
rank-one integral representation. The unresolved part is the **dependent
two-sided Gaussian action** \(\Gamma_3^*B_3\).

A sufficient quantitative certificate would be a restartable empirical
subexponential bound for this field and the response objects needed to
propagate it. In tail form,

\[
 \sup_{n,\pi,t\le T}
 \|Q_2^\pi(t){\bf1}_{\{|Q_2^\pi(t)|>R\}}\|_2
 \lesssim(1+R)e^{-cR}.                               \tag{5.2}
\]

Optimizing the bounded/tail split would give
\(s\log(e/s)\), an Osgood modulus. Once such a bound is available, the
deterministic stability and two-mesh readout passage are largely routine.
No current theorem proves (5.2) uniformly in time and mesh.

## 6. Best surviving machinery program

The only strategically credible continuation identified by independent
designs is a **restartable, stopped, two-sided one-site cavity/Volterra
calculus**, but its first version failed hostile audit. The corrected local
target is deliberately narrower than the full limit:

> From a compact-slab \(L^2\)/trace bound and an empirical \(\psi_1\) bound
> for \(Q_2\) at a restart time, propagate a mesh-uniform empirical
> \(\psi_1\) bound for \(Q_2\) on a short time slab, with a controlled
> restart recursion.

The proof would need:

1. direct \(\psi_1\) control at initialization;
2. an exact one-row/one-column conditional Gaussian equation that resums
   **all** repeated tagged returns as a causal Volterra resolvent;
3. uniform Orlicz plus operator/Hilbert--Schmidt response bounds;
4. an all-order response summability and remainder theorem, unless an exact
   finite Volterra closure is found;
5. noncircular stop removal through moderate deviations up to
   \(p\lesssim\log n\), or a response-stable good/bad-coordinate argument;
6. a one-step correlated-multiplier/gluing estimate
   \[
    \mathcal E^+\le(1+Ca)\mathcal E
      +Ca\,\omega_{\rm loc}+a r_n,
   \]
   with the width remainder multiplied by the step size; and
7. deterministic Bihari/Grönwall deriving, rather than assuming, mesh
   Cauchy convergence, followed by elimination of proof-only responses from
   the final current IDE.

This is a genuine machinery program in scope and structure, not one cute
lemma. It is also not yet built. Response-trace locality, all-return
summability, and stop removal remain deep leaves; the first blueprint merely
renamed them and was rejected.

## 7. Final assessment

The RCGC strategy is **validated through one nonlinear trained Gaussian
action** and is demonstrably more than a reformulation: it produced common
theorems and found two previously hidden false assumptions. It is **not yet
an extensible depth-general calculus**. Adding the second nonlinear trained
action crosses a qualitative threshold from an immutable Gaussian-mark
multiplier to a dynamically dependent Gaussian-action multiplier.

Accordingly:

- the base-model part of the exploratory promise succeeded;
- the claim that the current calculus could simply emit the D3 answer failed;
- D3 remains mathematically open, not conditionally resolved;
- the corrected Orlicz/Volterra program is the only route that survived as a
  serious next design, but even it has not passed its core local theorem;
  and
- no numerical experiment was used to upgrade a claim. The decisive present
  evidence consists of exact identities, explicit topology counterexamples,
  and independent proof audits.
