## 1. Fixed activation

Define

\[
 F(v)=\int_0^v(1+s^2)^{-3/4}\,ds,qquad
 \phi(x)=1+\frac18F(\operatorname {arsinh}x).          \tag{1.1}
\]

Its derivative is

\[
 d(x)=\phi'(x)=\frac1{8\sqrt{1+x^2}
 (1+\operatorname {arsinh}^2x)^{3/4}}.                \tag{1.2}
\]

Let

\[
 I_{3/2}=\int_0^\infty(1+s^2)^{-3/4}\,ds
 ={\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(1/4)\over2\Gamma(3/4)}
 \simeq2.62205755.
\]

Then

\[
 0<m:=1-I_{3/2}/8\le\phi(x)\le1+I_{3/2}/8=:M,        \tag{1.3}
\]

so the output has a fixed positive floor.  The activation is smooth,
strictly increasing, and genuinely nonlinear.  Neither it nor any of its
parameters depends on width, time, initialization, or trajectory.

The log derivative is

\[
 {d'(x)\over d(x)}=-{x\over1+x^2}
 -{3\operatorname {arsinh}x\over
 2(1+\operatorname {arsinh}^2x)\sqrt{1+x^2}},         \tag{1.4}
\]

and is globally bounded.  For a standard Gaussian `Z`,

\[
 \|d(Z)^{-1}\|_{L^p}\asymp
 \sqrt p\,(\log p)^{3/2}.                             \tag{1.5}
\]
