## Precommitted decision rule

The near-identity theorem is promoted to **proved** only if one route gives:

1. the exact fixed-step width-first identification;
2. an explicit activation class containing a genuinely nonlinear member;
3. a history-independent internal response/jet norm derived from activation
   data;
4. a complete bound for mixed moving-query and aggregate-adjoint terms;
5. a sub-\(t^5\) canonical remainder coefficient;
6. an independent reconstruction and hostile audit.

If the route stops at an assumed generated-core bound, fixed-\(t\)
continuity, or an ambient norm known to fail, it remains **open**.
