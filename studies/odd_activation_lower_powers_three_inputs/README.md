This is a study record, not an established-library entry. Historical claims and
review labels below retain their original scope; consult the
[reconciled research map](../project_wide_audit_2026_09_08/MASTER_RESEARCH_REPORT.md)

The [complete two-input proof](TWO_INPUT_PROOF.md) establishes the
original global population/GF/raw-GD theorem for every
0 < theta <= c_poly delta^2, retaining the previous explicit prefactor.
The requested delta^3 choice is included. See [review status](REVIEW_STATUS.md)
for the independent complete-proof audits and their precise scope.

Its two new companions are [weighted affine kernels and positive
closure](WEIGHTED_AFFINE_AND_CLOSURE.md) and [sector-resolved source

with M^16 <= 24^4 delta^-2. All original initialization, raw dynamics,
uniqueness/restart, finite-interval population limits, action/adjoint,
path/velocity, nonaffinity and initial-motion conclusions are retained.
The numerical prefactor remains extremely small.

The [three-input analysis](THREE_INPUT_ANALYSIS.md) does **not** establish
the analogous complete theorem for the odd mixture. It proves the
initial nonlinear Gram bound, explains the singular affine obstruction,
and gives quantitative excursion and conditional scalar-clock results.
It identifies the missing global source-tail/continuation mechanism
without treating failure of the affine method as a counterexample to
the positive-nonlinearity theorem.
