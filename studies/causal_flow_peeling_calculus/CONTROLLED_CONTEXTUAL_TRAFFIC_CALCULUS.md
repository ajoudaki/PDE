# Controlled Contextual-Traffic Calculus

## 1. Strategic verdict

The surviving machinery candidate is a synthesis of three exact pieces:

1. **MFP supplies fixed-program semantics** for one persistent Gaussian source
   and its exact transpose.
2. **Gate-resolved increment peeling** supplies a recursive, finite-step map
   whose local defects are causal and experimentally diffuse.
3. **A source-aware chronological forest calculus** must control only the
   perturbations actually generated by those defects and Gaussian cavities,
   rather than arbitrary directions in an energy ball.

The third item is not proved.  It is the genuinely new program.  Its master
estimate is a concrete combinatorial/occupation theorem, not a generic
adaptive-tail assumption.  If it passes first at two hidden layers, it gives
a plausible depth-recursive route; if it fails there, this calculus is
rejected.

The working name is **Controlled Contextual-Traffic Calculus (CCTC)**.  The
word `controlled` refers both to explicit seminorms on every context and to
the causal cone of admissible perturbations.  `Traffic` records typed matrix
orientation, equality partitions, and normalized contractions.  No traffic
independence theorem is being assumed.

## 2. Why deterministic completion failed

Normalized vector energy and matrix operator norms are not a sufficient
state topology.  Two exact counterexamples establish this:

- an `o(1)` one-coordinate change of a forward gate, paired with an
  energy-bounded response spike, changes the next backward field by order
  one;
- even adding any fixed `L^p` response certificate leaves an uncancelled
  transverse curvature direction with growth rate
  `epsilon^(-1/p)`.

The same phenomenon appears at random initialization if one takes a supremum
over all tangent directions: selecting the largest readout coordinate gives
a Hessian direction of size `sqrt(log n)`.  Thus neither full operator-norm
stability, arbitrary finite-rank stability, nor the linear span of all seed
cavities can be width-uniform.

What is different about a numerical defect or a natural Gaussian cavity is
its **source cost and causality**.  It does not choose the worst coordinate
after seeing the source, and its coefficients are normalized empirical
contractions.  CCTC preserves this distinction syntactically.

## 3. Exact one-time contextual state

Let `W_l=G_l(0)` denote the immutable Gaussian source of edge color `l`.
Define a countable typed algebra with:

- immutable seed fields `u_0,A_0,W_l,W_l^*`, with the two orientations of
  each `W_l` tied;
- current primal and reverse fields `u,A,G_l,z_l,x_l,r_l,b_l`;
- current learned morphisms and their adjoints;
- coordinate gates `arctan`, `d(z)=(1+z^2)^(-1)`, the bounded logarithmic
  derivative `h=d'/d`, and exact divided differences;
- normalized contractions, rank-one maps, equality partitions, and source
  colors;
- marked cavity, tangent, and higher-response words generated by the rules
  below;
- energy and occupation ports attached to every unbounded word.

A state `tau` is a positive normalized character on the completion of this
algebra, together with its explicit grade seminorms.  A convenient weak
metric on bounded contexts is

\[
 d(\tau,\sigma)=\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}
 \frac{|\tau(C_j)-\sigma(C_j)|}
      {1+|\tau(C_j)-\sigma(C_j)|},
\tag{1}
\]

for an effective enumeration of legal contexts.  Unbounded contexts are not
hidden by (1); their moment/occupation coordinates are part of the state
class.

Time differentiation of a finite word using the exact gradient equations,
Leibniz rule, gate rule, and rank-one rule defines a one-time derivation
`L`.  The intended limiting equation is

\[
 \frac d{dt}\tau_t(C)=\tau_t(LC).
\tag{2}
\]

The immutable source generators remain in `tau_t`; an arbitrary path prefix
does not.  Thus (2) is autonomous if its weighted completion is unique.

## 4. Exact finite-step compiler

For a short step `h`, use the paired-edge map from
`GATE_RESOLVED_INCREMENT_CALCULUS.md`:

1. solve the top coordinate block with the lower input frozen;
2. propagate the accumulated upper covector through each edge by

   \[
   \Delta W_-=q_-
   \left(G^*a+\frac12x\|a\|_n^2\right);
   \tag{3}
   \]

3. convert mobility displacement to the next accumulated covector by the
   continuous divided difference of `J=Phi^(-1)`;
4. update the corresponding rank-one morphism;
5. restore the forward graph using the updated parameters.

For fixed `h` and finitely many steps this is one finite tied-Gaussian
program, so MFP evaluates every fixed finite context.  Equations (3) and the
divided-difference rule are layer-recursive and contain no inverse feature-
Gram singularity.

The compiler is not justified by deterministic Lipschitz stability.  Its
role is to make local defects causal, time-integrated source words to which
the controlled forest rules apply.

## 5. The source-aware reachable cone

An admissible controlled perturbation is the derivative at `epsilon=0` of

\[
 \dot\theta^\epsilon
 =F_n(\theta^\epsilon)+\epsilon S_n^\epsilon,
\tag{4}
\]

where `S` is generated causally from:

- the current gradient and its short-time integrals;
- Euler, Runge--Kutta, or paired-block local defects;
- standardized endpoint, row, or column Gaussian cavities, measured after
  averaging over the cavity label;
- tangent rank-one factors
  `v tensor x_l + b_(l+1) tensor w` already in the cone;
- finitely many current empirical contractions with coefficients carrying a
  width-independent moment certificate.

Forbidden operations are maximum-coordinate selection, arbitrary realized
coefficients on cavities, arbitrary low-rank factors, future-dependent
coefficients, or rescaling that hides source cost.  The cone is deliberately
not a linear space under arbitrary random coefficients.

This restriction is observable and recursive: each admissible source has a
finite typed syntax and a declared cost.

## 6. Exact tangent reduction

For arctangent put

\[
 d(z)=\frac1{1+z^2},
 \qquad
 h(z)=\frac{d'(z)}{d(z)}=-\frac{2z}{1+z^2},
 \qquad |h|\le1.
\]

For a variation `D`,

\[
 Dz_1=Du,
 \quad Dx_l=d_lDz_l,
 \quad Dz_{l+1}=DG_lx_l+G_lDx_l,
\]

\[
 Db_L=d_LDA+h_Lb_LDz_L,
\]

\[
 Dr_l=DG_l^*b_{l+1}+G_l^*Db_{l+1},
 \quad Db_l=d_lDr_l+h_lb_lDz_l.
\tag{5}
\]

With normalized vector metric and ordinary Frobenius matrix metric, direct
second variation gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D^2f[D\theta,D\theta]
 ={}&2\langle DA,Dx_L\rangle_n
 +2\sum_{l=1}^{L-1}
   \langle b_{l+1},DG_lDx_l\rangle_n\\
 &+\sum_{l=1}^L
   \langle h_lb_l,(Dz_l)^2\rangle_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

On the deterministic energy envelope, every term except the last sum is
bounded by the tangent energy.  Hence the complete obstruction is the
occupation form

\[
 \mathcal O(D\theta)
 =\sum_l\langle |b_l|,|Dz_l|^2\rangle_n.
\tag{7}
\]

This is sharper than asking for a marginal tail of `r_2`: it measures the
alignment of response mass with the actual causal perturbation.

Higher arctangent variations remain in the same grammar because

\[
 \sup_z\left|\frac{d^{(m)}(z)}{d(z)}\right|
 \le C^m m!.
\tag{8}
\]

Every gate derivative has bounded coefficient times one existing `b` field;
no new naked response type appears.  The factorial in (8) must be paid by
tree symmetry and chronological integration, not ignored.

## 7. Chronological cavity forests

Repeatedly substitute (3)--(5) and peel every source action by the exact
colored Wick--Stein rule.  The resulting object is a rooted, time-ordered,
typed forest.  Its vertices are:

- rank-one update splits;
- bounded gate or divided-difference vertices;
- forward or transpose source half-edges with a common color;
- response edges created when Gaussian integration by parts differentiates
  an adaptive subtree;
- normalized scalar contractions and equality partitions;
- external moment or occupation ports.

The rules are:

1. perform global equality-partition power counting before pruning;
2. retain exact same-color forward/transpose pairings;
3. charge every genuine branch one chronological integration;
4. resum maximal degree-two response chains only after their kernel has been
   bounded in the same controlled norm;
5. charge a zero-width-defect component either to a declared external port
   or to a retained response type; all excess-cycle components must carry an
   explicit inverse-width deficit.

## 8. Falsified first estimate and surviving master gates

The first proposed estimate `C^(p+k)p!sqrt(k!)`, obtained by bounding its
ingredients separately and uniformly in `p`, is false.  A scalar high-valence
arctangent star already has factorial derivative mass before gate symmetry,
a branching tree does not have a blanket `1/k!` chronological volume, and at
fixed width exact forward/transpose backtracking can make absolute
coefficients as large as `(k!)^2`.  Joint packetization recovers a weaker
fixed-port leading theorem below; it does not rescue the original uniform
claim.  See `CHRONOLOGICAL_FOREST_AUDIT.md`.

The viable statement must combine tree symmetry, actual partial-order time
volume, gate Taylor factors, equality partitions, and Wick pairings in one
estimate.  Let `F^(0)_(g,p,k)(Delta)` be the **leading-width**, already time-
integrated mass on a short block of length `Delta`, for observable grade `g`,
fixed occupation-port number `p`, and response grade `k`.  A closed L2
first-variation audit proves, at the formal strict-leading level,

\[
 \boxed{
 F^{(0)}_{g,p,k}(\Delta)
 \le C_T^p p!\,
      \frac{(C_Tp\Delta)^k}{\sqrt{k!}},
 \qquad p<\infty.
 }
\tag{JC-p}
\]

The proof packetizes gate derivatives with their Taylor/Hermite symmetry,
uses a Catalan recurrence for exact tied ribbons, and proves one unbounded
readout-seed leaf per dynamical vertex.  See
`L2_ONE_EXCESS_LEAF_AUDIT.md`.  A corresponding unintegrated statement sums
over zero-defect gluings `Pi_0(tau;g,p)` with the actual number of linear
extensions and retains the necessary port factor:

\[
 \sum_{|\tau|=k}L(\tau)
 \prod_{v\in\tau}
 \frac{|\arctan^{(d_v)}(x_v)|}{d_v!}
 \sum_{\pi\in\Pi_0(\tau;g,p)}|w(\tau,\pi)|
 \le C_{T,g}^{p+k}p!\,p^k\sqrt{k!}.
\tag{9}
\]

For every fixed `p`, (JC-p) is summable.  But the `p^k` factor is sharp: a
single Gaussian diagonal tangent cell shared by `p` ports already produces
it.  Resummation grows as `p! exp(C_Tp^2)`.  Thus the earlier uniform-port
version of (JC), and the all-order Orlicz conclusion drawn from it, are false.
Only a fixed-port occupation route survives.

Leading-width control alone cannot be interchanged with the response sum.
The second gate is a sign-preserving finite-width remainder theorem.  If
`R^(n,h)_(>K,Delta)` denotes the response sector above grade `K` after the
closed unary chains have been resummed, the needed statement is

\[
 \boxed{
 \lim_{K\to\infty}\limsup_{n\to\infty}
 \sup_{h:\,\Delta/h\in\mathbb N}
 \Pr\!left[
   \|R^{(n,h)}_{>K,\Delta}\|_{\rm occ}>\epsilon
 \right]=0.
 }
\tag{FR}
\]

Termwise absolute estimates cannot prove (FR).  Width-defect factors,
localization, and any stabilizing sign must be retained jointly.  Calling a
chain a Volterra resolvent is not enough: its kernel must be bounded without
using the unknown response itself.

Thus CCTC now has two decisive gates.  The formal one-color fixed-port
leading theorem (JC-p) has passed its first nontrivial audit, but compiler
completeness and promotion to an actual trajectory estimate still require
checking.  The two-scale finite-width/restart theorem (FR) remains open.

## 9. Consequences if (JC-p) and (FR) hold

### 9.1 Occupation stability

For every admissible tangent `Dtheta` with source cost `Cost`, the fixed-port
forms of (JC-p)--(FR) would imply

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\mathbb E\sup_{t\le T}\|D\theta(t)\|_{\mathcal H_n}^2\\
 &\quad+
 \mathbb E\int_0^T\sum_l
 \langle(1+|b_l|)|Dz_l|^2\rangle_n dt
 \le C_T\,\mathrm{Cost}^2+o_n(1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}

The same fixed-port calculation with one extra port would yield uniform integrability
of the integrand.  Rare coordinates are controlled after averaging over the
Gaussian source and, for cavities, over their label; no coordinate maximum
is taken.

### 9.2 All-order moment route is not supplied

The sharp `p^k` insertion count resums to quadratic-exponential port growth,
not the `K_Tp` moment envelope previously claimed.  Therefore (JC-p) does not
yield the Yudovich--Orlicz multiplier hypothesis.  The surviving route must
prove the fixed-port occupation estimate (11) directly, preserving the
signed mobility/response structure, or obtain marginal response tails from
an independent cavity theorem.

### 9.3 Uniform mesh removal

An interpolated finite-step path satisfies the exact flow with a forcing
equal to its local defect.  Write the block as the chronological composition
of its layer vector fields.  The exact variation-of-constants identity
expresses its one-step defect as a double time integral of their Lie brackets,
plus a third-order remainder.  Each bracket is a first or second variation of
the network vector field applied to current gradient directions, so it is an
  admissible source tree under (4)--(8).  The multilinear version of
  fixed-port (JC-p)--(FR) must
therefore prove

\[
 \mathrm{Cost}(\mathrm{Defect}_h)\le C_Th^2.
\tag{13}
\]

This supplies an integrated forcing cost `O(h)` over `T/h` steps.  Applying
(11) along a homotopy of controlled paths then gives

\[
 \lim_{h\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty}
 \Pr\left[
 \sup_{t\le T}d_n(\theta_n(t),\theta_{n,h}(t))>\epsilon
 \right]=0.
\tag{14}
\]

For fixed `h`, MFP supplies the width limit.  Equation (14) makes these
limits Cauchy as `h` tends to zero and yields compact-time convergence.

### 9.4 Autonomy and computability

The weighted forest tail makes the derivation (2) unique in the reachable
state class.  Restarting the same finite compiler from the current character
and using (13) proves the semigroup identity.  Truncating at response grade
`K`, moment grade `p`, and finite context set gives a finite program; (9),
the moment tail, the time defect, and fixed-program width error are separate
computable certificates.

These conclusions are conditional until (JC-p), (FR), their one-extra-port
versions, and the local-defect source-cost estimate are proved.

## 10. Calibration and depth recursion

The calculus must be proved in this order:

1. linear `L=1`: no source forest;
2. linear `L=2`: one color and exact transpose backtracking;
3. linear `L=3`: alternating colors and balancedness checks;
4. generic nonlinear `L=1`: contextual flow equals the classical
   two-coordinate characteristic flow;
5. arctangent `L=2`: complete the fixed-port zero-defect gate (JC-p), the finite-width
   remainder gate (FR), occupation stability, mesh removal, and restart with
   one source color;
6. only then arctangent `L=3`: verify that a second independent color changes
   constants, not forest types or the joint contraction--symmetry rules.

The earlier ladder theorems verify the answers but cannot be imported as the
proof of these gates.  CCTC passes a rung only when its compiler, forest
bound, and certificate recover that answer.

A fixed finite training set adds a sample color and finite Gram contexts.
Singular/symmetric configurations are allowed because no inverse sample
Gram bound occurs in the rules.

## 11. Experimental pressure test

The deterministic witnesses show that (11) cannot hold for arbitrary
energy-ball perturbations.  The preregistered G2/G3 probes test the precise
restriction CCTC makes:

- through width `1024`, the paired block has approximately quadratic local
  defect on Gaussian-reachable trajectories;
- iterating it to `T=0.5` gives approximately first-order global error with
  stable constants;
- pure arctangent is at least as well behaved as the tested leaky variants.

This is positive evidence for a causal occupation estimate.  It supplies no
part of the forest proof.

## 12. Architecture decision under this program

CCTC currently favors the vanilla pure-arctangent MLP.

- Pure arctangent keeps every feature bounded and gives global deterministic
  energy majorants.
- Its logarithmic derivative `d'/d` is bounded, which produces (5)--(8).
- A leaky linear component makes mobility transport bi-Lipschitz but does not
  remove backward rare-coordinate instability; it also loses bounded
  features and permits finite-time norm blow-up on simple invariant rays.
- Residual/skip connections add identity traffic paths without removing the
  transverse gate occupation form.
- RMS, batch, group, or weight normalization introduces global denominators,
  new tangent projections, and additional source-dependent contexts.  No
  current estimate shows that those additions improve either (JC-p) or (FR).

Thus architectural modification would presently add proof obligations while
leaving the decisive source-aware forest estimate intact.  It should be
reconsidered only if the one-color arctangent forest gate fails for a reason
that a standard architectural invariant demonstrably removes.

## 13. Falsifiers and current status

CCTC is rejected or redesigned if any of the following occurs:

1. the causal-disk grammar omits a strict-leading first-variation primitive,
   or a fixed-port family violates (JC-p) after all symmetries are treated
   jointly;
2. a degree-two response chain cannot be bounded before its claimed signed
   resummation without assuming the desired response stability;
3. the finite-width collision sector remains order one in probability after
   the width-first, response-grade truncation required by (FR);
4. an unsuppressed same-source loop is neither width-deficient nor represented
   by the fixed type grammar;
5. the fixed extra occupation ports destroy summability (the already proved
   quadratic-exponential growth in an unbounded number of ports is not by
   itself a falsifier, because that route has been removed);
6. paired-block local defects require maximum-coordinate rather than causal
   source cost;
7. two consistent finite-step compilers converge to different characters;
8. adding the second source color at `L=3` creates a qualitatively new
   zero-defect primitive.

What is proved now: the finite-step compiler identities, exact tangent and
Hessian reduction, deterministic no-go results, fixed-program semantics,
resolved ladder by earlier completion rules, and the G2/G3 empirical
discriminators.

What is open: completion of (JC-p), (FR), their occupation-port and multilinear versions,
the CCTC-only recovery of the nonlinear `L=2` rung, and therefore the `L=3`
limit.  The original *uniform-port* proposal has been falsified, while a
fixed-port square-root-factorial leading theorem survives.  The two-scale
replacement is a candidate only: until the trajectory and remainder gates have independent,
noncircular proofs, CCTC may still be the original convergence problem written
as a diagrammatic hierarchy rather than a genuine completion calculus.
