# CFPC Evidence Ledger

## Ledger rules

Every entry is tagged as one of:

- **E**: exact finite-width identity or directly checked deterministic fact;
- **P**: established theorem with hypotheses checked for the stated use;
- **C**: conditional implication with explicit unmet premises;
- **H**: heuristic or machinery proposal;
- **X**: falsified route or false claim;
- **O**: open obligation.

An entry is promoted only when its proof and dependencies are recorded.  A formal equation is not promoted merely because it has the desired shape.

## Current frontier

The exploratory audit is complete.  The linear ladder, generic nonlinear
one-layer rung, and Gaussian-seed two-layer arctangent theorem are
mathematically established.  The last theorem uses mobility flattening and
seed clipping and therefore does not pass the separate machinery-only probe.
No proposed extensible calculus recovered the untailored two-layer rung.

Every candidate now exposes the same unresolved object: a width-uniform
same-disorder stability/occupation estimate restricted to the actual forced
causal defect cone, together with a finite or effectively truncatable
one-time source-action state.  Ambient versions are falsified.  Marked
cavity and random averaging work on independent or typical directions, but
adaptive source-row alignment defeats their uniform forms.  Hidden
backpropagates have a natural finite-width subexponential, not subgaussian,
tail class, and positive square-exponential moments are infinite at
initialization.

Operator/Dyson, entropy, weak compactness, typed renormalization,
Gevrey/Borel continuation, and gradient geometry all contribute exact
identities but fail as genuine reductions.  In particular, the internal
pullback cometric has irreducible full spectral bulk at initialization and
full-rank activation motion; low-rank learned updates do not yield a
finite-state closure.

The recommended future machinery is a dynamical marked-cavity /
forced-causal-response semigroup theory with MFP as its finite-mesh compiler.
Its central propagation theorem is open.  No depth-three convergence theorem
or completed autonomous IDE is claimed; see
[Machinery Verdict and Exact Proof Frontier](MACHINERY_VERDICT_AND_PROOF_FRONTIER.md).
