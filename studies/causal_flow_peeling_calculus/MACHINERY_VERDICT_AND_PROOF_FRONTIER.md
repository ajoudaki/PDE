# Machinery Verdict and Exact Proof Frontier

## Executive verdict

The proposed “MFP with richer nonlinear nodes and local flow-error
certificates” has **not** become the requested calculus.  The exploratory
program fails its mandatory untailored two-hidden-layer arctangent gate, so it
cannot honestly be applied as a theorem-producing machine at depth three.
The depth-three compact-time limit and autonomous IDE remain open.

This is not merely a failure to find one final estimate.  Every attempted
completion rule eventually needs the same missing object:

> a width-uniform stability theorem for **forced, causally generated
> perturbations** of the reused Gaussian-source flow, together with an
> effectively truncatable current-time representation of all source actions
> needed by those perturbations.

Ambient stability is false.  Ordinary nodewise current state is insufficient.
Finite fixed-order peeling does not control arbitrarily many time steps.
Spectral, entropy, Gevrey, compactness, and low-rank reformulations do not
remove the causal-source action; they re-encode it.

The user's high-level diagnosis is therefore correct: the missing ingredient
is a substantial machinery, not a cute local lemma.  But the particular
proposal of merely relaxing the MFP normal form is not that machinery.  MFP
is an exact **compiler and static evaluator** inside the eventual proof; it is
not the dynamical completion engine.

The most credible future engine is a rigorous **dynamical marked-cavity /
forced-causal-response semigroup theory**, with MFP used only after a finite
mesh and finite mark cutoff are fixed.  That program is concrete and
falsifiable, and several of its atoms are proved.  Its central propagation
theorem has not been proved here, so this report does not present it as a
solution.

## 1. What MFP contributes, exactly

MFP takes a fixed finite-width scalar contraction, exposes all neuron indices
and shared Gaussian sources, and eliminates source matrices one layer at a
time by exact Wick--Stein differentiation.  A forward use and a transpose use
of one matrix retain the same source coordinates; response contractions are
not optional corrections and cannot be replaced by independent Gaussian
copies.  Equality partitions and global width valuation are performed before
discarding branches.

For each separately fixed finite program, the resulting tensor-program
semantics gives joint Gaussian normal forms and convergence of the requested
scalar observables.  This is enough for:

- any fixed derivative order;
- any fixed number of Euler or gradient steps;
- exact source reuse and singular covariance configurations;
- fixed-depth response programs of substantial but finite complexity.

It does not give constants uniform in a program of length \(T/h\) as
\(h\downarrow0\).  Adding a node that stores an arbitrary recursively
transformed signal merely hides:

- the growth of its response registry;
- adaptive source/transpose tails;
- nonlinear product continuity;
- local \(O(h^2)\) consistency;
- stable accumulation of defects;
- projective consistency across meshes;
- positive-time restartability.

Consequently, “nonlinear MFP nodes plus an error annotation” is a data
structure, not a proof calculus, until those annotations are generated by a
closed theorem.

## 2. The exact missing theorem

Let \(Y_n(t)\) be the finite-width state and let
\(U_n(t,s)\) be the chronological tangent propagator.  Let
\(\mathcal C_n\) denote a recursively generated class of causal perturbations:
initial empirical/source defects, finite-program truncation defects, Euler
defects, and the marked leave-one-out differences created by the proof.
The class must be defined syntactically from source marks and network
operations, not retrospectively as “directions on which the estimate holds.”

A sufficient core theorem would provide typed spaces
\(\mathcal E_+\hookrightarrow\mathcal E\) and, for every \(T,p<\infty\),
a width-independent bound

\[
\left\|
\sup_{0\le s\le t\le T}
\left\|
U_n(t,s)v+
\int_s^tU_n(t,\tau)r(\tau)\,d\tau
\right\|_{\mathcal E}
\right\|_{L^p}
\le
C_{T,p}
\left(
\|v\|_{\mathcal E_+}
+\int_s^T\|r(\tau)\|_{\mathcal E_+}\,d\tau
\right)
\tag{1}
\]

for all \((v,r)\in\mathcal C_n\), together with:

1. **reachable acceleration**
   \[
   \sup_n\left\|\sup_{t\le T}\|\ddot Y_n(t)\|_{\mathcal E}\right\|_{L^p}
   <\infty;
   \tag{2}
   \]
2. **effective source-action truncation:** a finite marked/program state of
   complexity \(M\) whose causal residual is at most
   \(a_M(T)+C_M(T)n^{-1/2}\), with \(a_M(T)\to0\);
3. **forced-tube stability:** (1) remains true for the near-reachable
   trajectories produced by discretization and truncation;
4. **restart consistency:** the truncated state at an intermediate time can
   be used as new initial data with a defect tending to zero.

Equations (1)--(2) would give

\[
\sup_{kh\le T}d(Y_n(kh),Y_M(kh))
\le C_T\left[
d(Y_n(0),Y_M(0))+T\{h+a_M(T)+C_M(T)n^{-1/2}\}
\right].
\tag{3}
\]

After \(M,h\) are fixed, MFP evaluates the finite program.  One then takes
\(n\to\infty\), \(h\to0\), and \(M\to\infty\) in a controlled order.
This is the clean division of labor that the desired calculus would need.

The hard part is (1), not (3).  Calling (1) a lemma without a recursive proof
would be exactly the prohibited restatement of the original problem.

## 3. Why the missing theorem has this form

For arctangent,

\[
\kappa(z)=\frac{\phi''(z)}{\phi'(z)}
=-\frac{2z}{1+z^2},
\qquad |\kappa(z)|\le1.
\tag{4}
\]

Grouping a differentiated gate before estimating it gives

\[
\delta b_\ell
=D_\ell\,\delta r_\ell
+\kappa(z_\ell)b_\ell\odot\delta z_\ell.
\tag{5}
\]

The exact parameter Hessian therefore reduces its non-energy-bounded part to
weighted occupation terms of the form

\[
\sum_\ell
\left\langle |b_\ell|,|\delta z_\ell|^2\right\rangle_n.
\tag{6}
\]

For an upstream perturbation, the same structure appears as a weighted return
operator

\[
D_ub_1
=\sum_k
P_k^T\operatorname{diag}\!\bigl(\phi''(z_k)r_k\bigr)P_k,
\tag{7}
\]

where the \(P_k\) here are forward sensitivity products.  Fixed directions
independent of the relevant source and the first true initialization defect
obey dimension-free annealed estimates.  Arbitrary energy-bounded directions
do not: a source-row-aligned unit-energy direction produces
\(\sqrt{\log n}\) amplification.

This proves that the right theorem must be anisotropic.  It must control the
particular defects generated by the algorithm and proof, not the ambient
operator norm.

Gradient geometry gives a second exact view.  If
\(D_\ell=\operatorname{diag}\phi'(z_\ell)\), define

\[
\mathsf P_1=I,\qquad
\mathsf P_{\ell+1}
=\|x_\ell\|_n^2I+
G_\ell D_\ell\mathsf P_\ell D_\ell G_\ell^T.
\tag{8}
\]

Then

\[
\dot z_\ell=\mathsf P_\ell b_\ell,\qquad
\Theta_n=\|x_D\|_n^2+
\langle b_D,\mathsf P_Db_D\rangle_n.
\tag{9}
\]

The mobility conjugate
\(D_\ell^{-1}\mathsf P_\ell D_\ell\) is positive and self-adjoint in the
\(D_\ell^2\) metric.  This removes a coordinate condition number along one
trajectory, but it does not compare two trajectories with different
connections.

At depth three, \(\mathsf P_2\) contains at initialization

\[
W=\Gamma_1D_1^2\Gamma_1^T.
\tag{10}
\]

If \(q=\phi'(u)^2\), then

\[
\tau(W)\to\mathbb E q,\qquad
\tau(W^2)\to(\mathbb E q)^2+\mathbb E q^2,
\tag{11}
\]

so its distance from every scalar remains order one.  Activation motion
changes \(W\) by a generically full-rank term.  Thus low-rank learned
increments do not compress the internal pullback bulk.  Tracking finitely
many actions of that bulk generates the response/Krylov hierarchy whose
causal stability is exactly (1).

## 4. Calibration ladder: actual outcome

| Rung | Mathematical status | Did the new general completion machinery recover it? |
|---|---|---|
| Linear \(D=1,2,3\) | Compact-time finite-width convergence and the scalar majorant are proved. | Yes, by uniform finite-program/Picard rules. |
| Generic nonlinear \(D=1\) | Coordinate-flow pushforward and observable convergence are proved under explicit regularity/moment assumptions. | Yes; no adaptive matrix query occurs. |
| Arctangent \(D=2\) | A full compact-time theorem is proved in this study by mobility flattening, seed clipping, Euler stability, and fixed-program MFP. | **No for the requested probe.** That proof uses the special mobility/seed structure that the probe was instructed not to reuse as its completion engine. |
| Arctangent \(D=3\) | Formal equations, exact identities, and several conditional reductions exist. | No convergence theorem and no autonomous IDE. |

The distinction in the third row is essential.  The unconditional
two-hidden-layer theorem remains valid evidence about the model.  It is not
evidence that the proposed new recursive calculus passed its own ladder gate.
Every general candidate failed before reproducing that result without the
tailored route.

Because the mandatory depth-two gate failed, proceeding to a purported
depth-three “calculation” would have violated the frozen contract.  The probe
therefore stops with a negative machinery verdict rather than manufacturing
a conditional answer.

## 5. Established reusable results

The following are proved at their stated scope.

1. Fixed finite MFP programs admit exact same-source forward/transpose
   peeling and fixed-program Gaussian evaluation.
2. Deep linear \(D=1,2,3\), generic nonlinear \(D=1\), and Gaussian-seed
   arctangent \(D=2\) have the compact-time limits recorded above.
3. Arctangent mobility
   \(R(z)=z+z^3/3\) removes a bottom activation factor, and
   \(|\phi''/\phi'|\le1\) gives the grouped tangent identity (5).
4. Predictor ascent and the raw kernel satisfy
   \(\dot p=\Theta_n\); triangular energy/operator estimates give
   compact-time size bounds.
5. The pullback recursion (8)--(9), the flux identities, the nonlinear
   bottom invariant, and the intrinsic cap connection are exact at every
   finite width.
6. Each learned matrix increment is trace class:
   \[
   \|\Delta G_\ell(t)\|_*
   \le\int_0^t\|b_{\ell+1}(s)\|_n\|x_\ell(s)\|_n\,ds,
   \]
   with best rank-\(m\) Frobenius error at most \(V/\sqrt{m+1}\).
7. Internal depth-three backpropagates have a natural finite-width
   subexponential rather than subgaussian class.  At initialization,
   \(\|r_{2i}\|_{L^p}\le Cp\), while
   \(\mathbb E e^{\lambda r_{2i}^2}=\infty\) for every \(\lambda>0\).
8. A two-star dynamical cavity exactly separates independent upper/lower
   innovations around one marked hidden neuron while preserving its
   order-one causal response.
9. A fixed-port, strict-leading one-color response-forest estimate is proved.
   Its resummation uniformly in port number is false.
10. Predictor trajectories are compact; weak matrix/update compactness is
    available.  This does not identify source actions or give raw-kernel
    equicontinuity.

## 6. Routes falsified or killed

| Proposed engine | Exact useful residue | Fatal gate |
|---|---|---|
| Universal Wiener-chaos algebra | Static expansions and Malliavin identities | Arctangent coefficients are only root-exponential, while multiplication of normalized Hermite modes has exponential top-chaos growth. |
| Ordinary Koopman/Taylor continuation | Every fixed time derivative is an MFP program | Already at \(D=1\), Gaussian readout seeds force coefficient roots growing like \(\sqrt{k}\); no width-uniform analytic radius. |
| Finite adaptive-query compression | Exact conditional Gaussian innovation rules | Fixed query count does not approximate the unseen source block in operator norm; compression constants explode as tolerance vanishes; restart sufficiency is unproved. |
| Ambient energy/operator stability | Compact-time state-size bounds | A bounded one-row perturbation realizes \(\max_i|A_i\phi''(z_i)|\asymp\sqrt{\log n}\). |
| Absolute chronological forests | A genuine fixed-port leading theorem | Port factors produce \(p^k\); the sharp resummation grows like \(p!\exp(Cp^2)\). |
| Laminar colored BBGKY | Exact autonomous finite-width traffic hierarchy | Depth three creates leading alternating two-color caps, not laminar trees. |
| Square-exponential marked cavity | Exact two-star cavity and exponential-linear formulas | The proposed square-exponential response moment is infinite already at initialization. |
| Random-matrix smoothing | Annealed bounds for independent and first causal directions | Source-row-aligned directions destroy uniform smoothing; Malliavin contraction regenerates the response hierarchy. |
| Entropy/LDP transport | Exact Stein-cancelled entropy identity | Ambient Jacobian grows with width, while the needed log-determinant/tangent spectral law is stronger than causal stability. |
| Operator Dyson/local laws | Exact Bethe--Salpeter recursion and cap resolvent | Bulk spectra miss rank-one marked learning channels; the adaptive Schwinger--Dyson remainder contains the chronological tangent. |
| Weak compactness/monotonicity | Predictor subsequences and weak update limits | Current ordinary nodes are not restart-sufficient; an explicit transverse perturbation leaves them fixed but changes their derivative. The scalar Hessian is indefinite. |
| Typed Wick renormalization | Exact fixed-expression colored compiler | Centered Wishart returns remain order one at every fixed word length; no subcritical width grade, and ambient defect stability is false. |
| Banach-scale Gevrey/Borel completion | Honest response-tail bookkeeping and a solvable Stieltjes toy | Loss exponent \(>1\) does not imply evolution; Gevrey coefficients do not identify a function; common sectorial continuation and tangent control are new theorems. |
| Gradient invariants/low-rank motion | Pullback cometrics, bottom invariant, trace-class update tail | The internal cometric has irreducible full bulk and full-rank activation motion. |
| Direct invocation of rigorous DMFT | Valuable Euler/discretization/cavity architecture | Available theorems assume a single design and globally Lipschitz update/Jacobian structure, and return a two-time state; the network violates these hypotheses. |

These are route-specific negative results.  They do not disprove the
depth-three limit.

## 7. Empirical evidence and its exact weight

The preregistered numerical probes are consistent with a causal-cone theorem,
but none supplies it.

- The exact frozen-input gate block has approximately quadratic one-step
  error on sampled Gaussian-reachable depth-three states through width 1024,
  with no visible width growth.
- Iterating that block gives approximately first-order global error through
  width 1024 for pure and leaky arctangent.
- Replacing one source column gives order-one response at the marked neuron
  and approximately \(n^{-1/2}\) influence on the bulk.
- Sampled depth-three \(r_2\) moments and tails look stable with width and
  show no visible self-clip inflation on the tested grid.
- The locked response-occupation experiment found no positive rare-coordinate
  alignment in typical samples, but its simultaneous width-exponent gate was
  inconclusive.

The exact infinite square-exponential moment overrides any tempting
subgaussian interpretation of these finite samples.  The experiments support
the hypothesis that typical causal directions are much better behaved than
worst ambient directions; they do not prove uniform integrability, rare-event
control, forced-tube stability, or a width limit.

## 8. The machinery I would build next

The best rational bet is not a broader node language by itself.  It is a
six-module **Dynamical Marked-Cavity and Causal-Response Calculus**.

### Module A: source-aware current-time algebra

Use finitely many field/operator types, immutable Gaussian source colors,
learned trace-class increments, normalized contractions, arctangent gates,
and marked source actions.  The limiting state is a positive character on
this typed algebra completed in a certified causal norm.  The exact finite
flow induces a local derivation, so autonomy is present at the algebraic
level from the start.

### Module B: finite-mesh MFP compiler

For fixed time mesh and fixed mark cutoff, compile every observable and local
defect into a finite same-source program.  MFP performs all Gaussian
evaluation.  This module is already essentially available.

### Module C: multi-mark dynamical leave-one-out

Delete the incident source row/column around a finite set of marked neurons,
couple the cavity and original trajectories with the same remaining disorder,
and condition on the cavity filtration.  This gives exact fresh innovations
without pretending that an adaptively queried matrix is fresh.  Induct on
source color, mark count, and depth.

### Module D: subexponential packet and occupation estimates

Propagate full \(L^p\) profiles of the form \(Cp\), not impossible
square-exponential bounds.  Use the grouped arctangent identity (5) and
Hölder/Yudovich optimization to turn these profiles into an Osgood modulus.
The target is the weighted occupation (6), conditional on successively
deleted source rows/columns.  Signs and conditional centering must be retained;
an absolute forest bound is known to be too large.

### Module E: forced causal tangent theorem

Prove (1) simultaneously for exact marked differences, Euler defects, and
finite-program truncation defects.  The proof may use a two-time response
kernel internally in an exponentially weighted Volterra norm, as rigorous
DMFT proofs do.  That kernel is proof scaffolding, not the final state.  The
result must be uniform in every artificial hidden clip.

### Module F: semigroup completion and effective restart

Use Module E to compare meshes and mark cutoffs on the same source
probability space.  Establish a Cauchy semigroup on the completed one-time
character state, show that its local derivation is the IDE vector field, and
obtain an explicit truncation modulus.  Predictor and raw-kernel convergence
then follow by continuity of their algebra elements.

This design is not science fiction: every module has an exact finite-width
definition; Modules A--C have substantial proved atoms; the required output
of Modules D--E is a falsifiable inequality.  It is also not yet a proof.
Module E, fed by the occupation estimate in D, is the genuinely new research
theory.  If it cannot be proved at the untailored depth-two arctangent rung,
the entire program should be abandoned before depth three.

## 9. Why this is preferable to the alternatives

A dynamic operator-valued traffic/free-probability theory would be more
expressive, especially for the full pullback bulk (10), but the marked
isotropic local law it needs has the chronological tangent in its remainder.
It is therefore less constructable today.

Gevrey/Malliavin completion is attractive as an all-order language, but its
abstract evolution theorem is false without extra smoothing or sectorial
structure.  It should be used for finite-order bookkeeping inside the cavity
proof, not as the completion engine.

Gradient geometry should also remain inside the proof: (8)--(9) identifies
the correct cap and (6) the correct occupation.  It does not compress the
state.

Rigorous DMFT should not be ignored categorically.  Rigorous discrete and
continuous theorems exist for narrower one-design systems, including
dynamical leave-one-out response identification.  They cannot be invoked for
the present network, but their discretization and Volterra-contraction proofs
are the closest mature templates:

- [Gerbelot et al., discrete rigorous DMFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06591);
- [Celentano--Cheng--Montanari, continuous-time rigorous DMFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.07572);
- [Fan et al., adaptive Langevin cavity and response](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.15556).

Tensor Programs/MFP remains the appropriate rigorous evaluator after the
program is frozen; it does not replace Modules D--F.

## 10. Recommended order of future gates

1. Reprove single-input arctangent \(D=2\) using only Modules A--F.  The
   existing mobility/seed-clipping theorem may be used as an answer key, not
   as a proof ingredient.
2. Test two-input symmetric and antisymmetric \(D=2\) cases.  They are useful
   audits of color bookkeeping and singular Gram handling, but should not
   precede the single-input theorem.
3. Prove the one-mark/two-source-color occupation theorem at \(D=3\), then the
   full single-input limit.
4. Test symmetric and semi-symmetric three-input cases before generic finite
   input.  No inverse-Gram lower bound should be permitted.
5. Formulate the depth induction only after the two-color alternating-cap
   step is controlled.  Adding a layer must add a source color, not a new
   analytic oracle.

Multi-input pressure tests cannot compensate for failure at single-input
\(D=2\); they should not consume effort until the base causal theorem exists.

## 11. Final claim-level assessment

- **Proved:** the finite-width identities, the earlier resolved ladder,
  multiple compactness/tail/invariant facts, exact MFP compilation, and the
  route-specific counterexamples recorded in the evidence ledger.
- **Falsified:** ambient stability, universal chaos closure, analytic
  Koopman completion, square-exponential cavity control, laminar response
  trees, subcritical width renormalization, and several finite-state
  compression proposals.
- **Empirically supported:** width-stable local/global gate errors and
  anisotropic marked influence on the tested finite grids.
- **Still open:** forced causal tangent/occupation stability, effective
  current-time source-action truncation, untailored \(D=2\) recovery by the
  new machinery, and therefore the \(D=3\) compact-time autonomous IDE.

The honest answer to “does the proposed MFP extension work?” is **no**.  The
probe improved the diagnosis substantially and identified exact reusable
atoms, but it did not construct the promised calculus.  The honest answer to
“is there a concrete larger program worth trying?” is **yes**: the
dynamical marked-cavity/forced-causal-response program above is the strongest
current candidate, with a precise first kill gate and no claim that its core
theorem has already been reduced away.
