# L2 One-Excess-Leaf and Port-Growth Audit

## Disposition

For a closed formal class containing every first-variation constructor of the
two-hidden-layer arctangent dynamics, exact forward/transpose returns,
recursive base-state substitutions, and adaptive gate differentiation, the
strict-leading-width forest has at most one unbounded readout-seed leaf per
dynamical vertex.  After joint gate packetization and tied-ribbon pruning,
this gives a square-root-factorial response-grade bound for every **fixed**
number of ports.

The uniform port estimate proposed in the first CCTC draft is false.  A
necessary `p^k` word factor resums to quadratic-exponential dependence on
the number of ports.  Therefore this result supports fixed-port occupation
estimates, but cannot by itself produce an all-order Orlicz envelope.

## 1. Closed first-variation grammar

At depth two write

\[
 c_1=\phi'(u),\quad e_1=\phi''(u),\qquad
 c_2=\phi'(z),\quad e_2=\phi''(z),
\]

and `b=D_(c_2)A`, `r=G^*b`.  The exact first variations are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \delta x&=D_{c_1}\delta u,\\
 \delta z&=\delta G\,x+GD_{c_1}\delta u,\\
 \delta b&=D_{c_2}\delta A+D_{Ae_2}\delta z,\\
 \delta r&=\delta G^*b+G^*\delta b,
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]

and differentiation of the three base equations gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \delta\dot A&=D_{c_2}(\delta G\,x+GD_{c_1}\delta u),\\
 \delta\dot G&=
 (D_{c_2}\delta A+D_{Ae_2}(\delta G\,x+GD_{c_1}\delta u))
       \otimes_nx
 +b\otimes_nD_{c_1}\delta u,\\
 \delta\dot u&=D_{c_1}(\delta G^*b+G^*D_{c_2}\delta A
       +G^*D_{Ae_2}(\delta G\,x+GD_{c_1}\delta u))
       +D_{e_1r}\delta u.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

The forest class is generated by choosing individual summands of (1)--(2),
recursively substituting the base flow, expanding to the immutable Gaussian
seeds, retaining exact tied `G/G^*` ribbons, grouping all hits on a common
gate before absolute values, and finally keeping the globally zero-width-
defect sector.

## 2. Structural leaf lemma

Let `N(F)` count dynamical vertices and `a(F)` count unbounded `A_0` leaves;
structural matrix ribbons are not coordinate occupations.  Then

\[
 \boxed{a(F)\le N(F).}
\tag{3}
\]

Indeed every individual base or first-variation summand contains at most one
current `A`.  The two worst cells,

\[
 D_{c_1}G^*D_{Ae_2}GD_{c_1},
 \qquad D_{e_1G^*D_{c_2}A},
\]

contain exactly one.  Expanding a current `A` ends at one `A_0` or replaces
it by the integral of bounded `phi(z)`.  Expanding a learned matrix update can
introduce one new `A`, but simultaneously introduces a new dynamical vertex.
Gate vertices add no `A` leaves.  Induction proves (3) before, hence after,
leading-width pruning.

## 3. Exact tied-ribbon recurrence

The source covariance is

\[
 \mathbb E[G_{ij}(G^*)_{ab}]
 =n^{-1}\delta_{ib}\delta_{ja};
\tag{4}
\]

the two colors reverse exactly.  In the causal disk embedding, expose the
latest structural ribbon.  A leading-width partner is noncrossing; deleting
the covariance chord either removes an adjacent exact backtrack or splits the
disk into two smaller disks.  Thus, with finitely many typed orientations,

\[
 R_0=1,\qquad
 R_m\le Q\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}R_jR_{m-1-j},
\]

so `R_m<=Q^m Cat_m<=(4Q)^m`.  For a fixed number `p` of boundaries, the
boundary-connection factor is at most `C^p p!`.  Exact transpose reuse is
therefore retained without adding a second response factorial.

This is a theorem of the stated causal-disk formal class.  Applying it to
another compiler requires a completeness check that every leading diagram
inherits that embedding.

## 4. Gate packetization

At initialization, conditional on `x=phi(u_0)`, each row
`z_i=(G_0x)_i` is Gaussian with variance `q_n=<x,x>_n`.  The exact row
regression

\[
 (G_0)_{i\cdot}=\frac{z_i}{nq_n}x^T+h_i,
\qquad
 \operatorname{Cov}(h_i)=\frac1n
 \left(I-\frac{xx^T}{nq_n}\right)
\tag{5}
\]

turns every integration-by-parts hit on that row's gates into a longitudinal
Hermite leg; the residual ribbons obey the Catalan recurrence.

If equalities collect several gates into one row block `B`, expand their
product `F_B` in Hermite polynomials.  Parseval gives

\[
 \sum_m m!|\widehat F_{B,m}|^2
 =\mathbb E|F_B(\sqrt{q_*}Z)|^2\le C^{2|B|}.
\tag{6}
\]

The Taylor factor `1/m!` for indistinguishable gate hits cancels the Hermite
norm.  At an internal gate of arity `d`, sibling multiplicities similarly
produce the exact unordered Taylor denominator.  Since
`|arctan^(r)|<=(r-1)!`, the gate generating functions are bounded by

\[
 -\log(1-Y),\qquad (1-Y)^{-1},\qquad(1-Y)^{-2},
\tag{7}
\]

and have a positive local combinatorial radius.  Separating the raw
derivative factorial from its symmetry factor gives the false estimate in
the earlier audit.

## 5. Fixed-port chronological bound

The chronological tree volume is

\[
 \operatorname{Vol}_T(\tau)
 =\frac{T^{|\tau|}}{\prod_{v\in\tau}|\tau_v|}
 =\frac{e(\tau)T^{|\tau|}}{|\tau|!},
\tag{8}
\]

so the number of linear extensions remains coupled to the tree grammar.  The
finite constructor set and (7)--(8) give exponential total gate/tree mass at
each fixed response grade.

In the doubled diagram, (3) leaves at most `2N` Gaussian `A_0` leaves.  The
equality-pair recurrence `E_N<=(2N-1)E_(N-1)` gives `(2N-1)!!`; taking the
doubled-diagram square root gives

\[
 \mathfrak M_{1,N}\le C_T^N\sqrt{(2N-1)!!}
 \le C_T^N\sqrt{2^NN!}.
\tag{9}
\]

With `p` active chronological ports, every insertion may choose any port.
The correct word and time-integrated bounds are

\[
 \widehat{\mathfrak M}_{p,k}
 \le C^{p+k}p!\,p^k\sqrt{k!},
\tag{10}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak M_{p,k}(T)
 \le C_T^pp!\frac{(C_TpT)^k}{\sqrt{k!}}.
 }
\tag{11}
\]

For each fixed `p`, the response series is summable.  Uniformly in `p`, its
sum grows like `p! exp(C_Tp^2)` up to polynomial factors.

## 6. Sharp port counterexample

The strict-limit cavity field at a marked lower coordinate is Gaussian:

\[
 R_j^{(j)}=\sum_iG_{ij}\beta_i,\qquad
 D_j=\phi''((u_0)_j)R_j^{(j)}.
\]

The exact diagonal tangent constructor contains
`delta dot u_j=D_j delta u_j`.  For `p` ports sharing that context, every
chronological insertion chooses one of the `p` ports, and all `p^k` words
have the same scalar factor `D_j^k`.  Hence

\[
 \widehat{\mathfrak M}_{p,k}
 \ge p^kc^k\sqrt{k!}.
\tag{12}
\]

After resummation this subfamily is `exp(pTD_j)`, with `L^2` norm
`exp(Theta(p^2T^2))`.  Thus the port growth in (10)--(11) is necessary.

## Consequence for the program

The strict-leading, fixed-port L2 response grammar has passed a genuinely
nontrivial combinatorial gate.  This may be enough for a fixed number of
occupation/defect ports.  It does **not** establish finite-width response-tail
control, noncircular signed-chain resummation, compact-time convergence, or an
all-order marginal moment envelope.  In particular, the false uniform-port
bound cannot be used to deduce Yudovich--Orlicz stability.  Those obligations
must be discharged by a different, sign-preserving or cavity-level theorem.
