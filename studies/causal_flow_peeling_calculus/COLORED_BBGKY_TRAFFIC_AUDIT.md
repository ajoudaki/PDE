# Colored BBGKY / Traffic-Hierarchy Candidate: Depth-Three Audit

## Verdict

A one-time BBGKY-style hierarchy of rooted traffic observables is exact and
autonomous at finite width.  The natural depth-two proof grammar is a
one-color chronological forest.  Its hoped-for depth-three extension by
laminar monochromatic components is false: the actual chain rule creates
leading-order alternating response/cap squares.  They are neither
width-deficient collisions nor trees.

Retaining arbitrary alternating response complexes gives formal closure, but
bounding their signed propagator is the original reachable-stability problem;
bounding their absolute mass is stronger and may be false.  The route is
therefore rejected as an all-depth completion machinery.

## 1. Exact differential hierarchy

For arctangent put

\[
 q(z)=\phi'(z)=\frac1{1+z^2},
 \qquad
 a(z)=\frac{q'(z)}{q(z)}=-\frac{2z}{1+z^2}.
\]

Both `q` and `a` are bounded by one in absolute value.  If

\[
 Q_l=\|x_l\|_n^2,
 \qquad R_l=\|b_l\|_n^2,
\]

then the exact forward identities are

\[
 \dot z_1=b_1,
 \qquad
 \dot x_l=q_l\odot\dot z_l,
\]

\[
 \dot z_{l+1}
 =Q_lb_{l+1}+G_l(q_l\odot\dot z_l).
\tag{1}
\]

At the top,

\[
 \dot b_D=q_D\odot x_D
 +a_D\odot b_D\odot\dot z_D,
\tag{2}
\]

and below it,

\[
 \dot b_l
 =a_l\odot b_l\odot\dot z_l
 +q_l\odot\left(G_l^\top\dot b_{l+1}
                 +R_{l+1}x_l\right).
\tag{3}
\]

Equations (1)--(3), together with

\[
 \dot G_l=b_{l+1}\otimes_nx_l,
\]

differentiate every finite typed traffic cylinder into a finite sum of typed
cylinders.  Hence they define an exact one-time graded hierarchy

\[
 \dot\Gamma=\mathcal L_D^*\Gamma.
\tag{4}
\]

Formal autonomy is not the missing point.  The missing point is a topology in
which (4) is unique and its finite truncations converge.

## 2. The proposed boardgame estimate

Let `C_(m,k)^(D)` be the response-decorated, `(D-1)`-color collision
histories generated by `k` Duhamel applications from a normalized grade-`m`
test.  A BBGKY uniqueness proof would require a bound of the form

\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}
 \sup_{0<t_k<\cdots<t_1<T}
 \sum_{C\in\mathfrak C_{m,k}^{(D)}}
 |\operatorname{Amp}_n(C;t_1,\ldots,t_k)|
 \le C_{D,T}^{m+k}k!.
\tag{5}
\]

The ordered time simplex would cancel `k!`; subdivision into intervals with
`C_(D,T) delta<1` would then give hierarchy uniqueness, truncation tails, and
restartability without an ambient finite-width Lipschitz estimate.

For one source color, MFP's chronological response rules are compatible with
a rooted-forest grammar.  That observation motivates (5), but does not by
itself prove the full trajectory and finite-width remainder estimates.

## 3. Exact depth-three obstruction

At depth three,

\[
 b_{2,j}
 =q_{2,j}\sum_kG_{2,kj}b_{3,k},
 \qquad
 b_{3,k}=A_kq_{3,k},
\]

\[
 z_{3,k}=\sum_{j'}G_{2,kj'}x_{2,j'}.
\]

When a `G_1` response differentiates through `b_3`, the chain rule contains

\[
 \boxed{
 q_{2,j}\sum_{k,j'}
 G_{2,kj}A_kq'_{3,k}
 G_{2,kj'}q_{2,j'}x_{1,i}.}
\tag{6}
\]

The two `G_2` occurrences in (6) form a cap joining the layer-two indices
`j` and `j'`.  Each matrix factor contributes scale `n^-1/2`, while the
shared `k` sum restores a factor `n`; the cap is therefore leading order.
It is not an excess equality or genus defect that vanishes with width.

Repeating the same operation through the backward `G_1^T` contraction
creates alternating complexes

\[
 G_1\text{-response}
 \;--\;G_2\text{-cap}
 \;--\;G_1\text{-response}
 \;--\;G_2\text{-cap},
\]

including squares.  Thus leading two-color diagrams are not laminar trees of
monochromatic components.

## 4. Consequences

The following proposed implications are invalid:

1. extra color interactions always cost a width power;
2. a layer can be stripped after contracting an independent monochromatic
   leaf;
3. one-color boardgame counting extends by merely coloring its edges;
4. all fixed depth changes only an exponential combinatorial constant.

One can enlarge (4) to store every alternating complex.  But then the
missing estimate is precisely (5).  Taking absolute values destroys possible
sign cancellations and may demand more than the actual theorem.  Retaining
signs turns the sum into the norm of the true linearized propagator, which is
the original mesh-stability bridge by duality.

This is a genuine falsifier of the proposed machinery, not evidence against
the existence of the network limit.
