There is a consequential checkpoint-specific decision, but the present closure does not yet supply a convincing practical advantage. The strongest candidate is **choosing when to rehearse old examples while correcting a particular pretrained model**. Its plausible advantage is lower memory for many counterfactual continuations or long differentiation histories. It is not a cheaper forward simulator. I would not invest in a general checkpoint-control platform on the current evidence.

Consider an expensive, already trained smooth deep regression surrogate whose response in a newly encountered operating regime is wrong. A small corrective dataset is available, together with a small rehearsal set representing previously reliable regimes. Fix the actual checkpoint, full-parameter continuation algorithm, training budget, and evaluation distributions. The decision is the timing and allocation of rehearsal during correction: early protection, late repair, or interleaving. Select the schedule that minimizes held-out new-regime risk subject to a specified maximum increase in held-out old-regime risk. Execute the selected schedule on the actual checkpoint and score that resulting learner. This is a concrete repair decision; reproducing the closure's own predictions is not success. Its practical importance is plausible, not demonstrated by any supplied application.

The exact model matters. The paper has a scalar-output multilayer network with smooth activations, squared loss, full-batch gradient flow and block mobilities \(\mathcal D=\operatorname{diag}(nI,I,\ldots,I,nI)\). Its hidden-weight update is
\[
\dot W^{(\ell)}=-\frac{2}{nm}\sum_a r_a\delta_a^{(\ell)}h_a^{(\ell-1)\top}.
\]
Here \(m\) is the fixed training-set size, \(n\) the hidden width, \(r_a=f(x_a)-y_a\), and \(\delta_a^{(\ell)}\) the residual-free backward response. At a trained checkpoint \(\theta_*\), set the retained matrices \(W_0^{(\ell)}=W_*^{(\ell)}\), retain the trained first layer/readout, and initialize new response memories. The integrated-update identity is unchanged. The fixed-width learning-speed theorem explicitly allows arbitrary finite initialization and gives compact-horizon \(O_{n,T}(q^{-1})\) tracking, for sufficiently large order \(q\). Thus pretrained geometry can be retained exactly without recovering pretraining history. This is an actual mathematical foothold. [Model and theorem](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/paper/main.tex:835).

However, the width-uniform all-time theorem assumes independent Gaussian initialization and exactly zero readout. A trained checkpoint violates these assumptions; small correction residuals do not restore them. Moreover, the learning-speed closure's zero backward prefix generally jumps at restart when the readout is nonzero. The joint clock uses matching backward prefixes and subtracts their initial product, recovering a fixed-width \(O_{n,T}(q^{-2})\) theorem under stronger smoothness, at extra Gram-matrix and directional-pass cost. Neither theorem gives a useful small order for an arbitrary checkpoint. [All-time hypotheses](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/paper/results.tex:18), [joint-clock construction](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/paper/main.tex:1097).

Temporal credit really can matter here. Write \(\mathcal L_A\) and \(\mathcal L_B\) for corrective and rehearsal losses, and \(F_A=-\mathcal D\nabla\mathcal L_A\), \(F_B=-\mathcal D\nabla\mathcal L_B\). A schedule \(u(t)\in[0,1]\) produces
\[
\dot\theta=u(t)F_A(\theta)+(1-u(t))F_B(\theta),\qquad\theta(0)=\theta_*.
\]
For smooth fields, applying \(A\) for duration \(s\), then \(B\) for \(s\), versus reversing them gives
\[
\theta_{AB}-\theta_{BA}
=s^2\{DF_B(\theta_*)F_A(\theta_*)-DF_A(\theta_*)F_B(\theta_*)\}+O(s^3).
\]
Thus equal exposure need not mean equal repair or forgetting. More generally, the first-order contribution of a schedule perturbation at time \(t\) to a terminal score \(J\) is
\[
\nabla J(\theta(T))^\top\Phi(T,t)
[F_A(\theta(t))-F_B(\theta(t))]\,\delta u(t)\,dt,
\]
where \(\partial_s\Phi(s,t)=D_\theta F_u(s,\theta(s))\Phi(s,t)\) and \(\Phi(t,t)=I\). The propagator carries early feature changes through all later training. These are exact sensitivity statements, not evidence that a particular application has a large useful effect. Ordering effects alone also do not establish nonlinear necessity: an appropriate linearized model can reproduce some of them.

Weighted, changing losses require extending the closure's forcing and its theorem; the supplied fixed-dataset theorem is not already a control theorem. Piecewise restarting is possible conceptually, but materializing a new dense base at every switch sacrifices the shared-base benefit. Keeping separate phase memories instead accumulates their ranks. A claimed implementation must account for one of these costs.

The strongest cheap competitors all receive the actual checkpoint:

| Competitor | What makes it serious | What remains distinguishable |
|---|---|---|
| Actual-checkpoint low-rank continuation | Retains all pretrained features; branches share the frozen base. Include trained adapters and an adaptive projection/integration of the dense update, not only a poorly initialized factor flow. | Euclidean factor training changes the update law. Closure could better rank schedules for the stipulated dense learner, but this must be shown at equal total resources. |
| Distillation or structural compression | Amortizes a smaller simulator over many candidate schedules. Give it feature or Jacobian matching and checkpoint-generated repair/rehearsal data where affordable. | Matching the present function does not identify its training vector field. But failure of ordinary output distillation does not defeat stronger dynamics-aware distillation. |
| Checkpoint linearization/influence | Uses the checkpoint's own tangent geometry. A small training set permits sample-space response computations; periodically refreshed Jacobians/Hessians strengthen the baseline. | Frozen coefficients can miss substantial evolving geometry. Local repair may be precisely where they suffice. |
| Truncated AD and exact AD with checkpointing | Differentiate actual-checkpoint training; tune truncation depth, include recomputation, and compare at matched memory/work. | Truncation drops early sensitivity. This matters only when its omitted propagated contribution remains consequential; long elapsed time alone proves nothing. |

These are established starting points: [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) freezes pretrained weights and trains factors; [GaLore](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03507) targets optimizer memory through gradient projection and should not be misrepresented as eliminating dense weights; [distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531) compresses predictive knowledge; [influence functions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/koh17a.html) use gradients/Hessian-vector products; [truncated backpropagation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/shaban19a.html) can work well with short differentiation windows. The proposed stronger variants and their limitations above are analytic assessments, not claims proved in those papers.

There is a sharp cost ceiling. Per hidden link, closure retains \(n^2\) checkpoint entries plus \(2mnq\) moving entries. Dense gradient flow can overwrite its \(n^2\) weights. For one sequential counterfactual, closure therefore does not reduce persistent parameter storage relative to in-place dense flow, and its RHS work remains
\[
O(Lmn^2+Lm^2nq+Lmnq+nmd).
\]
The dense checkpoint multiplication is still present; the learned factors add work. Any saved dense-update bandwidth is a constant-factor possibility requiring measurement, not an asymptotic speedup. The model has no Adam states whose removal could be counted as an existing benefit. [Resource accounting](/home/amir/Codes/PDE/paper/comparison_appendix.tex:124).

For \(B\) concurrent branches, shared checkpoint storage changes the comparison to roughly \(Ln^2+B\,2Lmnq\) versus \(BLn^2\), omitting common outer blocks/workspace. This is meaningful only when \(2mq\ll n\), and low-rank checkpoint adaptation enjoys the same sharing. Here \(m\) includes **all distinct rehearsal/correction examples whose histories are retained**, not minibatch size. A realistic rehearsal corpus can remove the advantage immediately. At \(m=1000,n=1024\), even \(q=1\) has more hidden moving coordinates than dense weights.

For long-horizon differentiation, fewer evolving coordinates could reduce saved trajectory/sensitivity state. But an autonomous finite-dimensional ODE does not give free reverse-mode gradients: its trajectory still needs storage, recomputation, or an adjoint solver. Crucially, prediction/parameter convergence does not imply convergence of intervention derivatives. A new sensitivity estimate or reliable derivative-free ranking guarantee is required. The two-candidate “early versus late” problem is especially unfavorable: just run both dense continuations sequentially.

The narrow surviving opportunity is therefore **many memory-limited counterfactual schedules on one checkpoint, a genuinely small union of adaptation examples, substantial feature evolution, and important early credit beyond a cheap truncation window**. Response memory has a distinctive justification there: it approximates the stipulated dense flow while retaining all checkpoint geometry, rather than merely offering another adaptation parameterization. No current result establishes that all these conditions overlap in a consequential application, that the required order remains small, or that it beats adaptive low-rank actual-checkpoint dynamics. Before implementation investment, require an actual repair workflow satisfying this resource intersection and an identified decision that cheap actual-checkpoint sensitivity cannot already resolve. Absent that, the checkpoint framing rescues relevance but not advantage.

One proposed first discriminator, **not launched**, is repairing a fixed checkpoint's learned shortcut while preserving clean behavior. Use a smooth scalar-output squared-loss classifier within the paper's architecture, with a documented nuisance feature whose correlation reverses on the repair distribution. Freeze its existing trained weights, repair/rehearsal examples, horizon, and numerical update rule before comparing methods. If obtaining such a checkpoint requires a favorable-case search, stop: that would not be this discriminator.

Predeclare six equal-exposure schedules: correction then rehearsal, reverse order, four-block alternation starting with either set, and eight-block alternation starting with either set. All receive the same total correction/rehearsal weight and training time. Use disjoint validation data to select a schedule, then evaluate its actual dense continuation on untouched shortcut-shift and clean tests. Define admissibility as clean accuracy dropping by at most one percentage point. The primary metric is repair-accuracy regret relative to the best admissible dense schedule in this fixed catalogue; any clean-budget violation is failure. An evaluator computes all six dense references but does not reveal their outcomes to selectors.

Compare closure against actual-checkpoint frozen features, the full checkpoint tangent model, adaptive low-rank continuation, and short dense previews with exact AD for continuous schedule-weight sensitivities. The last baseline can score candidates by local sensitivities and short-rollout validation; give it both an initial quarter-horizon preview and a later local window under the same total work allowance. No random proxy is needed. Count total preprocessing/selection work and measured peak memory, including checkpoint storage and tapes; allow at most twelve dense-trajectory-equivalent units per method, with at most six candidate evaluations. Require closure's hidden moving state to be at most one quarter of dense, so \(2mq/n\le1/4\), including all phase banks. This is only a decision-fidelity screen: six sequential dense runs already solve this small catalogue within that work ceiling. They must be reported as an exact competitor, preventing a speed or total-memory claim from this test.

The hypothesis is that compressed nonlinear continuation resolves a useful schedule ranking missed by these cheaper checkpoint-aware controls. First require the dense catalogue to contain an admissible repair gain of at least five percentage points and a schedule spread of at least five points; otherwise this instance lacks the intended decision. A provisional pass requires closure regret at most one point and every matched cheap control's regret at least three points, with numerical/test uncertainty below half a point. Halving the reference step must change relevant accuracy estimates by less than that tolerance; near-threshold prediction margins and confidence intervals must also support the distinction. Failure of a validity gate is inconclusive. If any cheap control is within one point, recommend hold. Only a provisional pass permits two preregistered independent checkpoint confirmations with the same protocol, no tuning or further search; stop after three checkpoints regardless. Even three passes justify investigating this repair niche, not claiming economic advantage or broad checkpoint control.
