## Specific adversarial checks

The inverse coordinate $u=a/2+\sinh(2a)/4$ has a single-valued holomorphic
inverse on a fixed horizontal strip. Its derivative and the transformed
activation derivative are bounded there. The $H=\sqrt n W$ scaling
in the unnormalized deletion comparison cancels the dimensional factor
in the rank-one update and the matrix action. This gives finite-difference
stability without the maximum carrier in the constant.

The column deletion comparison includes the actual initial column term
in the top pole transfer. Its coordinate size is
$O(\sqrt{\log(en/\delta)/n})$; it is not silently discarded.
Row deletion bounds the source needed for the forward preactivation
derivative, in addition to the reverse carrier. Each conditional Gaussian
tail is evaluated on its own autonomous cavity's stopped domain, determined
by retained initialization. The full-network event is intersected afterward.
This avoids conditioning a Gaussian vector on its own trained history.

The source proof closes its full carrier, forward derivative, and complex
pole caps with strict margins. It uses only singleton cavities; no
unclosed deletion hierarchy is hidden. The query proof establishes first
query gates, then forward derivatives, then top query gates in that order,
so it does not use a top gate bound to prove itself.

Activity parity supplies a symmetric rectangle, not an initial Taylor disk
of fixed radius. The explicit disk-to-rectangle map proves the all-activity
remainder using only initial derivatives. Finite trigonometric interpolation
is controlled by summing Fourier aliases of an analytic function. It adds
only $2L+1$ coefficient vectors per time derivative, needs no trained
snapshot, and preserves linear pairing with $W_0$ and its transpose.

The selected weights match products of basis coordinates and the constant.
Consequently both source spaces are isometrically represented, the
projected mixer's weighted operator norm is no larger than the original
operator norm, and the initialized scalar training Gram is exact.
Forward sources and their mixer images are approximated with identical
scalar coefficients; the same is true of reverse sources and transpose
images. Bounding only a source RMS remainder would not suffice for the
sampled image, and the proof does not make that invalid inference.

The learned matrix is retained in the comparison through its exact
rank-one activity integral. Both its forward and reverse defects reduce to
pairings covered by the positive cubature. Its state is fully evolved in
the smaller network and included in the coordinate count.

The reduced equations use their own prediction and residual. Stability
is in positive weighted Euclidean and Hilbert--Schmidt norms; neither the
minimum weight nor the number of retained nodes enters the error constant.
All real readout coordinates remain bounded by the fixed activity length,
and both mixer operators remain bounded independently of width.

The physical clocks are compared by scalar contraction using the positive

The resource count is for $2N_1+N_1N_2+N_2$ moving real coordinates,
