original training-only event E_n, not an event containing restrictions
on the passive matrix G. Its failure estimate is
Pr(E_n intersect bad)<=C_M n^(-M), for every fixed M. Its proof adds

query/train endpoint trace. Query caps affect only vanishing remainders
and width thresholds; their multiplier does not occur in the finite
singleton shift. The training event may have only o(1) failure.

The downstream check then uses exactly those moments, integrated over
training initialization with the indicator E_n. It does not require
uniform conditional moments for each training initialization. Gaussian
rotation changes G only, so the F-measurable event stays fixed. A finite
moment order chosen from fixed dimension is sufficient for chaining.
There is consequently no unproved Sobolev mixed-response assumption in
the composition. The earlier generic-Lipschitz obstruction is not being
ignored: the neural carrier moments supply the additional regularity.

Both checks include the real fitting tube for all times; only training

## 4. Restriction, sphere source and counted runtime

Q=[U,e] has orthonormal columns and e belongs to V-perp. In the
dimension-D convention their unnormalized inputs are
x'_a=sqrt(D) Q^T x_a/sqrt(d). Their norms are sqrt(D), their
pairwise normalized inner products are unchanged, and Q depends only
on fixed data. Thus A_0Q has exactly the canonical iid Gaussian law,
independently of all initialized hidden blocks. No resampling or
population replacement occurs.

The actual first-weight equation restricted to Q and every remaining
training equation coincide. The restricted model therefore has the
same hidden matrices, readout path, residuals, physical time and gamma.
Its source theorem has the same activation beta and label condition.
The already checked spherical count can be applied with D in place of
d, with its all-time error unchanged. A union bound combines it with
folding; independence of the two success events is unnecessary.

The nonlinear query map keeps the radius of the unobserved input part,
and its image lies on S^(D-1). Hence a sphere-uniform bound for the
restricted model covers every original query. Its potential derivative

abs(||a||-||b||)<=||a-b||. No input derivative of that map enters
the trained ODE or the comparison theorem.
