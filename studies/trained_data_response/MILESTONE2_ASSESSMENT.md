metric and physical T=40. Let `mu` be every separately fixed probability law
in a specified smaller positive W1 neighborhood of `nu_*`, including

minimum-mass or deterministic-label assumption is implicit. With iid
`Z_i~mu`, set `mu_m=m^(-1) sum_i delta_(Z_i)` and let

`H = L2(sqrt(2) S1, rho)`,

where rho is normalized uniform arc length. This chooses a norm for the
entire prediction function; it supplies no teacher labels or selected test

such events vanish; an expectation theorem must also control their probability

not a change to the admitted neural algorithm.

`f_(mu_m)(40,.) - f_mu(40,.) = m^(-1) sum_i I_mu(Z_i) + r_m`,
