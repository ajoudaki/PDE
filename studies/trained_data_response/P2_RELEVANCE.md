Hilbert–Schmidt increment. Changing these ingredients would change the

The relevant positive neighborhood is relative to **all** laws on
`sqrt(2) S^1 × [-Y,Y]`, for each fixed `Y>=1`, in the stated joint input/label
W1 metric. It imposes no atom-count, minimum-weight, correlation, rank or

sufficiently small by the diameter bound `2+2Y`. Thus small amounts of
arbitrary off-support data, nonatomic mass and label noise are admitted;
the theorem does not silently collapse to two orthogonal atoms.
