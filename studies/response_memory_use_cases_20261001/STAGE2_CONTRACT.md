## Questions and required discrimination

1. **Memory indexing.** Determine what input-function or other shared indexing
   changes in the learning rule, then test a mechanism-based extension against
   well-tuned conventional factors and dense/projected learning. Working on a
   circle or merely fitting better than a frozen network is insufficient.
   Favor a principled construction with exact identities and a measurable
   advantage, or a precise obstruction explaining why the attractive shortcut
   fails. Data-law assumptions, projected geometry and retained base storage
   remain explicit.
2. **Temporal coordination.** Determine whether pairing evolving features with
   credit at the same training times carries identifiable, functionally relevant
   information beyond separate temporal marginals and their means. Interventions
   must preserve specified nuisances and include matched controls, causal scope,
   and direct functional tests. The stage1 q1 repair dominance and failed large
   coordination effect are adverse evidence, not successes to relabel.

The larger ambition is a mechanism or tool that transfers across domains. The
actual tested scope must be stated; success on a few datasets does not prove
universal value. Each route writes its own protocol before scientific execution,
including seed split, primary effect threshold, validity gates and allowed
follow-ups. Pilot-based algorithm choices must freeze before confirmation.
