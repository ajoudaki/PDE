# Visual-first research presentation

## Objective

Create an approximately ten-slide, high-level presentation of the established
closure theory and the internally checked numerical evidence. Slides must be
visually led and sparse. Each slide has speaker notes deliverable in at most
two to three minutes. The presentation distinguishes established theorems,
internally checked experiments, interpretations, and open implications.

## Process

One mathematical deep-learning researcher authors each frozen round. Three
isolated professors review only that round's slides and notes, respectively for
technical intelligibility, long-range meaning, and visual communication. A
fourth project-scope critic audits exact claims against their evidence. Reviews
are independent. The author revises until all four accept the same version.

## Inputs and limits

The deck is an editorial synthesis and creates no new theorem or experiment.
Canonical notation and established scope come from `docs/`. Numerical statements
must cite their originating internally checked evidence and must be labelled as
empirical. No construction detail that would unnecessarily disclose the method
is required. The discarded earlier deck is not an input.

## Outputs

Sources and review records remain flat in this folder. Compiled slides and
previews belong under `data/generated/presentation_advocate_review_20260918/`.
