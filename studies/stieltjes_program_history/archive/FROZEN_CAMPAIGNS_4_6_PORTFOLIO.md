## Frozen scope

This portfolio was frozen before inspecting any new decisive output.  Its
purpose is to obtain discriminating exact evidence about the output-kernel
Stieltjes conjecture without turning marginal coefficient collection into an
open-ended compute program.

The accepted claim ladder remains:

1. fixed-order MFP identities and exact arithmetic certificates;
2. finite Hankel inequalities for named parameter families;
3. the all-order formal Stieltjes conjecture;
4. determinacy and global resolvent identification;
5. identification with an independently constructed mean-field trajectory.

No finite campaign below may be promoted past level 2.

## Campaign 4: independent block metrics

Use

$$
D_{\alpha,\beta}=D_a+\alpha D_u+\beta D_W,
\qquad \alpha,\beta\geq0.
$$

The common positive block scale has already been removed.  The target is the
exact output jet through $F^{(9)}(0)$ and the shifted determinant

$$
\Delta_1^+(\alpha,\beta)
=\mu_1\mu_3-\mu_2^2.
$$

Every derivative sector is checkpointed atomically.  The production cap is
4 GiB per sector and 30 cumulative wall minutes after validation.  Required
regressions are the entire diagonal $\alpha=\beta=\lambda$ from Campaign 1,
the canonical point $(1,1)$, and an independent low-order off-diagonal
calculation.  Only an exact quadrant certificate or an exact rational
counterexample is accepted; a grid is not a sign certificate.  This campaign
stops at order nine and does not compute hidden observables.

## Campaign 5: three equicorrelated inputs

Use

$$
G_3(\rho)=(1-\rho)I_3+\rho\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top,
\qquad -\frac12\leq\rho\leq1,
$$

with equal labels and

$$
g=\frac{f_1+f_2+f_3}{3}.
$$

This is the only nondegenerate binary-label scalar character channel for
three inputs.  Mixed labels and generic multi-angle triangles are outside the
scalar-$K$ contract.

The computation is gated:

1. $F^{(3)}$ under 2 GiB and 10 minutes, including transparent Wick checks,
   the canonical endpoint, and a nonzero genuine three-color contribution;
2. $F^{(5)}$ under 4 GiB and 30 minutes, including a scale-invariant novelty
   check and a measured order-seven resource projection;
3. only if both gates pass, sectorized $F^{(7)}$ under 4 GiB per sector and
   six cumulative CPU-hours.

The first scientifically meaningful result is the exact full-interval sign
of

$$
\mu_0,\quad\mu_1,\quad\mu_2,
\quad\mu_0\mu_2-\mu_1^2.
$$

Orders three and five alone are feasibility results, not Hankel evidence.

## Campaign 6: threshold-aware canonical order thirteen

Let

$$
T=1.300194546159283\times10^{32}.
$$

The objective is not the full integer $F^{(13)}(0)$.  It is one of:

$$
L\leq F^{(13)}(0)\leq U,
$$

with either $L>T$ or $U<T$, or another exact one-sided certificate that
separates the same threshold.  The former falsifies the canonical
conjecture; the latter proves only the next finite shifted Hankel test.

The existing multi-day full enumeration and the earlier eight-hour branch
computations are forbidden.  Initial exploration is capped at two total
CPU-hours, 4 GiB per process, and 15 minutes per benchmark.  Any extension
requires, before it begins, an exact regression through the accepted
order-nine and order-eleven sectors and a measured projection showing a
credible threshold-separating certificate within at most eight additional
CPU-hours.  Without that gate the campaign stops and records the missing
algorithmic lemma.

Admissible routes include positive retained subsums, rigorous omitted-mass
upper bounds, sector intervals, checkpointed branch-and-bound, and exact
partition acceleration.  Uncalibrated Monte Carlo and incomplete positive
subsums below the threshold do not update the conjecture.

## Conditional branches

### Metric-ray order eleven

An output-only $F^{(11)}(\lambda)$ campaign, yielding the first ordinary
$3\times3$ Hankel determinant over a continuum, may begin only after
Campaigns 4--6 report.  It first receives a two-hour sector pilot.  A longer
run requires a projection below 12 further CPU-hours and 4 GiB per process.
$Q_2^{(10)}$ is a separate conditional branch and cannot be bundled into the
output run.

### Four inputs

No four-input production run begins unless Campaign 5 reaches $F^{(7)}$ and
its measured compiler growth supports a bounded continuation.  The first
four-input pilot is equicorrelation with the uniform and balanced $2+2$
character channels.  Generic $3+1$ labels and nonsymmetric Gram matrices are
excluded because they require a multidimensional kernel.  Structured
two-angle and three-angle $V_4$ families are later branches, not part of the
initial portfolio.

## Global stopping rule

The round stops after Campaigns 4--6 and at most one authorized conditional
branch.  Passing finite tests does not automatically authorize another
parameter family.  Further computation requires either a new Hankel matrix
size, a direct canonical falsifier, or an explicit all-order structural
mechanism suggested by the completed algebra.
