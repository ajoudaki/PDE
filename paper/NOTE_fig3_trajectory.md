## The problem

The lower panel of Figure 3 (`trajectory.pdf`) plots RMS difference from
dense against physical time. The bundle only holds the eight shared
checkpoints t = 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 40, 80. Seven nonzero points on a linear
axis, joined by straight lines, put four of the seven in the first eighth of
the width and make every curve a broken hockey stick. It cannot be fixed from
the bundle alone.

## What is needed from the archives

Re-export `common_times`, `common_dense`, `common_memory_{1,3,7}`,
`common_rms_{1,3,7}` and `common_sensitivity_{1,3,7}` with denser shared
times. Anything like 25-40 checkpoints, roughly log-spaced between 0.5 and 80
(so the early phase is resolved as well as the plateau), would let the curves
be drawn as measured curves rather than interpolated sticks. If the
paired-label archives were not saved at more times, this needs a rerun of the
dense reference and the three closures with checkpoint capture at the same
physical times; nothing else in the figure changes.

Keep the same bundle keys and shapes (times, then `(n_times, 2048)` arrays),
and `tikz_figures.py trajectory` will pick them up unchanged.
