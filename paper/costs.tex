\section{Initialization, training and inference}
\label{sec:costs}

We cost the prescribed models in \Cref{thm:compression}, at the same dense
width $n$ and their common dense-variability guarantee. Initialization
includes every one-off operation needed to build a model. For the neural
constructions, a training stage means one full-batch vector-field
evaluation; a numerical update pays for all its stages. Initialization and
training memory are total peaks, including data. Query workspace is
additional to the loaded, inference-ready model. The decoder instead has
a finite-word contract and a bound for its complete training schedule.

\subsection{The neural constructions}

For this section define the sufficient state envelopes
\begin{align*}
 S_{\rm Leg}&=Cm\left(\frac m\gamma\right)^3
 n^{5/4}[\log(en)]^2e^{\sqrt{\log(en)}/2},\\
 S_{\rm H}&=C(m+d)^2+
 \left(\frac Cd\right)^{d+1}
 \left(\frac m\gamma\right)^4[\log(en)]^{3d+2}.
\end{align*}
$S_{\rm Leg}$ bounds Legendre's moving coordinates; its fixed dense
matrices are additional. $S_{\rm H}$ bounds Harmonic's complete retained
state. These are the sizes selected by \Cref{thm:compression}, rather
than separately supplied orders. Under the theorem's eventual-width
qualification and with $n\ge\max(m,d)$, their classical
arithmetic costs are as follows; $C$ retains the theorem's
activation/depth convention.

Legendre streams its correction factors and uses cumulative prefixes for
the moment updates. Its $n^{5/4+o(1)}$ moving state therefore retains the
fixed dense-matrix cost. Harmonic's training row includes the moving
feature-Gram solve and readout-cache refresh; the cubic solve is absorbed
because $S_{\rm H}\ge Cm^2$. Its query row uses that refreshed cache.

The table counts non-activation arithmetic. Dense and Legendre add $Cmn$
activation-value and first-derivative calls per stage, and $Cn$ value calls
per query. Legendre initialization adds $Cmn$ value calls. Harmonic adds
$Cm\sqrt{S_{\rm H}}$ value and first-derivative calls per stage and
$C\sqrt{S_{\rm H}}$ value calls per query. Add each routine's work and
scratch. Dense-reference generation also requires $nd+(L-1)n^2$ Gaussian
draws; omit these draws when the reference is supplied. The
\hyperref[int:computational-costs]{complete execution inventory}
retains the unsimplified counts. These arithmetic bounds do not specify
the number, precision, or accuracy of subsequent numerical training steps.

\subsection{Two ways to initialize Harmonic compression}

error at most $Y/n$. At fixed problem parameters, its exact construction
has a source horizon $O(\log(en))$ and logarithm of inverse source tolerance
$O(\log(en))$. Substituting these into the certified local-order recipe
gives $O([\log(en)]^{3/2})$ time panels, Taylor degree $O(\log(en))$,

Both initializers discard their temporary trajectory, source arrays and

\subsection{The finite-word decoder}

The decoder counts word operations. Its explicit width gates bound the
resource logarithm by $C\log(en)$ and its member moment order by
$C\log(em)$, so the retained-word bound in \Cref{thm:compression}
already displays its dependence on $m,d,\gamma$. Each word has $C\log(en)$
sufficient bits under the same activation/depth convention. With all
problem parameters fixed, the complete operation bounds become
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rl}
\text{initialization:}&O(n[\log(en)]^{29/2}),\\
\text{complete training:}&O([\log(en)]^{12}\log(e+\log(en))),\\
\text{one unseen query:}&O(n[\log(en)]^{12}+[\log(en)]^{14}).
\end{array}
\label{eq:decoder-work}
\end{equation}
Training and live query memory fit the complete retained inventory,
$O([\log(en)]^6\log(e+\log(en)))$ words at fixed parameters. Initialization
has peak $O(n[\log(en)]^{5/2}+[\log(en)]^{15/2})$ words. The
\hyperref[int:decoder-finite-backends]{full word-operation ledger} keeps the structural
prefactors and the separate temporary arrays. These query-memory counts
are total memory, unlike the additional workspace in \Cref{tab:runtime}.

The finite tanh evaluator is included. For another analytic activation,
add its supplied evaluator's work at every recorded value call and its
scratch, together with external data/query access and output. The finite
training table is counted; other retained external descriptions require
additional words. Analyticity supplies no evaluation-cost bound for an
arbitrary function. The training row covers the complete prescribed
schedule. A query streams the regenerated source rows, retaining the
displayed width factor in its work.
