A neuron's history, in two clocks

Ten histories from one training run (two hidden tanh layers, width 512, 8 training points, gradient flow). Along ordinary time t they are a violent transient followed by a long stretch of nothing. Re-parameterised by the learning clock τ(t) = ∫₀t ρ, with ρ = √loss the residual, the same curves become smooth, slowly varying functions — the kind a handful of moments can store.
