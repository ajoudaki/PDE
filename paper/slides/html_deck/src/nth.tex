\textbf{Theorem} (Matched literal storage).
\textit{Take two identity hidden layers, a fixed short time window, labels
$0<Y\le1$, and correlated inputs with $m$ samples, $m$ a multiple of four,
$4\le m\le\sqrt n$. The smallest literal frozen-top hierarchy whose error is
within a fixed multiple of the actual dense-run discrepancy, with fixed
probability, has retained size}
\[
 \exp\big[c_Y\log m\,\log n\big]\ \le\ S_n\ \le\ \exp\big[C_Y\log m\,\log n\big],
 \qquad\text{that is,}\qquad S_n=n^{\Theta(\log m)}.
\]
