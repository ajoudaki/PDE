\textbf{Theorem 2.1} (Compression of the complete learning trajectory).
\textit{Train a width-$n$ network of fixed depth $L\ge2$ by full-batch gradient
flow on squared loss with block mobilities $(n,1,\dots,1,n)$, from Gaussian
weights and zero readout, on $m$ training pairs on the sphere. Let the
activations be analytic on a strip with bounded derivative, the population
feature Gram have gap $\gamma>0$, $m/\gamma\ge1$, and
$0<Y\le(\gamma/m)\,\beta^{-30L}$, all fixed as $n\to\infty$. Each
construction can be chosen so that}
\begin{equation}\tag{7}
 \frac{\|f_{\rm model}-f_n\|_{\mathcal X}}{\|f_n-\widetilde f_n\|_{\mathcal X}}
 \xrightarrow{\ \mathbb P\ }0,
 \qquad
 \|g\|_{\mathcal X}=\sup_{t\in[0,\infty]}\ \sup_{x\in\mathcal X}|g(t,x)|,
\end{equation}
\textit{where $\widetilde f_n$ is an independent dense run, with at most these
real coordinates:}
\begin{align}
 \text{Legendre, moving:}\quad&
 Cm\Big(\frac m\gamma\Big)^3 n^{5/4}[\log(en)]^2e^{\sqrt{\log(en)}/2},
 \tag{8}\\
 \text{Harmonic, total:}\quad&
 C(m+d)^2+\Big(\frac Cd\Big)^{d+1}\Big(\frac{Ym}\gamma\Big)^4[\log(en)]^{3d+2},
 \tag{9}\\
 \text{Taylor, total:}\quad&
 C(m+p)^2\Big[\Big(\frac{Ym}\gamma\Big)^4[\log(en)]^3
 +\Big(\frac m\gamma\Big)^2[\log(en)]^2[\log(e+\log(en))]^2\Big]
 +C(m+p)d.
 \tag{10}
\end{align}
\textit{Legendre also keeps $(L-1)n^2$ fixed mixer coordinates. Harmonic and
Taylor also satisfy $\|f_{\rm model}-f_n\|_{\mathcal X}\le Y/n$ \textup{(11)} at
every fixed confidence for large width. For Legendre and Taylor the
sufficient width is polynomial \textup{(12)}. $\mathcal X$ is the whole sphere
for Legendre and Harmonic, and the training inputs with $p$ declared passive
inputs for Taylor.}
